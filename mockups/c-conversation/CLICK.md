# Direction C: Conversation first

Open http://localhost:8790/mockups/c-conversation/index.html

1. On **Team setup**, choose **Upload or try a sample**, then **Use sample org chart**. Review the confidence values. Check the cropped Payroll Clerk label, then **Use this roster**.
2. Choose **Try a voice session**, then **Play sample voice**. Watch the transcript and extracted people fill in. This is scripted and uses no microphone. Choose **Use this roster**.
3. Click the pencil beside a role. Edit people, FTE or systems and **Save role**. **Add a role** demonstrates manual entry. The twins retain the supplied sample estimates when the roster is changed.
4. Choose **Looks right. Show me the goal**. Inspect the five destination outputs and the illustrative **Requests Resolved Right First Time** North Star with its four inputs.
5. In the conversation choose **Sort five tasks with me**, then **Sort my first task**. Type **Keep with me**, **Help**, or **Take it off my plate**, or use the agent’s buttons. Watch the canvas advance and count your choices. Review all five in the conversation; **Edit choices on the canvas** lets you change a pile and updates the agent’s recap. Choose **Add my priorities to the goal**, then **See what changed in the goal**. The original canvas sorting controls also work.
6. Open **Team twin**. Read the gross-estimate sentence and capacity bar, proposed reinvestment, six role shifts, quarterly roadmap and human boundaries. Choose **Meet Yara’s re-imagined role** for her before/after week and skills.
7. Open **Opportunities**. Explore ten changes across all six roles; the list scrolls within its panel. Change the sort order, open **One trusted employee record** or **Catch payroll discrepancies before payday**, inspect the steps, then choose **See this in the plan** to jump to its quarter. Follow the initiative’s link back. The approach legend explains all nine approaches.
8. Open **Modules**, expand **Strategic assumptions** and choose **Start Strategic assumptions**. Answer the agent’s belief, fear and hype prompts in the conversation, or choose the labelled sample answers. Choose **Review on the canvas**, edit a note or its “widely held / specific” mapping, then **Apply to the goal**. The completion links show the human boundary in the team twin, the employee-support opportunity’s new review gate, and the Q2 pilot check. **See the goal update** shows the contribution together.
9. Return to **Modules** and start **Metrics inventory**. The agent lists illustrative current HR measures and flags prompts sent and licences issued as vanity candidates. Answer three prompts, then review on the canvas. Change a classification or **Add a measure**, then **Apply to the goal**. Follow the links to the employee-record opportunity and Q1/Q4 checks; the goal now shows both module contributions. These are candidate measures, not measured baselines. **Requests Resolved Right First Time** remains illustrative.
10. Inspect the other North Star modules in order and expand **North Star live workshop** for the separate eight-part Guide agenda: 120 minutes without the optional exercise, 135 with it. Use the conversation suggestions or send **What stays human?** on another screen. At 390px, **Conversation / Canvas** switches work areas; exercises open in Conversation and finish on Canvas. The screen navigation scrolls horizontally.

All data is a fictional, illustrative sample. No files, voice, account connections or messages are sent. State stays in this tab until refresh. The supplied team estimates are about 8,850 annual hours, 5.5 FTE, EUR 586k annual capacity value, all gross estimates, and a 2.2× model ceiling.
