# agentlabs conversation mockup

<!-- impeccable:product-schema: 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Purpose and Audience
A team lead at the fictional Noordhaven Logistics explores a more AI native HR team. Amplification comes first: people should see what they can do more of and better.

## Primary Workflow
An agent leads a conversation beside a canvas. Configure the team, see the destination, inspect twins and ten selected opportunities, run a module in the conversation as the canvas responds, and leave with a quarterly plan.

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript modules. No build, CDN, external fonts, paid APIs or backend. Existing server: port 8790.

## Evidence on Hand
fleet/MOCKUP-BRIEF.md supplies the six roles, ten people, systems and team estimates. prototypes/flow/MODEL.md explains the model. Task and step texts come from jobs/13-1071.00.json and jobs/43-4161.00.json. Names, conversations, module answers, allocations and initiative estimates are explicitly illustrative mock data.

## Constraints
Eight reachable screens, complete card sorting plus AI-guided Strategic assumptions and Metrics inventory, editable roster, three input methods, approach legend and traceable plan. Both guided exercises collect answers in conversation, require an editable review, and show the exact goal, opportunity, initiative or twin additions after applying. The HR North Star remains illustrative. Screenshots at 1440 and 390. Write only in this directory, apart from the explicitly requested status line.

## Product Principles
Show the goal early. Make the next action concrete. Protect human judgement. Label gross estimates. Let the canvas make the conversation tangible.
