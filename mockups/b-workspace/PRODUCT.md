# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules. No build, CDN, external fonts or paid APIs. Confirmed by fleet/MOCKUP-BRIEF.md.

## Users
A team lead at the fictional Noordhaven Logistics, configuring an HR team and deciding what to improve.

## Product Purpose
Make a team more AI native. Show the destination first, then use individual and team modules to sharpen role twins, a team twin, opportunities with step-level approaches, and an owned plan.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Direction B is a dense, calm workspace with a team map, opportunity board by approach and module side panel. Eight reachable screens. Ten people, six roles, 134 tasks, 945 steps. Baseline model: about 8,850 annual hours returned, 5.5 FTE, EUR 586k annual gross capacity value, 2.2x ceiling. All hours and money are gross estimates, not measured savings. Preserve human judgement and lead with amplification. Data is mocked; the flow works. Only this folder is writable, with the expressly requested completion line in fleet/STATUS.md.

## Evidence on Hand
fleet/MOCKUP-BRIEF.md; prototypes/flow/MODEL.md; prototypes/flow/data/jobs/43-4161.00.json and 13-1071.00.json; research/north-star.md, including section (e) and the eight proposed team modules. People are invented as requested. Opportunity allocations, week allocations, roadmap assignments and module progress are illustrative, labelled in the interface. The eight North Star exercises are standalone team-library modules with format tags; the Guide agenda is a separate live workshop bundle. “Requests Resolved Right First Time” and its four inputs are illustrative on the goal and team twin; no baseline or target is invented.

## Product Principles
Show what the team can do more of. Trace a plan back to real task and step text. Show what stays human. Let a person review every proposed input. Never present an estimate as an organisational measurement.

## Runnable North Star modules
Confidence ladder rates four illustrative employee-journey moments, marks optional AI support and records observable trust signals. Its reviewed completion updates the team twin, linked opportunities and goal evidence. Bet up traces three sample pilots through two increase/decrease levels to a North Star input, or explicitly stops them. Its reviewed completion links test signals to initiatives and removes untraced pilots from active quarters. Both preserve editable drafts and applied decisions in this browser. The AI guide is labelled a simulation. These two flows and card sorting run end to end; the other modules remain previews.

## Judge follow-up
The three requested B fixes preserve the Workspace direction and all existing flows. Internal source identifiers stay in data, outside the visible interface. Approach labels use sentence case. The eight North Star exercises retain their source order and formats under plain names. All have durations; the four absent from the source are explicitly proposed planning estimates, not attributed timings. Opportunities expose their effort range and real step-approach counts. The nine-approach legend sits beside the desktop steps and folds immediately above them on mobile.

- Feedforward → act → every North Star module states its duration and marks proposed estimates → all eight display numeric durations, with four marked proposed.
- Cognitive load → understand → definitions sit beside the work they explain → the legend is adjacent at 1440 and directly before steps at 390.
- Visual hierarchy → filter → effort bands and actual approach counts make the nine opportunities comparable → all nine show their own effort range and distinct step mix.
