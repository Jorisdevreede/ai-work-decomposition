# Workspace B: what to click

Open http://localhost:8790/mockups/b-workspace/index.html. A fresh browser context starts from the supplied Noordhaven HR sample. Roster edits, module drafts and applied decisions persist in this browser.

1. On **Overview**, inspect the five goal outputs, people navigation and **Shape the goal** panel. Expand the four inputs under **Requests Resolved Right First Time**. The metric and inputs are illustrative; no baseline or target is invented.
2. Open **Team settings** or **Edit team**. Try the sample org chart for extraction confidence and **Check this** marks. Choose a file for labelled demo extraction, or **Start demo conversation** for a transcript and filling roster. No data is sent and no microphone is used.
3. Edit roles, people, FTE and systems. Add and remove a role, then **Save team & see the goal**. Roster edits persist; gross model estimates retain the supplied 10-FTE baseline.
4. Open **Team twin** for capacity, new work, North Star inputs, role shifts, human boundaries and quarterly roadmap. Open the HR Specialist row for Noor’s week before/after, amplification, boundaries and skills.
5. Open **Opportunities**. Compare small, medium and large effort ranges and each opportunity’s actual step approach mix. Switch **Priority / By approach**, search or filter, then select **A considered goodbye, less paperwork**. The nine-approach legend sits beside its steps at 1440px, and folds immediately above them at 390px. Labels use sentence case; task IDs stay out of the interface.
6. Choose **See this in the plan**. Inspect the highlighted initiative, owner, build and **Review dependency**. **Trace to opportunity** returns to that work.
7. Open **Module library**. Expand individual modules, SIPOC and value stream analysis. Eight plainly named North Star exercises appear in source order with format tags and durations. Four durations are explicitly **proposed** planning estimates because the source gives no timing. The separate **North Star live workshop · Bundle** holds the Guide’s eight-part agenda, timings and outputs.
8. Under **Confidence ladder**, choose **Run Confidence ladder**. Rate all four employee-journey moments from 1 to 5, optionally mark AI support, and enter a trust signal for each. **Review the journey map**, go back to change a rating, then **Add this journey to the goal**. The receipt links to four changed opportunities and the new journey section in the team twin. The goal gains journey evidence.
9. Under **Bet up**, choose **Run Bet up**. Use the labelled sample guide suggestions or enter each pilot’s first increase/decrease change, second-level input and test signal. Edit a suggestion. Give the forecast pilot **No credible link → stop pilot**. **Review pilot decisions**, then **Add decisions to the plan**. The receipt identifies the changed initiatives; stopped pilots move out of active quarters into **Stopped after Bet up**. Follow the links to inspect the changes and updated goal.
10. Run **Card sorting**. Sort Noor’s four tasks into Amplify, Hand over or Protect the human part, undo if needed, move a task during review, then **Add my priorities to the goal**. Read the receipt and updated goal. At 390px, the menu and Modules panel support keyboard focus, Escape and closing; changing to desktop resets the mobile navigation.

Original eight routes: `#/setup`, `#/goal`, `#/team`, `#/role/specialist`, `#/opportunities`, `#/plan`, `#/modules`, `#/module`. Runnable North Star routes: `#/confidence-ladder`, `#/bet-up`. Three modules run end to end; all other modules identify themselves as previews.
