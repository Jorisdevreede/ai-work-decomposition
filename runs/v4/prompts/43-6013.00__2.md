# 43-6 Secretaries and administrative assistants

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 54 automate/amplify tasks across 4 occupations (executive, legal, medical and general secretaries and administrative assistants). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot in Outlook and Teams (Microsoft)**: "Schedule with Copilot" turns a mail thread into an invite with an agenda; Teams Facilitator takes live notes for minutes. [S1, S2] E2
- **iBabs Debrief (iBabs, Netherlands)**: transcripts, summaries and minutes of board meetings inside the board portal, data in the EU. [S3] E2
- **Autoscriber with ChipSoft HiX (Netherlands)**: summarises the recorded consultation; the doctor pastes it into HiX. Replaces "transcribe practitioners' diagnoses". [S4] E2
- **Juvoly (Tandem Health, Sweden)**: speech to GP notes; claims 35% of Dutch GP practices. [S5] E2
- **BaseNeQXt (BaseNet, Netherlands)**: law-firm case files, conflict check, templates, time registration, invoicing. [S6] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **UK civil service, 3-month trial**: 20,000 Copilot users self-reported saving 26 minutes a day. [S7] E3
- **UK Department for Business and Trade, Oct-Dec 2024**: 1,000 licences, diary study plus observed tasks. Mean time saved per task: drafting 1.3 h, meeting summaries 0.7 h, scheduling −0.6 h (took longer). No evidence of higher productivity; hallucinations seen. The department's own evaluation. [S8] E3
- **Capio, Sweden, 2024-2025**: self-estimated documentation time per note fell from 6.69 to 4.72 minutes (−29%). Commissioned by Tandem. [S9] E3
- **LUMC, 2024**: Autoscriber on mock consultations: median 202 s manual, 152 s edited, quality lower (PDQI-9 31 versus 29). Vendor co-authors. [S10] E3

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Configure**: "maintain scheduling and event calendars", "confirm appointments", "patient diagnostic appointments": booking rules, slot types and reminders in the agenda or HIS.
- **Deterministic**: "invoices, reports, memos, letters", "summonses, subpoenas", "complete forms": a template generator fed by the records app (case file, HIS, CRM); invoices from time entries. Structured lab results route by rule.
- **Bounded model step**: a routine reply, minutes or a consult summary from a transcript, sorting free-text mail. Check: every field traced to the record, every action has an owner, a confidence threshold with a fallback queue. Stop: one draft; a named person approves before sending or filing. LUMC shows the edit cannot be skipped.
- **Glue today**: the shared mailbox, shared calendar and Excel lists link executives, boards and departments; travel and catering go by mail. A request form plus a records app replace most of it.
- **Stays with a person**: judging the "significance" of incoming memos, office policy, training staff.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR art. 9**: health data is a special category [S11]. A GP-practice employee pasted patients' medical data into an AI chatbot; the AP treats this as a breach and wants rules on what may be entered [S12]. DPIA for transcription (art. 35): E1.
- **WGBO**: the file is kept 20 years (BW 7:454) and confidential (7:457) [S13]; a filed AI summary is part of it. NEN 7510: E1.
- **Advocatenwet 11a** binds "medewerkers en personeel" to the lawyer's secrecy [S14]. NOvA: no confidential data in free tools, verify citations by hand [S15].
- **Copilot**: SLM Rijk and SURF's DPIA reassessment leaves two medium risks, output accuracy and diagnostic-data retention (Microsoft's account) [S16].
- **EU AI Act**: no Annex III point covers drafting, scheduling or minutes; point 4 applies if a tool allocates tasks or monitors staff. Article 50(1): a patient-facing booking bot must say it is AI. Article 4: AI literacy. [S17]
- **WOR art. 27(1)(k, l)**: transcription and usage dashboards need works-council consent [S18].
