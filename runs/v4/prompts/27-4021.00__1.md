# 27-4 Photographers, camera operators, editors and AV technicians

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 11 automate/amplify tasks across 5 occupations (photographers, camera operators, film and video editors, audio/video and broadcast technicians). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Excire Foto (Pattern Recognition Company GmbH, Germany)**: AI culling on aesthetics, sharpness and facial expression; automatic keywording; face and free-text search, run locally. For "review sets of photographs". [S1] E2
- **DaVinci Resolve 20 (Blackmagic Design)**: AI IntelliScript matches clip transcripts to the script and builds a rough cut, alternate takes stacked above; SmartSwitch cuts multicam to whoever speaks. For "select and combine the most effective shots". [S2] E2
- **Premiere Pro text-based editing (Adobe)**: transcribes footage so the editor cuts by editing the transcript. Same task. [S3] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Wirtualna Polska Media, Poland**: Google Vision AI tags every image entering its repository; about 45 seconds of manual tagging saved per image, "almost 400 work hours a month". Self-reported in a Google customer story. [S4] E3
- **RedShark News test, December 2023**: one editor cut 20 minutes of podcast footage to 10 twice: 117 minutes on the timeline, 64 by transcript in Premiere Pro. One run; he knew the cuts by the second pass. [S3] E3
- Context, not a case: Imagn Images tested AI captions on 3,000+ Super Bowl photos (2025); no accuracy reported. [S5]

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Eliminate with a calculator**: "read charts and compute ratios" (lighting, shutter angles, filter factors, distances) is fixed formulas; no model.
- **Records app with reminders**: "compliance, license, and warranty information" is an asset register with expiry dates and notifications (configure). "Scheduling appointments, keeping books" is a booking tool plus a bookkeeping package (configure).
- **Integration report**: "reports outlining past and future programs" come from the playout as-run log and schedule.
- **Bounded model loops**: captions: the model drafts from tags, capture time and place, and the assignment sheet; check: every named person is on the assignment list and who, what, where, when are filled; a person approves. Culling: the model flags blur and closed eyes and ranks; the photographer picks. Rough cut: transcript matching builds the first timeline; the editor shapes the story. Manuals, broadcast scripts, outlines and storyboards: the model drafts from a template, a person edits.
- **Glue today**: shot lists, caption sheets and equipment licences in spreadsheets and mail (E1).
- **Stays with people**: story and pacing, creative retouching, what to publish.

## 4. Gates

- **AI Act article 50**: an AI-altered image or video that resembles real people or events and would falsely appear authentic is a deep fake and must be disclosed, for evidently artistic work without hampering it (art. 3(60), 50(4)). Tool providers must mark synthetic output machine-readably (art. 50(2)) [S6], by 2 December 2026 for systems already on the market [S7].
- **Prohibited from 2 December 2026**: using AI to make realistic intimate images of an identifiable person without explicit consent (new art. 5(1)(ba)) [S7].
- **Annex III**: no point applies; editing, captioning and culling are not listed [S6].
- **Portrait right**: a portrait made without commission may not be published if the person has a reasonable interest against it (Auteurswet art. 21) [S8].
- **GDPR art. 9**: naming people by face recognition processes biometric data for unique identification, prohibited without an exception such as explicit consent [S9].
- **Risks reported**: Imagn said more investment is needed for accuracy across sports [S5]; IntelliScript yields a rough cut, not a finished edit [S2].
