# 41-9 Real estate agents, sales engineers and telemarketers

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 38 automate/amplify tasks across 6 occupations (real estate brokers and agents, sales engineers, telemarketers, demonstrators). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Realworks Zoekers (Realworks, NL)**: matches listings to a buyer's hard and soft wishes; sends new ones daily or in real time. For "generate lists of properties that are compatible with buyers' needs". [S1] E2
- **Realworks Koopovereenkomst and Wwft-check (Realworks, NL)**: purchase agreement from CRM data, e-signing, copy to the notary; Wwft ID and questionnaire, risk rating by the agent. For "prepare documents such as ... purchase agreements". [S2, S3] E2
- **Calcasa Model Waardering (Calcasa, NL)**: automated valuation, tested monthly against sale prices; not for mortgage lending. For "compare a property with similar properties". [S4] E2
- **Funda virtuele styler (Funda, NL)**: AI-restyled listing photos from September 2026. [S5] E2
- **Tacton CPQ (Tacton, Sweden)**: rule-based configuration and quoting. For "plan and modify product configurations". [S6] E2
- **Verint Wrap Up Bot (Verint)**: call transcript and summary written to the CRM. For "maintain records of contacts, accounts, and orders". [S7] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Meyn (poultry-processing equipment), page 2025-09**: about 2,000 quotes a year. Single-machine quote from two days to a week down to under 15 minutes; questions per quote down 85%. Vendor claim; rules, not a model. [S6] E3
- **AXA Health (UK), undated**: Wrap Up Bot for 1,100 agents, 50,000 calls a week, after-call work up to six minutes; handle time down 60 seconds. Vendor claim; service desk. [S7] E3
- No Dutch makelaar case found (searched Realworks, Funda, Calcasa).

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Configure**: contact and order records, "enter orders into computers", buyer matching (a filter, not a model).
- **Deterministic**: contracts and closing statements filled from CRM fields; "monitor fulfillment of purchase contract terms" as deadlines with notifications; CPQ rules; forecasts as a pipeline dashboard; valuation as an AVM plus comparables table.
- **Bounded model loops**: call summary into CRM fields (check: agent edits before save; stop: one draft per call); listing text from object data (check: each claim traces to a field; stop: agent signs off); RFP answers from an approved library (check: cites an entry; stop: gaps to the sales engineer).
- **Glue today**: buyer lists and viewing feedback in mail and Excel; call lists in spreadsheets.
- **Stays with a person**: advising, negotiating, selling, training, on-site support.

## 4. Gates

- **Telemarketing**: from 1 July 2026 consumers, zzp and vof may be called only with explicit prior consent, existing customers included; ACM enforces [S8]. Bought lists need consent records.
- **Wwft**: makelaars, rental brokers from EUR 10,000 a month and appraisers are Wwft institutions (art. 1a(4)(h),(m)) [S9].
- **EU AI Act**: no annex III point applies; a house valuation is not credit scoring (5(b)). Article 50(1): bots must say they are AI. Article 50(4): an AI-edited photo of a real home is a deep fake and must be labelled [S10]; in August 2026 Funda still had many, acting only on reports [S11]. Article 4: AI literacy.
- **WOR article 27(1)(l)**: call recording or speech analytics that can monitor staff need works council consent [S12]; AI scoring of agents is annex III 4(b) [S10].
- **GDPR**: prospect lists need an article 6 basis; large-scale call recording calls for a DPIA. E1.
- **Failure**: Zillow quit home buying in November 2021 after a USD 304 million inventory write-down, blaming unpredictable price forecasts [S13].
