# 43-4 Information and Record Clerks

O*NET minor group 43-4: 16 occupations (e.g. customer service representatives, order, file, loan and credit clerks, HR assistants, receptionists, reservation agents, eligibility interviewers), 145 automate and amplify tasks (100 automate, 45 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

Court, municipal and food-stamp tasks are US public-sector work.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Customer questions: Zendesk AI agents answer by chat, email and voice, check orders and change addresses through integrations [S1] E2.
- Answering questions: TOPdesk Robin answers from the knowledge base, else opens a pre-filled request [S2] E2.
- Product advice: bol's Shophulp, an AI shopping assistant in beta since September 2026 [S3] E2.
- Order entry from email and PDF: Rossum checks customer, stock and price rules, routes exceptions to people, posts to the ERP [S4] E2.
- Order records: Exact imports webshop orders and syncs stock [S5] E2.
- HR records and questions: AFAS self-service changes flow to payroll; Jonas AI answers from the cao; Vacaturematching matches applicants to vacancies, the recruiter decides [S6] E2.
- Hotel desk: Mews offers online check-in, kiosks and automatic charge routing [S7] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- 5,179 support agents with a generative AI assistant resolved 14% more issues per hour, novices 34%. NBER study [S8] E3.
- Klarna's assistant handled two-thirds of chats in its first month; resolution time fell from 11 to under 2 minutes. Company figures [S9] E3.
- TeamSystem 80% automation with Zendesk [S1]; Anglia Ruskin University 75% of chats with TOPdesk [S2]; allnex 70% touchless orders with Rossum [S4]. Vendor cases, E3.
- Amsterdam's Smart Check welfare-fraud model, piloted on about 1,600 applications, flagged more people, was no better than caseworkers and was stopped in November 2023 [S10] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- Records, filing, data entry (about a quarter): deterministic. Self-service forms and integrations write once into the records app [S5][S6][S7]. Shared mailboxes and Excel trackers, the glue today, give way to ticketing and document capture [S2][S4].
- Computations (fares, interest, benefits; about 8): plain code (E1).
- Orders and bookings (about 20): deterministic plus a bounded model loop for free-text orders; check: every line matches master data [S4].
- Letters, reports, minutes (about a fifth): artefact generator; a model drafts free replies from the case record, the clerk sends (E1).
- Answering questions (about a sixth): answer desk over a maintained knowledge base, with handover [S1][S2].
- Eligibility, credit and applicant checks (about a sixth): rules for completeness and fixed criteria; judgement stays with a person [S10]. Agent: rare. Fraud investigation stays manual.

## 4. Gates

- AVG art. 22: no solely automated decision with legal effect unless contract, law or consent allow it [S11]. UAVG art. 40 allows it for legal duties and public tasks, not profiling [S12]. A score a lender "draws strongly on" counts as the decision (SCHUFA, CJEU 2023) [S13].
- Consumer Credit Directive 2023/2225 art. 18(8): human intervention and explanation for automated credit checks, from 20 November 2026 [S14].
- EU AI Act Annex III high-risk: recruitment (4a), benefit eligibility (5a), creditworthiness (5b), justice (8a) [S15], from 2 December 2027 under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 [S16]. Narrow procedural tasks fall outside, profiling never [S17]. Chatbots must disclose they are AI from 2 August 2026 [S18][S19]. Emotion recognition on agents is prohibited [S20].
- WOR art. 27(1)(l): call recording and agent dashboards need works council consent [S21].
- SyRI: a court struck down the benefits-fraud risk system as insufficiently transparent (2020) [S22].
