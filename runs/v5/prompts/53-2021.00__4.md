# Digest 53-2: Air Transportation Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 11 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 5 occupations (airline pilots, commercial pilots, air traffic controllers, airfield operations specialists, flight attendants).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **NetLine/Load CockpitMobile (Lufthansa Systems, Germany), load control:** pilots run weight and balance and generate and approve the load sheet on a mobile device [S1]. E2.
- **Lido Flight 4D (Lufthansa Systems), flight planning:** routes, profiles and fuel against weather and airspace restrictions; digital NOTAMs; take-off and landing performance [S2]. E2.
- **AVIATAR Technical Logbook (Lufthansa Technik, Germany):** prefilled text, input masks, offline sync, link to the maintenance system [S3]. E2.
- **smartSTRIPS (Frequentis, Austria), electronic flight strips:** replaced paper strips in two Schiphol towers for LVNL in 2019 [S4]. E2.
- **EUROCONTROL, NOTAM prioritisation:** an AI module ranks NOTAMs; operators check 600 to 800 a day, typically 1.5% need a system update [S5]. Live use not stated: E1.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Wizz Air, electronic technical logbook:** three months after rollout on 140+ aircraft, calls to maintenance control fell by more than 25% [S3]. E3, vendor case, September 2022.
- **KLM, passenger questions on social media:** AI supported over 50% of inquiries; agents reviewed its proposed answers [S6]. E3, company release, December 2017 (old).

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Weight and balance, take-off speed (2 tasks, importance 4.60 and 4.93):** configure existing software, computed from flight manual data [S1][S2]. No model. Add input checks (booked against load sheet children): a title-mapping bug once put 38 women at child mass, take-off mass 1,244 kg low [S7].
- **Logs and flight records (3 tasks):** integration plus records app. Times, fuel and sectors flow from aircraft and load sheet into the electronic log and on to crew records and logbooks (E1). Glue today: paper logs re-keyed by crew control.
- **ATC activity reports and record review (2 tasks):** artefact generator from strip and message data [S4]; completeness checks are rules. A bounded model may draft notes on unusual events; the controller signs (E1).
- **Flight plan review, airfield publications, weather and plan posting (3 tasks):** feeds into planning and airfield displays. A bounded model can rank NOTAMs [S5]; check: none hidden, a dispatcher confirms; stop on any it cannot classify (E1).
- **Passenger questions (1 task):** answer desk in the app on live flight data; cabin talk stays manual.
- **Agent:** none needed.

## 4. Gates

- **EFB rules (Regulation 965/2012):** performance and mass and balance apps need accuracy tests against the flight manual across the operating envelope; EASA found test methods not always adequate [S8].
- **Flight time limitations:** actual flight, duty and rest times are kept 24 months [S9]: logs are compliance records.
- **AI Act:** aviation law sits in Annex I Section B [S10], where only Art. 6(1), 102-109 and 112 apply, so requirements come through EASA rules [S11]. Annex III point 2 names digital infrastructure, road traffic and utilities, not aviation [S12]. EASA has guidance for Level 1 and 2 machine learning [S13]. Chatbots must say they are AI [S14].
- **NIS2:** air carriers, airports and ATC operators are in Annex I [S15]; the Dutch Cyberbeveiligingswet applies from 15 August 2026 [S16].
- **WOR art. 27:** logs that tie times and fuel to named pilots can check performance: consent under 27(k) and (l) [S17].
- **Reported failures:** NATS flight planning failure, 28 August 2023: over 700,000 passengers affected, 34 recommendations [S18].
