# 19-4 Life, Physical, and Social Science Technicians

O*NET minor group 19-4: 14 occupations (e.g. precision agriculture, food science, environmental and remote sensing technicians, research assistants, QC analysts), 68 automate and amplify tasks (43 automate, 25 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Sample tracking: Thermo Fisher SampleManager LIMS barcodes samples, assigns tests and logs each change [S1] E2.
- QC reports: LabWare LIMS generates certificates of analysis, with e-signatures and audit trails [S2] E2.
- Data entry from documents: Benchling AI structures PDFs and CRO reports, drafts study reports [S3] E2.
- Coding research data: MAXQDA AI Assist (Germany) suggests codes with reasons, EU storage or local model [S4]; ATLAS.ti claims "up to 90%" less time (vendor estimate) [S5] E2.
- Validated data entry: Castor EDC (Dutch) forms with conditional logic, audit trail [S6] E2.
- Task maps: Farmmaps fertilisation task maps, variable-rate zoning [S7] E2.
- Image preparation: Copernicus offers free, atmospherically corrected Sentinel-2 data and mosaics [S8] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- NPPL Dutch farm trial, 2018-2019: soil-herbicide doses from soil-scan task maps: 13% less product than growers' rates (EUR 25.59/ha), 35% below KWIN norms, no extra weeds [S9] E3.
- Gilardi et al., PNAS 2023: zero-shot ChatGPT beat crowd workers on 4 of 5 coding tasks at under USD 0.003 per item [S10] E3.
- Novo Nordisk NovoScribe: report writing time down 90%, verification protocol effort down 95%, people approve; vendor case (Anthropic) [S11] E3.
- None found for LIMS or QC.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Records, data entry, data checks** (about 17 of 68): records app (LIMS, ELN, EDC), instrument integration, entry rules. A model only reads contract-lab PDFs [S3], checked field by field (E1).
- **Calculations, spec comparison** (about 10): plain code over fixed limits and formulas (E1).
- **Reports, SOPs, deviation reports** (about 17): artefact generator for tables, certificates [S2]; narrative by bounded model loop, numbers traced to records, QA signs [S11].
- **Maps and imagery** (about 17): analysis-ready data eliminates most image preparation [S8]; zones and task maps are configured rules [S7]; linking maps to machines is hardest [S9].
- **Survey design, coding, literature** (about 5): bounded model loop; a person double-codes a sample [S10]; check every citation [S12].
- **Public information** (3): answer desk over published data (E1). **Evaluate methods, QC procedures** (4): manual.
- Glue today: Excel between instrument and LIMS, re-keyed contract-lab PDFs (E1). No agent needed.

## 4. Gates

- **GMP:** EudraLex still lists Annex 11 from 2011 [S13]. Draft Annex 22 bars generative AI from critical GMP decisions, allows deviation summaries with a qualified person reviewing; final expected 2026 [S14] (sponsored).
- **Accreditation:** official control labs must be ISO/IEC 17025 accredited [S15]; the standard requires validated software; GLP: OECD rules (E1).
- **Radiation:** Bbs art. 7.34 sets worker dose limits, arts. 7.16-7.17 dose registration [S16].
- **AI Act:** Annex III 2 covers safety components in water, gas, heating and electricity supply [S17], from 2 December 2027 [S18]. Art. 50(4): disclose AI text published to inform the public, unless human-edited [S19].
- **GDPR:** research needs art. 89 safeguards [S20]; dose records are likely health data, art. 9 [S21] (E1).
- **Works council:** WOR 27(1)(l) covers systems fit to monitor performance [S22], such as per-user audit trails (E1).
- **Failures:** GPT-3.5 invented 55%, GPT-4 18% of citations (2023) [S12]. An NPPL grower: not "plug-and-play" as suppliers say [S23]. An Oklahoma nitrogen algorithm in Illinois saved fertiliser but lost 33 bushels per acre [S24].
