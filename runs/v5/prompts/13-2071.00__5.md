# 13-2 Financial specialists

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15, with XSCOUT signals. 210 automate/amplify tasks across 16 occupations (accountants, credit analysts, underwriters, tax preparers, fraud examiners). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **DataSnipper (NL)**: Excel agents pull evidence and run test of details; "your team reviews and signs off". For "verify totals". [S1] E2
- **AFAS Jonas (NL)**: assistant in income and corporate tax returns; the user stays responsible. For "compute taxes owed". [S2] E2
- **Ockto (NL)**: passes consented Belastingdienst, UWV and MijnOverheid data to the lender; an integration, no model. For "obtain and compile ... credit histories". [S3] E2
- **Moody's Credit Memo**: drafts memos from spreads, deal documents and the bank's template. Unnamed European bank: drafts in 15 minutes, not two weeks (vendor claim). For "credit analyses". [S4, S5] E2
- **Cytora (Applied Systems)**: turns broker submissions into structured risks. For "determine degree of risk". [S6] E2
- **Hawk (Munich)**: AML monitoring: rules plus machine learning, a reason per alert, four-eye review. For "detect irregularities". [S7, S8] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Baker Tilly Netherlands, since 2022**: DataSnipper. Statement tick-and-tie fell from 8 to 2 hours; test of details: 40-50% time saved ("observed"). Vendor claim. [S9] E3
- **Zurich Insurance, five countries, 2026**: Cytora on commercial submissions. Triage fell from 75 to 15 minutes; straight-through intake rose from 10% to 95%. Insurer's figures. [S6] E3

## 3. Deterministic first

The top tasks are rules and arithmetic ("compute payment schedules", "calculate tax bills", "generate financial ratios"): plain code and configuration in the ledger, loan or tax system. Gathering data is integration (Ockto, KvK, credit-bureau and bank feeds). Checks ("verify totals", "review loan agreements") are rules over every record, exceptions to a person. Dashboards replace "draw charts".

Bounded model steps: reading submissions, contracts and invoices into fields (check: totals and IDs tie to source; stop on mismatch); drafting credit memos, audit and investigation reports (every figure traces to a workpaper; a reviewer signs); alert triage with a reason per alert, closing nothing outside a logged rule. Tie-outs matter: in GPT for Work's own Excel-agent test, most models ended a cash waterfall at €1.16M, not €580K [S10].

Spreadsheets glue it today (credit spreads, audit request lists, mailed budget packs); a records app with an audit trail replaces them. Skin Pharm (US clinics) moved Google Sheets orders and Slack approvals into Ramp; month-end close fell from 25 to 10-15 days (vendor claim) [S11].

Stays with people: loans above limits, "decline excessive risks", materiality, FIU reports, debt advice, interviews.

## 4. Gates

- **EU AI Act**: credit scoring of natural persons is high-risk (annex III 5(b), fraud detection excepted), as is life and health insurance pricing (5(c)) [S12], from 2 December 2027 [S13]. Firms fall outside, sole traders not (E1). Proposed supervisor: AFM, with DNB [S14].
- **GDPR art. 22**: a score is an automated decision when the lender gives it a determining role [S15]. Scoring needs a DPIA (art. 35, E1).
- **Consumer credit**: from 20 November 2026 consumers may demand human review and an explanation of automated creditworthiness assessments [S16, S17].
- **Wwft**: banks, external accountants and tax advisers report unusual transactions to the FIU "onverwijld" [S18]. DNB fined bunq €2.6 million (2025), partly for reporting similar transactions inconsistently, without explanation [S19].
- **Failure**: the tax office's fraud list (FSV) held about 270,000 people without legal basis, nationality a factor; AP fine €3.7 million [S20].
- **WOR art. 27(1)(l)**: fraud analytics on staff expenses monitor behaviour: works council consent [S21].
