# 17-2 Engineers

O*NET minor group 17-2: 34 occupations (e.g. civil, chemical, electrical, industrial, mechanical, validation, energy engineers), 224 automate and amplify tasks (71 automate, 153 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Control code: Siemens Industrial Copilot writes PLC code and HMI screens in TIA Portal at thyssenkrupp Automation Engineering [S1] E2.
- Design checks: Dassault Systèmes LEO "flags violations of parameters, regulations and constraints"; MARIE evaluates formulations [S2] E2.
- Simulation: Ansys SimAI predicts new designs from past simulations; vendor claims 10-100x shorter cycles [S3] E2.
- Site design: Bentley OpenSite+ optimises layouts and earthwork, drawings "up to 10 times" faster (vendor) [S4] E2.
- Plan review: Solibri (Nemetschek) runs rule-based BIM checks for clashes, fire stopping and accessibility [S5] E2.
- Requirements and verification: Relatics (Ridderkerk; TenneT, Dutch government), no AI listed [S6] E2.
- Validation protocols and records: Kneat Gx [S7] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- MSD with Kneat Gx, 27 sites: validation cycle time down "over 50%", workflows from 15 steps to 8. Vendor case, no method [S7] E3.
- Writing, 453 professionals (no engineers): 40% less time, 18% better quality [S8] E3.
- Code: Copilot users finished a set task 55.8% faster [S9] E3; 16 experienced developers on real tasks were 19% slower with AI, believing they were faster [S10] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Documents, 93 of 224 tasks (42%):** reports, specifications, procedures, proposals. Deterministic plus a bounded model loop: an artefact generator fills templates from records (requirements, test results, calculations); a model drafts prose; every number must trace to a record; the engineer signs. Recurring status reports become a dashboard (eliminate).
- **Cost estimates, 27 (12%):** deterministic: quantities from the model or bill of materials times a price table; a model only reads supplier quotes, checked against totals.
- **Calculations, simulation, code, 21 (9%):** solver and tests are the check; a model may set up runs or draft code. FEABench's best strategy made executable COMSOL calls 88% of the time; executable is not correct [S11].
- **Test data, root cause, 22 (10%):** dashboard and statistics; a model clusters free-text failure reports, sample-checked.
- **Records 11 (5%), plan and code reviews 10 (4%), literature 8 (4%):** records app; coded rules first [S5]; answer desk with citations.
- **Advice, design, research, 32 (14%):** stays manual, copilot at most. Agents rarely.
- Excel calculation sheets and e-mailed review comments glue teams today; PLM with document control replaces them (E1).

## 4. Gates

- **EU AI Act:** high-risk are safety components in road traffic, water, gas, heating or electricity (Annex III point 2) and employment uses (point 4) [S12], from 2 December 2027; AI in Annex I regulated products from 2 August 2028; Article 50 from 2 August 2026 [S13].
- **Machinery Regulation 2023/1230:** from 20 January 2027, covers AI safety functions [S14].
- **Pharma validation:** GMP Annex 11 (2011) governs computerised systems [S15].
- **Hazardous waste:** cross-border procedures electronic via DIWASS since 21 May 2026 [S16].
- **Energy:** RVO information duty every 4 years; research duty from 10 million kWh per site [S17].
- **GDPR and WOR:** art. 22 bars solely automated decisions on workers [S18]; injury records are health data, so a DPIA (E1). Time studies checking "aanwezigheid, gedrag of prestaties" need works council consent, WOR 27(1)(l) [S19].
