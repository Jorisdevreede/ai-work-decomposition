# Digest 53-3: Motor Vehicle Operators

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 7 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 6 occupations (truck, driver/sales, bus and shuttle drivers), all paperwork: reading load documents, keeping logs and trip reports.
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **TransFollow eCMR in Qargo TMS (TransFollow, Netherlands), consignment notes:** eCMRs created, issued and signed inside the TMS [S1]. Albert Heijn started eCMR in 2021 with carriers Simon Loos, Peter Appel and Cornelissen [S2]. Covers reading and checking load documents. E2.
- **Webfleet Tachograph Manager (Webfleet Solutions B.V.), logs:** remote tachograph download and archiving, remaining-driving-time alerts [S3]. Covers vehicle logs and hours. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **FCE Transport (NL), Qargo TMS with driver app:** the old TMS had no driver app; since the switch, invoices go out in 1-2 days instead of up to a week; no period given [S4]. E3, vendor case.
- No driver-only or bus-reporting case with a measured result found; searches covered eCMR and tachograph tools, and the shared search budget ran out before bus reporting.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Read and check load documents (2 tasks):** records app, configure existing software. The eCMR shows the assignment on the phone; rules check mandatory fields, weights and signatures. A paper CMR from a foreign shipper gets one bounded model step: photo to fields, compared with the TMS order; mismatches go to the planner (E1).
- **Logs and trip reports (5 tasks):** eliminate most of it. Tachograph, telematics and the driver app record trips, hours, mileage and fuel; the report becomes a dashboard. Delivery records come from proof-of-delivery scans. A fare log is not needed where passengers check in electronically (E1). Agent needed: none.

## 4. Gates

- **Reg. 561/2006:** 9 hours driving a day (10 twice a week), 56 a week, 90 per two weeks, a 45-minute break after 4.5 hours, checked through tachograph records at the roadside and the firm [S5].
- **Reg. 165/2014:** from 1 July 2026, vans of 2.5 to 3.5 tonnes in paid international transport need a tachograph [S6], so cross-border light-truck logs move to it.
- **eCMR and eFTI:** authorities must accept certified eFTI platform data from 9 July 2027 [S7]; abroad, a driver may still need a paper note [S8].
- **WOR art. 27 and GDPR:** telematics and driver apps observe location and behaviour; works council consent (k, l) [S9].
- **EU AI Act:** logs and documents touch no Annex III area; scoring drivers or allocating work by behaviour is 4(b) [S10], from 2 December 2027 [S11].
- **Reported failures and risks:** France's CNIL fined Amazon France Logistique €32 million (December 2023) for scanner indicators of exact idle time and 31-day retention [S12]. Trip data turned into per-person scores carries the same risk.
