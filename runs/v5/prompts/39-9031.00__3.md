# 39-9 Other Personal Care and Service Workers

O*NET minor group 39-9: 3 occupations with tasks in scope (Childcare Workers, Nannies, Residential Advisors), 3 automate and amplify tasks (3 automate, 0 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

Reading O*NET: two tasks are US-shaped. In the Netherlands paid home childcare runs through a gastouderbureau, which collects and passes on payments [S1]. Student rooms are let by housing providers by lottery or waiting time [S2], not by residential advisors.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Lesson plans: Teachmate (UK) drafts early-years activity ideas from an area of learning or a child's interest, and turns practitioner notes into observation drafts; educators stay responsible for checking [S3] E2.
- Records of play and meals: the Konnect OuderApp (Dutch) shows parents a timeline, a daily report in a digital diary, photos and the agenda [S4] E2.
- Room assignment: StarRez lets students pick rooms online within rules staff set by gender, year and community [S5], and shows students only rooms that fit an approved accommodation [S6] E2.
- Room lottery: SSH& (Nijmegen) has software draw its weekly room offer; students see how many others reacted [S2] E2.
- Gastouder billing software: no product page verified.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- Lesson planning: in an EEF trial in England evaluated by NFER, 129 science teachers using ChatGPT with a guide cut weekly planning from 81.5 to 56.2 minutes (31%) against 130 controls; a blind panel saw no quality difference [S7] E3. School teachers, not childcare.
- Room assignment: Gonzaga University (US) reports 97% of students with approved accommodations select rooms online and 235 staff hours saved per summer on room swaps (vendor case; per-round hours given as both 25 and 28) [S6] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Lesson plans (1 of 3 tasks): deterministic plus a bounded model loop.** A records app holds the centre's activity library, tagged to its pedagogical plan and age band. A model drafts an activity from a logged interest (artefact generator); the practitioner edits and signs. Check: each plan names a goal from the centre's plan (E1).
- **Records of play, meals and payment (1 of 3): configure.** A parent app's diary replaces the paper notebook and WhatsApp photo threads [S4]. Payment is plain code: registered hours, then the bureau invoices and pays by bank [S1].
- **Room assignment (1 of 3): deterministic.** Eligibility, lottery or waiting time and roommate preferences are configuration [S5]. One records app with self-selection replaces applicant spreadsheets mailed between housing office and landlord. A judgement step may read free-text accommodation requests; staff decide (E1).

## 4. Gates

- **AI Act:** Annex III point 3 covers assignment to education and evaluating learning outcomes [S8]. Lesson-plan drafting is not listed; scoring a toddler's development might come close to 3(b) (E1, untested). Parent-facing chatbots must say they are AI from 2 August 2026 [S9]. Annex III duties apply from 2 December 2027 [S10].
- **AVG:** Konnect asks parents for photo and diary consent at first login [S4]. Health and allergy notes are special-category data; pasting them into a general chatbot means a processor without a contract (E1).
- **Toeslag:** gastouder costs go via the bureau, by bank, not cash [S1].
- **Room allocation:** the Wet goed verhuurderschap requires a transparent procedure, objective published criteria and reasons for rejected candidates [S11]. Fully automated allocation needs a GDPR art. 22(2) basis [S12].
- **Works council:** apps that time-stamp each staff action can monitor performance: WOR art. 27(1)(l) consent [S13].
