# 39-4 Funeral attendants

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 2 automate/amplify tasks across 1 occupation (funeral attendants). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

The work is hands-on. The two scored tasks are paperwork: "prepare obituaries for newspapers" and "provide advice to mourners on how to make charitable donations in honor of the deceased". In the Netherlands the funeral director usually handles both (E1).

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Rouwadvertentiemaken.nl**: the family or funeral director builds a death notice online, or a designer sets it; placement in over 1,000 Dutch newspapers; the team checks each notice against the paper's requirements. For "prepare obituaries for newspapers". [S1] E2
- **Mensenlinq**: death notices in almost all Dutch daily and weekly papers, mourning cards, and a free online condolence register that funeral directors embed on their own site; "more than 100" funeral directors use it. [S2] E2
- US memorial sites offer AI obituary drafting (E1; their pages blocked our fetch).

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found. Searched Dutch death-notice platforms and funeral vendor pages for measured results; the web search budget ran out before a wider search of US funeral software.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Obituary**: an artefact generator. A form holds the facts (names, dates, place and time of the ceremony, wishes such as "no flowers, a gift to ..."). Code fills the newspaper template, checks size and deadline per paper, and prices it. Rung: deterministic, as the products above already work.
- **The personal sentence**: a bounded model step. The model drafts two or three versions from the family's own words. Check: every name, date and place in the draft must equal a form field, and nothing new may appear. Stop when the family picks or edits one. Nothing goes to print without the family's approval of the proof.
- **Donation advice**: an answer desk that is mostly lookup. The family names the cause; code checks it against the Belastingdienst ANBI list [S3] and the CBF register of recognised charities [S4], then prints the account, a payment reference and a QR code on the card, the notice and the condolence page. Rung: deterministic. Tax deductibility follows fixed rules [S3]; a model adds nothing.
- **Glue today**: phone and e-mail between family, funeral director, printer and newspaper. One funeral file feeding card, notice and condolence page ends retyping (E1).
- **Stays with a person**: the conversation with the family and the ceremony.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR**: it does not apply to the personal data of the deceased (recital 27) [S5], but it does apply to the relatives named in a notice and to people who sign a condolence register. Set a retention period; Mensenlinq lets the family take a page offline [S2].
- **Tax**: gifts are deductible only to an ANBI (or, as periodic gifts, to a qualifying association), above 1% of threshold income with a EUR 60 minimum and up to 10% [S3]. Point mourners to the ANBI list and the CBF register [S4], not a model's memory.
- **EU AI Act**: no Annex III point applies to death notices or donation advice. Art. 50(4) requires a deployer that publishes AI-generated text "to inform the public on matters of public interest" to say so, unless a person reviewed it and holds editorial responsibility [S6]. A family-approved notice falls outside that duty or within the exception (E1 reading).
- **Main risk**: a wrong name or date in print cannot be recalled, so the field check is a hard stop.

## Sources

[S2] Online condoleanceregister, Mensenlinq, undated, https://mensenlinq.nl/mogelijkheden/online-condoleanceregister
[S3] Zijn giften aan goede doelen aftrekbaar?, Belastingdienst, undated, https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/gift-aftrekken
[S4] CBF: toezichthouder goede doelen, CBF, undated, https://cbf.nl/
[S5] Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), EUR-Lex, 2016-04-27, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
[S6] EU AI Act Article 50: transparency obligations, Future of Life Institute (artificialintelligenceact.eu), 2024-06-13, https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/
