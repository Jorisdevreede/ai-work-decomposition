# 39-7 Tour and Travel Guides

O*NET minor group 39-7: 2 occupations (tour guides and escorts; travel guides), 9 automate and amplify tasks (3 automate, 6 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

O*NET mixes North American wilderness guiding (hunting, fishing, expeditions) with tour escorts; in the Netherlands the desk work sits at tour operators (E1).

## 1. Products that do this today

- Plan itineraries, arrange details: Lemax (Croatia) builds tours from reusable components and automates supplier orders through confirmation and payment [S1] E2.
- Sightseeing advice: TUI piloted ChatGPT in its UK app to shortlist excursions bookable through TUI, August 2023 [S2] E2.
- Plan itineraries by chat: Layla (Berlin) plans and books trips in conversation [S3] E2.
- Wildlife information: ObsIdentify (Observation International, Naturalis) names wild species in Europe from a photo [S4] E2.
- Fishing rules: the VISplanner map shows where a permit holder may fish and, with the Kleine VISpas, is the legal permission [S5] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- My Costa Rica, Lemax customer: "can now produce itineraries 3x faster"; vendor testimonial, no baseline [S1] E3.
- Layla: USD 1 billion planned trip value from 5 million users, company figures, March 2026; usage, not an outcome [S3] E3.
- None found for advice quality or problem resolution.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Plan itineraries; select routes and sites** (2 of 9): rung configure. A product library holds sites, opening and travel times; code checks the sequence. A model may draft a variant; every stop must match a product record (E1).
- **Arrange accommodation, transport, equipment, medical cover** (1 of 9): supplier integration and automated confirmations [S1]; medical cover stays a phone call (E1).
- **Clerical work, routing messages** (1 of 9): workflow automation from mailbox to booking record (E1).
- **Advice on sightseeing and shopping; wildlife and rules** (2 of 9): answer desk over the operator's catalogue [S2] and official rule data [S5]. Species naming is a bounded model step the guide confirms (E1).
- **Research site history, conditions, client abilities** (1 of 9): bounded model loop, a source for every fact [S6].
- **Sell packages** (1 of 9): records app and booking engine; the pre-contract information form [S7] comes from the record, not from a model (E1).
- **Resolve itinerary and service problems** (1 of 9): conversation plus notification; a person decides on site (E1).
- Glue today: supplier confirmations by e-mail, itineraries in Word, guide WhatsApp groups; a booking records app with supplier links replaces most (E1). No agent needed.

## 4. Gates

- **Package travel:** the organiser answers for all package services whoever performs them, must remedy non-conformity and assist travellers in difficulty without undue delay [S7]; Dutch law: Book 7 title 7A BW [S8]. AI-made itineraries stay the organiser's liability.
- **Wrong answers:** a Canadian tribunal held Air Canada liable for its chatbot's wrong refund advice in 2024 [S9]; not Dutch law, but a warning.
- **Invented places:** in 2025 chatbot itineraries sent tourists in Peru to a canyon that does not exist [S6].
- **AI Act:** from 2 August 2026 article 50 says to tell travellers they talk to an AI [S10]. No Annex III area covers tourism [S11]; Annex III duties start 2 December 2027 [S12].
- **GDPR:** client fitness and medical needs are health data (art. 9); explicit consent is one exception [S13].
- **Works council:** WOR 27(1)(l) gives a consent right on systems fit to monitor attendance, behaviour or performance [S14], such as GPS tracking of guides (E1).
