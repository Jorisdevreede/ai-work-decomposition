# 51-8 Plant and system operators (power, nuclear, chemical, gas, refinery)

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 17 automate/amplify tasks across 8 occupations (power plant, nuclear reactor, chemical plant and refinery operators; power dispatchers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Shiftconnector (eschbach, Germany)**: digital shift handover with sensor data and field notes, linked to SAP and AVEVA PI; used by Covestro, BASF, DSM. For "maintain logs, reports" and "read logsheets". [S1, S2] E2
- **AVEVA Mobile Operator with PI System (AVEVA, UK)**: digital operator rounds feeding the historian. For "record and compile operational data". [S3] E2
- **j5 Operations Logbook and Shift Handover (Hexagon, Sweden)**: replaced paper handover with standard entries and automatic reports at INPEX (LNG). [S4] E2
- **Probabilistic congestion forecasting (N-SIDE, Belgium)**: day-ahead congestion scenarios, load flow per critical element, operator dashboard. For "calculate load estimates". [S5] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **TenneT control room, Netherlands, 2025**: six-month test on all voltage levels against TenneT's point forecast: 63% fewer missed congestions at equal false-alarm rate, 47% fewer false alarms at equal security. Vendor-published; it notes the baseline lacked intraday updates. [S5] E3
- **BASF Antwerp, Belgium, 2024**: 40 large production sites built their own digital rounds; 650,000 readings reached PI in one year. No time saving reported. Customer talk at a vendor event. [S3] E3
- **Northern Star, Pogo plant, Alaska**: Excel logsheets with VBA uploads replaced by historian-based logsheets; "more than 1500 hours per year" freed. Vendor case, no method given. [S6] E3

## 3. Deterministic first

The calculations are fixed formulas: "calculate test result values", "material requirements or yields", "receipts and deliveries of oil" (gauging, temperature correction). Configure them in LIMS, historian or tank management.

"Record operating data", "compile operations records... gauge readings": the historian already holds most values (eliminate re-keying); field readings go through a rounds app with limits. The glue today is Excel logsheets with macros, paper rounds and handover notes in documents and mail.

"Read logsheets to... detect malfunctions": limit and trend rules first. A bounded model step drafts the handover from free-text entries. Check: each line links to a log entry. Stop: the incoming operator signs.

"Prepare and submit compliance... reports", "verify well field monitoring data conforms": a report generator with rule checks; a model drafts only exceedance narratives.

"Review and edit standard operating procedures": a model proposes redlines and flags tag mismatches. Check: management of change and independent review. Stop: nothing takes effect unsigned. "Read and interpret instruction manuals": an answer desk that cites the page or stays silent.

Control actions, switching, permits and reactor operation stay with operators.

## 4. Gates

- **EU AI Act annex III point 2**: high-risk when AI is a safety component in the supply of gas, heating or electricity [S7], meaning it directly protects physical integrity and is not needed for the system to function. Log drafts and forecasts are not; a model that trips or interlocks would be.
- **Seveso III**: the safety management system covers operating procedures and management of change (annex III) [S8]; Bal article 4.11 imposes it in the Netherlands [S9]. An AI-edited procedure is a change.
- **NIS2**: covers electricity producers and grid operators, refineries, gas networks and chemical makers [S10]. Article 21(2)(d) supply-chain security: a cloud model reading plant data is a supplier to assess.
- **Nuclear**: ANVS supervises Borssele; EPZ's licence runs to end 2033 and life extension needs a safety review [S11].
- **WOR art. 27(1)(l)**: logs and rounds record who did what, when; works council consent [S12].
