# 41-2 Retail Sales Workers

O*NET minor group 41-2: 5 occupations (cashiers, gambling change persons and booth cashiers, counter and rental clerks, parts salespersons, retail salespersons), 17 automate and amplify tasks (12 automate, 5 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

Mostly till and record work. The casino booth tasks are US-flavoured (E1).

## 1. Products that do this today

- Recommend merchandise: bol Shophulp, a beta for all customers since 15 September 2026, asks follow-up questions and compares features, price and delivery [S1] E2. Sponsored products can appear in it [S2].
- Part lookup and substitutes: Algolia auto parts search filters by vehicle fitment and suggests alternatives [S3] E2; TecDoc Catalogue finds parts by VIN, plate or OE number [S4] E2.
- Determine a part from the old one: Kramp app recognises products from a photo, in beta [S5] E2.
- Rental reservations and records: Booqable tracks availability and rental history per item, with barcode check-in [S6] E2.
- Till reconciliation: Lightspeed X-Series shows expected totals per payment type at closing; staff enter the count [S7] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- Albert Heijn service agent on Rasa: about 30 fixed flows plus retrieval over FAQs; 50% prevented contact rate, satisfaction up 0.5 on a 5-point scale. Vendor case, AH's own metrics [S8] E3.
- ECR Retail Loss study (Prof. Matt Hopkins, 39 retailers): 54% of transactions in equipped stores go through self-checkout; shrink up 22% in the first year; losses 33% above comparable stores; missed scans in 1% to 4.8% of transactions [S9] E3.
- Dutch supermarkets: nine in ten registers are self-service (CCV via De Telegraaf); Jumbo staffs four in ten [S10] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Totals, reconciliations, balance sheets, chip values, posting charges, sales slips, sales records (9 of 17 tasks):** eliminate or configure. The POS computes and records; closing compares expected with counted [S7] (E1 for charge posting). Glue today: Excel cash-up sheets and mailed Z-reports; replace with a POS-to-ledger integration and a variance dashboard (E1).
- **Catalogue lookup, rental reservations (2 of 17):** records app plus fitment data integration [S4] [S6].
- **Rental information, complaints, back-orders (2 of 17):** answer desk, fixed flows first, retrieval for the long tail [S8]; back-order updates are a notification (E1).
- **Shipping costs and tracking (1 of 17):** carrier API integration (E1).
- **Recommend merchandise, advise substitutes, determine parts (3 of 17):** deterministic plus a bounded model loop. The model reads the need; fitment and stock data check each suggestion; the clerk confirms photo matches (E1). No agent needed.

## 4. Gates

- **Cameras:** the AP formally warned a Jumbo store in 2020: entering is not consent to facial recognition, and shoplifting is no overriding public interest [S11]. Veesion behaviour cameras ran in over 110 Dutch supermarkets in 2024, with false alarms; the AP wants necessity shown [S12].
- **AI Act:** emotion recognition at work is banned, art. 5(1)(f) [S13]. Remote biometric identification and staff performance monitoring are high-risk, Annex III 1(a) and 4(b) [S14], from 2 December 2027 [S15]. Shopping chatbots must say they are AI (art. 50) [S16], from 2 August 2026 [S15].
- **GDPR:** art. 22 bars solely automated decisions with significant effect [S17]; a self-scan ban from a risk score needs a person, cameras a DPIA (E1).
- **Works council:** WOR 27(1)(l) consent for tools fit to monitor staff, such as till cameras and per-cashier variance reports; 27(1)(k) for staff data [S18].
- **Risks:** self-checkout loss [S9]; advice bots depend on seller product data [S1].
