# Digest 23-1: Lawyers, Judges, and Related Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 36 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 5 occupations (lawyers, judicial law clerks, administrative law judges, arbitrators and mediators, judges).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Libra Legal AI Workspace (Wolters Kluwer, Netherlands), research and drafting:** research, drafting and review over Dutch legislation, case law and commentary; live in the Netherlands and Belgium [S1]. E2.
- **Legora (Sweden), review and drafting:** tabular review of document sets, legal research, a Word add-in [S2]. E2.
- **AAA-ICDR AI Arbitrator (US), draft awards:** opt-in, documents-only construction cases; a human arbitrator reviews and authorises each award [S3]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Ashurst, drafting trial (400+ people):** 45% time saved on first-draft legal briefings, 80% on corporate filings drawn from articles of association [S4]. E3, firm's own trial, June 2024.
- **Law students, randomised trial:** Vincent AI and o1-preview raised productivity by 50% to 130% on five of six legal tasks, with better quality [S5]. E3, academic, 2025.
- **Stanford RegLab/HAI, research tools:** on 200+ queries Lexis+ AI was wrong over 17% of the time, Westlaw AI-Assisted Research over 34% [S6]. E3, academic, May 2024.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Case admin (calendar, dockets, mediation appointments, practice admin; 4 tasks):** records app with date rules and notifications; a docket dashboard flags late cases. No model. E1.
- **Law updates and library (2 tasks):** integration with wetten.overheid.nl and publisher feeds, matched to open cases by article number. A model writes the impact note, which must cite the changed article. E1.
- **Research and reading (statutes, pleadings, claim documents, records, staff questions; 11 tasks):** answer desk on a licensed source base, deterministic plus a bounded model loop. Every citation must resolve to a real ECLI or article in that base; two failed retries go to a person. E1.
- **Drafting (contracts, briefs, opinions, decisions, settlements, laws, appeal explanations; 10 tasks):** artefact generator. Templates and clause libraries first; the appeal-rights text is fixed. A model drafts only free-text reasoning; a person signs. E1.
- **Judgement (outcomes, strategy, liability, claim advice; 5 tasks):** judgement step. **People work (court, clients, probate, trusteeship; 4 tasks):** stays manual.
- **Glue:** deadlines and case status live in Outlook and Excel; a case records app with a deadline engine replaces them. Agent needed: none.

## 4. Gates

- **Advocatenwet art. 10a and 11a:** confidentiality is a core duty and binds staff [S7]. NOvA: no confidential data in free tools, check citations and case law by hand, the lawyer stays responsible [S8].
- **AI Act Annex III 8(a):** AI used by or for a judicial authority, or in ADR, to apply law to facts is high-risk [S9], from 2 December 2027 [S10]. This hits clerk, judge and arbitrator tasks; 5(a) hits public benefit claims. In-house legal work is not in scope.
- **Rechtspraak:** targets low-risk uses such as scheduling and pseudonymising rulings; no AI in high-risk processes for now, no "robotrechter" [S11].
- **GDPR and WOR:** legal tool vendors need an Art. 28 processor contract [S12]; Art. 22 covers solely automated decisions [S13]; WOR art. 27(l) applies if tools log lawyers' output [S14].
- **Reported failures:** €2,315 in costs for a lawyer's fake Hoge Raad quote (August 2026) [S15]; full costs for unchecked AI-drafted pleadings, Rechtbank Oost-Brabant, December 2025 [S16]; a Gelderland judge used ChatGPT to set solar panel lifespan (June 2024) [S17].
