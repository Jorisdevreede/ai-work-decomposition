# 47-4 Other Construction and Related Workers

O*NET minor group 47-4: 2 occupations (Energy Auditors; Weatherization Installers and Technicians), 6 automate and amplify tasks (4 automate, 2 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source. Weatherization is a US low-income programme role; the Dutch match is the energy adviser (E1).

## 1. Products that do this today

- Analyse bills and usage: Ella Energy (Flanders) reads three years of quarter-hour Fluvius meter data, predicts normal use, flags deviations, checks invoices; generative AI writes the alerts [S1] E2.
- Patterns of use, customer report, job records: OptiMiser calibrates its home model against utility bills, writes customer-ready reports, and its Nexus module keeps job records and quality checks [S2] E2.
- Answer customer questions: ENGIE's Agentforce chat in Belgium answers in French and Dutch, explains high bills and suggests off-peak use [S3] E2.
- Draft replies: Kraken's Magic Ink drafts replies for Octopus Energy staff [S4] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- ENGIE (Belgium, Italy): 71% of cases resolved without staff, 1,500+ conversations a day, 83% response accuracy. Vendor case, Salesforce [S3] E3.
- Octopus Energy: about 35% of customer emails written with Magic Ink, around 70% satisfaction; a third of drafts need zero to minimal edits. Kraken's own figures via techUK, 2024 [S4] E3.
- Audit reports and insulation bids: none found.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Bills and tariffs (1 of 6):** integration: meter data with consent, tariffs as configuration, weather normalisation in code. A bounded model step reads scanned bills only, checked by lines adding up to the total (E1).
- **Patterns of use (amplify):** degree-day regression and load profiles are deterministic, shown on a dashboard. A model may draft the anomaly explanation; numbers come from the calculation [S1].
- **Educating customers, explaining measures (2 of 6):** an answer desk over a fixed table of measures and savings. The model writes, a calculator supplies every number, contract questions go to a person, as at ENGIE [S3].
- **Work logs (1 of 6):** a records app (job, hours, materials, photos) linked to invoicing replaces paper forms and crew spreadsheets [S2].
- **Bids and reports (1 of 6):** artefact generator. Quantities and prices fill a template from the job record, a model drafts the narrative, a person signs. A Dutch energy label is computed by NTA 8800 software after a site visit and registered in EP-online, so a model prepares, never issues [S5].
- Mailboxes now carry bill PDFs and Word quotes; the meter link, records app and quote generator replace them (E1). No agent needed.

## 4. Gates

- **AVG:** meter readings are personal data, open only to grid operator and supplier [S6]. A subsidy or credit decision made solely by a model falls under art. 22 [S7]. Profiling many households needs a DPIA (E1).
- **AI Act:** a chatbot must say it is AI (art. 50(1)) [S8], applying since 2 August 2026 [S9][S10]. High-risk only if a public body uses AI to judge eligibility for public assistance (Annex III 5(a)) or as a safety component in heating or electricity supply (point 2) [S11], from 2 December 2027 [S9][S10].
- **WOR 27(1)(l):** GPS and time stamps in a crew app monitor attendance and performance: works council consent [S12].
- **Sector:** labels need a site visit [S5]. Firms report energy measures every four years against the recognised measures list; large firms also face the EED audit [S13].
- **Reported risk:** at 83% accuracy, about one ENGIE answer in six falls short, by the vendor's own figure [S3].
