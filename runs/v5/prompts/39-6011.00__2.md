# Digest 39-6: Baggage Porters, Bellhops, and Concierges

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 6 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 1 occupation (concierges).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **HiJiffy, hotel guest messaging:** a Virtual Concierge answers guests around the clock on the website, WhatsApp and booking-site chat, and hands the rest to staff with context and a suggested reply; 100+ hotel-system integrations [S1]. Vendor says up to 90% of conversations run automatically and 2,600+ hotels use it [S1]. For local information and simple bookings. E2.
- **Tiqets, museum, attraction and tour tickets:** a free affiliate and API programme lets partners resell its catalogue for commission [S2]. For tickets and sightseeing. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **AX Hotels (Malta), HiJiffy:** over 50,000 conversations, 93% handled automatically, 45 to 70 staff hours saved per month and add-on sales up 18%, February 2025 to February 2026 [S3]. E3, vendor case.
- **Lake District Hotels (UK, six hotels), HiJiffy:** phone calls down over 70% for the question types the bot answers, £52.7K direct room bookings and 500+ table reservations, March to August 2022 [S4]. E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Local information (1 task, importance 4.59):** answer desk over a list the concierge keeps (venues, hours, notes, own tips). A bounded model phrases the answer. Check: every venue named comes from the list. Stop: no match, hand to the desk. E1.
- **Reservations and transport (2 tasks):** integration where a booking API exists (tickets, shuttle timetable, spa in the hotel system). Elsewhere a request ticket in a records app tracks status and notifies the guest; phone bookings stay manual. E1.
- **Tours, events, unusual requests (3 tasks, mostly with people):** stay manual and rest on judgement and contacts. A model may draft options and a checklist; the request log tracks them. Agent: none needed. E1.
- **Glue:** WhatsApp, phone notes and a desk logbook carry requests between concierge, restaurant and housekeeping today. One request ticket in the messaging tool or hotel system, visible to each team, replaces them. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **Package travel rules (EU):** booking two travel services for one trip can create a package or a linked travel arrangement, with liability for performance and insolvency protection. A tour or ticket counts only at 25% or more of the trip's value or as an essential feature [S5]. Check before the desk bundles train, car and tours (E1).
- **GDPR:** allergies or accessibility needs count as health data (E1), a special category; explicit consent is one lawful ground [S6].
- **EU AI Act:** no Annex III area applies [S7]. Guests must be told they are chatting with AI [S8].
- **WOR article 27:** console statistics on staff response times can check performance; works council consent needed [S9].
- **Reported failures and risks:** a Canadian tribunal held Air Canada liable for its chatbot's wrong fare advice (CA$812, February 2024) [S10]. A hotel bot quoting wrong prices or opening hours carries the same kind of risk (E1).
