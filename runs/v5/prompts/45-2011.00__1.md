# 45-2 Agricultural Workers

O*NET minor group 45-2: 2 occupations (Animal Breeders; Farmworkers and Laborers, Crop, Nursery, and Greenhouse), 2 automate and amplify tasks (2 automate, 0 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

Both tasks are record keeping. In the EU the crop-protection record is a legal duty of the professional user, not only good practice.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Semen logs and animals bred: CRV Dier (Dutch breeding cooperative CRV) records do-it-yourself inseminations and natural services and keeps straws per bull per canister; stock updates when an insemination is entered [S1] E2.
- Crop records, pesticide use: AgroVision Crops (Netherlands, Belgium, Poland) logs sowing, spraying, fertilising and harvest on a phone in the field and "warns when unauthorized products are used or when the authorised dose is exceeded" [S2] E2.
- Crop registration in the greenhouse: LetsGrow's app registers plants and clusters directly in the greenhouse [S3] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found. Vendor farm-app pages with time-saved claims blocked access, so they are not cited.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Semen and breeding log (1 of 2 tasks): deterministic, close to eliminate.** Entering the straw at insemination writes the cow's record and lowers stock, as in CRV Dier [S1]. The straw already carries collection date, donor and the centre's approval number [S4]; an integration links it to the breeding company's records. Today a barn notebook or spreadsheet sits between the tank and the herd system (E1). No model step is needed.
- **Crop records: pesticide use, yields, costs (1 of 2 tasks): deterministic.** A records app with a product database checks authorisation and dose at entry [S2]. Sprayer and harvest data (time, area, dose, yield) come in by integration from machine terminals and weighbridges; costs come from purchase invoices in the bookkeeping (E1). A dashboard shows cost and yield per plot. Today paper notes, the adviser's e-mail or WhatsApp advice and a spreadsheet for the accountant glue this together (E1). A bounded model step can read free-text spray advice or a scanned invoice into the record; the check is the product database and dose limit before saving. An agent is not needed.

## 4. Gates

- **Plant protection records:** users must record product name, time and dose of use, area and crop. Records not made electronically must be moved to a machine-readable format within 30 days, for uses from 1 January 2026. Member States may waive the transfer for uses before 1 January 2027 [S5]. A Dutch draft makes incorrect or incomplete (digital) records fineable at EUR 1,500 or EUR 750, from 1 January 2027 [S6].
- **Germinal products:** semen collection centres keep records of each donor, its health tests, collection dates and the semen's identification and destination [S4].
- **Data ownership:** the EU Data Act has applied since 12 September 2025. Users of connected products, farm machines included, may access the data they generate and share it with third parties [S7]. So sprayer data can feed a records app.
- **GDPR and WOR:** a sole trader's farm records can be personal data (E1). Logging harvest or registration per greenhouse worker is monitoring of "aanwezigheid, gedrag of prestaties" and needs works council consent under WOR 27(1)(l) [S8].
- **EU AI Act:** no Annex III area touches these tasks. AI in regulated products (Annex I, such as machinery) is high-risk from 2 August 2028; Article 50 transparency applies from 2 August 2026 [S9].
