# Digest 49-9: Other Installation, Maintenance, and Repair Occupations

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 15 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 8 occupations (door, valve, HVAC, industrial machinery, medical equipment, vending, locksmith, geothermal).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Ultimo EAM (IFS Ultimo Group B.V., Dutch, owned by IFS), records and mobile work orders:** preventive plans and repair history [S1]; a medical-device module and barcode fault reporting for hospital technicians [S2]. E2.
- **OutSmart (Visma, Nieuwegein), digital work orders:** "quote to invoice" for installers and door companies, with AFAS and AccountView links [S3]. E2.
- **MaintainX, AI features:** voice memos transcribed into the work order; a procedure generator that drafts checklists, reviewed before release; a prompt to confirm unusual readings [S4]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Dr. Schär (food, 12 plants), Ultimo rollout:** about 30% less downtime, no baseline period stated [S1]. E3, vendor case.
- **Altitude Infra (French fibre network, 500 technicians), Praxedo work orders:** about 8 hours per technician per week saved on work-order mail, entry and reports; period not stated [S5]. E3, vendor case.
- **North County Vending (US), Cantaloupe telemetry:** San Francisco routes went from 12 to 6 drivers; fills per visit rose 75% (2009) [S6]. E3, trade press.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Repair and maintenance records (8 tasks):** records app, configure existing software. The record falls out of closing the work order: fault and cause pick lists, parts, start and stop times. Bounded model step: draft text from a voice note and suggest a fault code; the technician confirms; no matching code goes to the planner. E1.
- **Cash, merchandise and transaction logs (2 vending tasks):** integration plus code. Telemetry feeds sales and cash; a rule flags a count gap above a set amount and notifies a named person. E1.
- **Key and lock register (1):** records app with holder, dates, audit trail. E1.
- **Parts sourcing (1 amplify):** catalogue and purchase integration. Bounded model loop matches a free-text part to catalogue lines; a buyer approves; no match or price over limit stops it. E1.
- **SOP drafting (1 amplify):** artefact generator; an engineer signs off; versions live in the CMMS [S4]. E2.
- **Load computations (1):** deterministic calculator. E1.
- **Crew schedules (1):** planning board with rules for skills, shifts and priority. E1.
- **Agent:** none.
- **Glue:** paper work orders, Excel logs and mail between technicians, planners and finance; a mobile work order plus an ERP link replaces them [S5]. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **F-gas Regulation (EU) 2024/573:** leak checks (article 5) and logbook rules (article 7) bind HVAC records; put the logbook fields in the work order [S7].
- **Dutch hospital medical technology covenant:** procedures for preventive maintenance by internal and external technicians, and formal release after every job; one hospital runs this in Ultimo [S8].
- **Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230:** from 20 January 2027; covers AI-powered safety functions, not records [S9].
- **WOR article 27(k) and (l):** work orders with time stamps or location can observe technicians; works council consent first [S10].
- **AI Act Annex III 4(b):** only if a model allocates crew work by behaviour or traits, or monitors performance [S11]; from 2 December 2027 [S12]. Rule-based scheduling is outside it.
- **Reported failures and risks:** documented failures sit in the old process: no mobile use, double entry, manual logbooks [S1][S5]. Generated procedures need review [S4]. No independent report found.
