# 39-1 Supervisors of gambling, recreation and personal service workers

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 6 automate/amplify tasks across 3 occupations (first-line supervisors of gambling services, of entertainment and recreation, and of personal service workers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Shiftbase (Shiftbase B.V., NL)**: shift scheduling with availability, open shifts, swaps, conflict warnings and auto-scheduling; labour cost targets against turnover from the till; time tracking; payroll export to Nmbrs, AFAS and Exact Online. For "assign work schedules, following work requirements" and "analyze and record personnel or operational data". [S1] E2
- **Planday**: AI-built rotas, staffing forecasts, availability in the schedule, shift swaps approved from anywhere. Same tasks. [S2] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found for casinos, attractions or salons. Searched Shiftbase and Planday pages for scheduling time or labour cost results. Context only: on Planday's page Meddyg Care says the Planday and Xero link cut payroll from a whole week to three days; a customer quote on a vendor page, not about scheduling. [S2]

## 3. Deterministic first

- **"Assign work schedules"**: configure a scheduler. Hard rules: skills, availability, contract hours, rest times. The supervisor approves and publishes.
- **"Determine how many gaming tables to open each day and schedule staff accordingly"**: a dashboard with a plain forecast from past visitor counts, bookings and events, and a rule for players per table. Staffing follows the table plan. No model needed.
- **"Direct workers compiling summary sheets ... amounts wagered and amounts to be paid to winners"**: the betting or cash system already holds these figures; the sheet becomes a generated report (eliminate the manual compilation).
- **"Analyze and record personnel or operational data and write related activity reports"**: a dashboard plus a bounded model step that drafts the narrative. Check: every number in the text matches the dashboard. Stop: the supervisor edits and signs.
- **"Furnish customers with information on events or activities"**: a website or answer desk fed by the event calendar. A chat step only if it answers from the calendar; stop: hand over to staff.
- **Glue today**: rosters in Excel, shift swaps in WhatsApp groups, paper tallies.
- **Stays with a person**: floor supervision, talking to players about their gambling, disputes, coaching staff.

## 4. Gates

- **Arbeidstijdenwet article 4:2**: without a collective rule, a roster is announced at least 28 days ahead; where work makes that impossible, working times at least 4 days ahead [S3]. Auto-scheduling must keep to this.
- **WOR article 27(1)(b) and (l)**: a working and rest-time arrangement, and a time clock that can watch attendance or performance, need works council consent [S4].
- **EU AI Act**: annex III 4(b) applies if an AI tool allocates shifts based on individual behaviour or personal traits, or monitors staff performance [S5]. A scheduler on availability and contract hours does not. Article 4: AI literacy.
- **Gambling**: the Kansspelautoriteit supervises the Wwft for land-based and online providers since 2016; unusual transactions go to FIU-Nederland [S6]. Casinos and arcades check each player in Cruks before play [S7]. Casino and arcade licence holders report quarterly and yearly, totalisator holders quarterly [S8]. Payout figures feed these reports, so a generated summary sheet needs an audit trail.
