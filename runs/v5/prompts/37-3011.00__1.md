Reading O*NET: the record task is written for US pesticide rules. In the Netherlands professional use outside agriculture falls under a ban with exceptions [S1], so the Dutch volume of this task is small.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Pesticide application records: AgroVision Crops (Dutch) keeps spray registrations and updates product stock from suppliers' electronic delivery notes, ready for an NVWA inspection [S2] E2. Its material targets growers, not green-space contractors.
- Tree information to the public: Ezytreev (UK) takes resident enquiries through a map portal or FixMyStreet, routes them to a tree officer's app and sends standard letters and emails with progress updates [S3] E2.
- Tree information to the public: ArcGIS Tree Management (Esri) offers a public viewer of the condition of public trees, a programme website and a request form [S4] E2.
- A model that drafts tree-care advice for residents: none found.

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Record pesticide applications (1 of 2 tasks): deterministic, records app.** The record is a fixed form: product name and authorisation number, day, dose per hectare, location, area treated, EPPO land-use code [S5]. A picklist from the authorisation register and supplier messages fill most fields [S2]. Today paper spray books and spreadsheets travel by mail to the client; the rule that contractors must give their client access or a copy [S5] is an integration or a shared export. A notification covers the transfer deadline. No model step is needed.
- **Give the public tree information (1 of 2): deterministic plus a bounded model loop.** Most questions are about status or ownership ("when is my tree pruned", "whose tree is this"). A records app, a public map and notifications answer those [S3][S4]. Care advice can come from an answer desk that drafts only from the council's own tree policy and FAQ; an arborist checks anything about safety, felling or permits (E1). On-site risk advice stays manual.

## 4. Gates

- **EU records rule:** Implementing Regulation 2023/564 applies from 1 January 2026: machine-readable electronic records, paper records transferred within 30 days [S5]. Regulation 2025/2203 lets member states exempt uses before 1 January 2027 from that transfer [S6]; the Dutch minister said he would support it [S7]. Whether the Netherlands applied the option: not verified.
- **Dutch ban:** the government calls the ban on professional use outside agriculture "already in force" but is repairing its legal basis; the earlier bill was withdrawn on 20 December 2024 [S1]. Check the status before advising a Dutch client.
- **Reported risk:** the NVWA found 68% of inspected growers applied products correctly in 2025, low for the third year; most breaches were drift measures [S8]. This is farm data, not amenity.
- **AI Act:** neither task falls in an Annex III area [S9]. A resident-facing chatbot must say it is an AI system (art. 50(1)), from 2 August 2026 [S10].
- **AVG and works council:** records name the applicator. Sprayer GPS or telematics that show who sprayed where can monitor workers, which needs works-council consent under WOR art. 27(1)(l) [S11]. Systematic location tracking points to a DPIA (E1).
