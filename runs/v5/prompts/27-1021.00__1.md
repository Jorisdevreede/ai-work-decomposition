# 27-1 Art and Design Workers

O*NET minor group 27-1: 8 occupations (e.g. art directors, graphic, interior, industrial and fashion designers, fine and craft artists), 30 automate and amplify tasks (5 automate, 25 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Generate new images: Adobe Creative Studio generates and edits images and attaches C2PA Content Credentials naming system, model and time [S1] E2.
- Layouts and web designs: Figma Make turns a prompt plus the team's design system into an editable prototype [S2] E2.
- Exhibit and campaign materials: Figma Buzz locks template areas, fills variants from a spreadsheet, edits images by prompt [S3] E2.
- Image archive: Bynder face recognition suggests names from earlier tags; a person confirms [S4] E2.
- Print instructions: Enfocus PitStop Pro (Ghent) checks PDFs against PDF/X profiles, fixes with action lists, reports each issue [S5] E2.
- Interior cost estimates: pCon.planner (EasternGraphics, Germany) builds priced article lists from a room plan, exports to Excel [S6] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- IBM: its marketing SVP says Firefly "cut content costs by 80%" and ideation went "from weeks to just two days" in one campaign. Vendor press release, no method [S7] E3.
- Zhou and Lee (PNAS Nexus, 2024), platform data: text-to-image adopters produced 2x more in month one, settling at +25%; ratings rose up to 50%; average novelty fell [S8] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- Research (12 of 30 tasks: trends, materials, codes, audiences, competitors): deterministic plus a bounded model loop. Answer desk citing the pages it read; a designer opens every link (E1).
- Charts, print notes, asset variants, image archive (about 5 tasks): deterministic. Charts from the data source, preflight profiles, spreadsheet-driven templates [S3][S5], a records app where the model only proposes tags (E1).
- Cost estimates and purchase lists (3 tasks): configure the catalogue tool [S6].
- Images, layouts, concepts, copy, marketing materials (about 10 tasks): artefact generator plus judgement step. The art director picks and edits; Content Credentials stay attached [S1] (E1).
- Client advice, feasibility, final selection: stay manual (E1). No agent needed.
- Glue today: briefs, proofs and approvals run through mailboxes and spreadsheet trackers (E1). Replace with an intake form, a workflow automation for proof rounds, and the DAM as record.

## 4. Gates

- AI Act art. 50, from 2 August 2026: providers mark generated images machine-readably; deployers disclose deep fakes, lighter for evident art or satire [S9][S10]. Systems already on the market have until 2 December 2026 to mark [S9]. In Dutch 2026 municipal campaigns, 90% of 192 AI posts had no label [S11].
- AI Act art. 5(1)(ba) and (bb), from 2 December 2026: bans generating non-consensual intimate images and child abuse material [S9]. Annex III does not cover design work (E1).
- Copyright: the Munich Local Court (13 February 2026) denied protection to three AI-generated logos, despite a 1,700-character prompt [S12]. A European Parliament study finds fully AI outputs unprotected and the training opt-out ill-suited [S13]. Hamburg's appeal court rejected a photographer's claim against LAION (10 December 2025) [S14]. A client may not own an AI logo; contracts should say so (E1).
- AVG art. 9: face recognition in an archive processes biometric data, banned without explicit consent [S15]. DPIA first (E1).
- WOR art. 27(1)(k) and (l): staff-data rules and tools fit for monitoring need works-council consent; void if the council objects within a month [S16].
