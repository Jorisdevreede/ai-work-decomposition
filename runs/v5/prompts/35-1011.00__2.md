# 35-1 Supervisors of Food Preparation and Serving Workers

O*NET minor group 35-1: 2 occupations (chefs and head cooks; first-line supervisors of food preparation and serving), 7 automate and amplify tasks (3 automate, 4 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Menu pricing from recipe cost: Apicbase (Antwerp) rolls ingredient prices up to portion cost, applies connected suppliers' price changes and adds labour for a prime cost [S1] E2.
- Staff and supply forecast: Tenzo (London) forecasts sales with machine learning; managers adjust it in an app and schedule from it [S2] E2.
- Recording production data: Orbisk (Amsterdam) photographs food as it is binned and records item, reason and waste stream [S3] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- Nando's Singapore, 8 restaurants: forecast 25% more accurate, 30% after manager adjustments; sales per labour hour up 15%. Vendor case: three-month pilot at four sites against sites without the tool, method not stated, about 2020 [S2] E3.
- Novotel Amsterdam City: food waste down 33% in under 12 months, 3,300 kg and EUR 27,000 saved, from the hotel's Orbisk data. Vendor case, 13 June 2025 [S3] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Recipe arithmetic, 3 of 7 tasks** (price menu items, estimate supplies and cost, ingredients per recipe): rung configure. A records app holds recipes as bills of materials; an integration pulls supplier prices and POS sales. Covers times quantity times price is plain code [S1].
- **Record production data** (1 of 7): records app with digital checklists and fridge sensors replaces paper HACCP forms. Waste-camera labelling is a bounded model step, checked by the scale weight and a person confirming (E1).
- **Forecast staff, equipment and supply from the master menu** (1 of 7): statistical forecast and dashboard; the manager adjusts. In the Nando's case that correction added accuracy, so keep it [S2].
- **Coordinate purchasing and budgets; set objectives and policies** (2 of 7): judgement step. A model may draft a budget note or policy; the manager signs off (E1).
- Glue today: Excel cost sheets, supplier orders by phone, mail or WhatsApp, paper temperature lists. Replace with one recipe and stock app linked to supplier webshops and the POS (E1). No agent needed.

## 4. Gates

- **HACCP and NVWA:** a food business needs a HACCP plan, met by the NVWA-approved Hygiënecode voor de horeca; inspectors check the records unannounced [S4]. The business chooses how it shows compliance; keep records six months, one year for unchilled display [S5].
- **Allergens:** guests must get information on the 14 EU allergens [S6]. In 2022 only 41% of inspected horeca complied; often the information was not available in writing or electronically [S7]. Recipe data that feeds allergen cards needs a person to check each change (E1).
- **Scheduling:** the Horeca cao lets on-call staff be called 24 hours ahead; the law says 4 days [S8].
- **Works council:** WOR 27(1)(k) and (l) give consent rights on staff data and on systems fit to monitor attendance, behaviour or performance [S9].
- **AI Act:** Annex III 4(b) covers task allocation by individual behaviour or traits and performance monitoring [S10], from 2 December 2027 [S11]. A headcount forecast is outside; ranking staff for shifts is inside (E1). Article 50 applies from 2 August 2026 if a chatbot talks to guests or staff [S12].
- **GDPR:** art. 22 bars decisions based solely on automated processing with significant effect, save exceptions with safeguards [S13]. Auto-assigned shifts without review come close (E1).
