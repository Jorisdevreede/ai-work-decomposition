# Digest 25-4: Librarians, Curators, and Archivists

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 40 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 5 occupations (archivists, curators, museum technicians, librarians, library technicians).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Transkribus (READ-COOP SCE, European cooperative), handwriting recognition:** over 90 million images processed by October 2024 [S1]; a model for one hand needs 30-40 transcribed pages [S2]. E2.
- **Alma AI Metadata Assistant (Ex Libris), catalogue drafts:** drafts a record from title, author or a title-page photo; the cataloguer accepts, corrects or discards [S3]. E2.
- **Annif (National Library of Finland, open source), subject suggestions:** the KB tested it on the Dutch Brinkman thesaurus; demo removed October 2025 [S4]. E2.
- **Primo Research Assistant (Ex Libris):** answers reference questions from the top five abstracts, with source links [S5]. E2.
- **Microsoft Purview, retention labels:** labels company records by keyword query or trained classifier; up to seven days to apply [S6]. E2.
- **Hanwell (UK), climate monitoring:** sensors with alarms hold the Mary Rose at 18-20°C and 50-58% humidity [S7]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Nationaal Archief and Noord-Hollands Archief, HTR:** about 6,000 hand-transcribed scans trained a model that transcribed about 2 million pages, with over 90% of characters correct [S8]. E3, academic.
- **GLOBALISE (Huygens Institute), VOC archive:** 4,802,212 scans from 1610-1796 searchable since October 2023, uncorrected [S9]. E3, academic.
- **OCLC WorldCat, duplicates:** a model tuned on about 34,000 user-labelled duplicates removed about 5.4 million duplicates, August 2023 to February 2025 [S10]. E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Admin (8 tasks: patron records, notices, orders, statistics):** configure the library system: scheduled notices, shared-catalogue copy cataloguing, a usage dashboard. E1.
- **Description (8 tasks: classification, archival descriptions, data entry, cataloguing, bibliographic checks):** records app plus a bounded model loop. The model suggests headings or a description; code checks headings against the thesaurus and fields against the shared catalogue; a cataloguer accepts. Stop: nothing accepted, catalogue by hand. HTR only where a sample meets an agreed error rate. E1.
- **Reference (9 tasks: reference searches, lists, bibliographies, policy questions):** answer desk over the own catalogue; code drops citations that resolve to no record; no hit goes to a librarian. E1.
- **Writing (6 tasks: grants, reports, policies, posters, summaries, publication selection):** artefact generator from templates; a person edits and signs. E1.
- **Preservation (1 task):** sensors, thresholds, notification. E1.
- **Stays manual (8 tasks):** teaching, programmes, directing staff, appraisal, acquisition research. No agent needed. E1.
- **Glue:** Excel and Access lists, mailed order sheets and shared drives move into the acquisitions module, collection database and a retention file plan. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **Archiefwet 2026:** passed the Eerste Kamer 12 May 2026, published 19 June 2026, start by royal decree [S11]. VNG expects 1 January 2027, transfer within 10 years instead of 20; the Woo demands more active publication [S12]. Both bind government bodies, not a private firm (E1).
- **Company records:** basic administration 7 years, real-estate data 10; files must stay usable at an audit [S13].
- **GDPR:** public-interest archiving gets safeguards and narrow rights exemptions [S14]; a company archive does not (E1).
- **EU AI Act:** no Annex III area applies [S15]. A reference chatbot must say it is AI [S16].
- **WOR article 27:** consent needed if system logs check a cataloguer's output [S17].
- **Reported failures and risks:** the Library of Virginia estimates 15 percent of emailed reference questions are ChatGPT-generated, some citing invented works [S18]. GLOBALISE warns of transcription errors [S9].
