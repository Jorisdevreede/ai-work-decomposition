# 51-3 Food Processing Workers

O*NET minor group 51-3: 2 occupations (Bakers; Butchers and Meat Cutters), 2 automate and amplify tasks (1 automate, 1 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

The butcher task ("issued to cooks") reads like a US institutional kitchen; in a Dutch shop it is goods-in, batch and sales records.

## 1. Products that do this today

- New recipe ideas: Tastewise narrows trend data to flavour concepts and finalists [S1] E2.
- Trend to draft recipe: Red Rabbit's nextfood.ai (Hamburg) drafts formulations and screens them on synthetic consumers, linked to Crespel & Deiters' ingredient navigator. Vendor claim: "from several weeks to just a few days", no customer named [S2] E2.
- Recipe specs, allergens, cost price: Aptean Bakery ERP (on Business Central) keeps formulas, flags allergens and shows cost per product [S3] E2.
- Meat received and batch records: Bizerba BizTrack at E.Leclerc Trélissac creates batches each morning from carcass traceability numbers; the labeller prints the batch on every tray; yield and margin per carcass follow [S4] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- Mademoiselle Desserts with Tastewise: one seasonal cake launched after "1–2 iterations", 145% sales lift since the 2024 launch. Vendor case; the page does not say who measured the lift or against what [S1] E3.
- Butcher records: none found.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Record meat received, issued and sold (1 of 2 tasks): deterministic, close to eliminate.** Scale and till already record every sale. Goods-in is a records app fed by an integration: the supplier's delivery data or label scan gives description, quantity, supplier, lot and dispatch date, as EU law asks, updated daily [S5]. Beef labels carry a reference code, slaughterhouse and cutting plant numbers and countries of birth, rearing and slaughter [S6]. Stock (received minus sold minus issued) goes on a dashboard. Today a paper register or spreadsheet glues supplier notes to the till (E1). A bounded model step only reads a paper delivery note, checked against the order and the label lot.
- **Develop new recipes (1 of 2 tasks): deterministic plus a bounded model loop.** A model drafts ideas and variants. Plain code computes baker's percentages, cost price and the allergen table from ingredient specs, never from the model's text. Test bakes and tasting stay manual.

## 4. Gates

- **Food law:** one step back, one step forward; records kept until the food is assumed eaten, handed over "without undue delay" [S5]; beef labelling under Regulation 1760/2000 [S6].
- **Allergens on loose goods:** a new recipe must update the allergen information. In 2021 the NVWA inspected 1,910 artisan producers (bakeries, ice cream shops, butchers); about half did not comply [S7].
- **Tax:** stock and sales records are basic records (basisgegevens), kept 7 years [S8].
- **EU AI Act:** no Annex III area applies (those rules start 2 December 2027). Article 50 transparency applies from 2 August 2026 if customers meet a chatbot or generated content [S9].
- **GDPR and WOR:** no personal data except till logins. Ranking staff on them is personnel monitoring, consent under WOR 27(1)(l) where a council exists (E1, model knowledge).
