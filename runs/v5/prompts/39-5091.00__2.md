# 39-5 Barbers, hairdressers and skincare specialists

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 7 automate/amplify tasks across 4 occupations (barbers, hairdressers, shampooers, skincare specialists). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

This is hands-on work. The automate tasks are the booking and record-keeping around the chair.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Salonized (Treatwell Salonized NL B.V., NL)**: online agenda and 24/7 booking from the salon's website, Google or Facebook; automatic reminders against no-shows; payments; financial overview. For "schedule client appointments". [S1] E2
- **Salonized client management**: paperless client cards with treatment and purchase history, appointment and treatment notes, before-and-after photos, intake forms and signed digital forms. For "keep card files on clientele", "maintain treatment records" and "keep records of client needs and preferences". [S2] E2
- **Treatwell (Treatwell NL)**: marketplace where clients find and book salons; Treatwell Connect app for the agenda and contactless payment. [S3] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found. Searched Salonized and Treatwell pages for no-show or admin-time results. They give usage counts (15,000+ salons, 150,000+ salons) and one owner's statement that at least 80% of bookings come in online, with no baseline; not counted. [S1, S3]

## 3. Deterministic first

- **"Schedule client appointments"**: configure online booking with service durations, staff calendars and reminders. Most phone booking disappears.
- **"Keep card files ... notes of work done, products used and fees charged after each visit"**: the client card in the salon system. Checkout writes products and fee to the card; the stylist types the colour formula or skin note. A model step adds little here.
- **"Paying bills"** and bookkeeping: bank feed and till export into an accounting package (deterministic).
- **"Stay abreast of latest industry trends"**: reading; stays with the person.
- **Glue today**: paper card boxes, WhatsApp and Instagram messages for bookings.
- **Stays with a person**: the cut, colour and treatment, advice, hiring and supervising staff.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR**: notes on skin conditions, allergies or reactions to dye are health data. Processing them is prohibited unless an exception applies; outside a healthcare treatment contract that usually means explicit consent, with strict requirements [S4]. Signed intake forms can record it [S2]; keep only what the treatment needs.
- **Tax records**: the business keeps its administration, bills and receipts for seven years (AWR article 52(4)) [S5].
- **EU AI Act**: no annex III point applies. Article 50(1) only if a chatbot takes bookings [S6].
- **WOR**: a works council is required from 50 staff (article 2); below that, article 27 consent for booking or time-clock systems does not arise [S7].
