# 49-1 Supervisors of mechanics, installers and repairers

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 2 automate/amplify tasks in 1 occupation (first-line supervisors of mechanics, installers and repairers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

Both tasks are about the maintenance system: compiling its records, and choosing and running it.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **IFS Ultimo (Ultimo Group B.V., Netherlands)**: enterprise asset management with downtime reporting by technicians, failure-mode (FMECA) maintenance strategies, ERP and supply-chain links, and "embedded AI features" the page does not name. For "compile ... repair or maintenance statistics" and "develop or implement electronic maintenance programs". [S1] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Castrol, Ghent (Belgium), Ultimo since 2016**: maintenance had been followed up partly in Excel. Time spent on breakdowns fell from about 300 to 115 hours a month, breakdowns halved, and savings were €360,000 a year, nearly 20% of the maintenance budget. Maturity rose from 40% to 82% over five years in yearly assessments. The maintenance manager is quoted on the vendor's page: a vendor case. [S2] E3
- **Dr. Schär (food, 12 production sites in 9 countries), 2025**: about 30% less downtime and shorter repair times after moving from reactive to preventive work. Vendor case study, no baseline given. [S3] E3

## 3. Deterministic first

- **"Compile operational or personnel records, such as time and production records, inventory data, repair or maintenance statistics, or test results"**: dashboards on the maintenance system (backlog, preventive share, mean time to repair, parts stock). Rung: configure. The Excel compile that Castrol started from goes away [S2].
- **Failure coding**: a bounded model step reads technicians' free-text notes and proposes a failure cause from a fixed list. Check: the code is on the list; a supervisor samples a share each week. Stop: low-confidence or unlisted cases go to the supervisor.
- **"Develop or implement electronic maintenance programs"**: a configuration and data-migration project. AI coding speeds up import scripts and integrations (E1). The maintenance strategy stays with people.

## 4. Gates

- **WOR article 27(1)(k) and (l)**: rules on processing staff data, and on systems able to monitor attendance, behaviour or performance, need works council consent [S4]. Time and production records per mechanic qualify.
- **EU AI Act**: AI that monitors and evaluates workers' performance is high-risk under annex III point 4(b) [S5], from 2 December 2027 [S6]. Asset dashboards are not; ranking mechanics would be.
