# Digest 43-9: Other Office and Administrative Support Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 75 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 9 occupations (keyers, typists, desktop publishers, insurance, mail and office clerks, proofreaders, statistical assistants, others).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Doxis, formerly Klippa (Groningen; SER Group, Germany):** reads invoices, receipts and email attachments into fields [S1][S2]. E2.
- **Rossum (Czech office; acquired by Coupa):** extracts, validates and posts invoices to the ERP [S3]. E2.
- **Paperbox (Ghent, Belgium), insurance mailroom:** turns insurers' emails and attachments into organised case files [S4]. E2.
- **PostNL digitale postkamer:** scans business post; turns forms into data [S5]. E2.
- **Shift Claims (Shift Technology):** triages claims, automates whole claims or parts [S6]. E2.
- **Amberscript (Dutch B.V.):** Dutch transcription, "90%+" by machine, "99%+" checked (vendor) [S7]. **CHILI GraFx (CHILI publish NV):** fills governed layout templates [S8]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Nationale-Nederlanden, home and windscreen claims:** simple claims paid in about six minutes; customers may ask for review; share unknown [S9]. E3, trade press.
- **Unnamed Dutch insurer, motor:** 91% of eligible claims (clear cover, two parties, under a threshold) decided automatically [S10]. E3, vendor case.
- **Fugro (Netherlands), 300,000 invoices a year:** 70% automated; 2 minutes to 35 seconds each [S3]. E3, vendor case.
- **Trust (Portugal), 150,000+ medical invoices a year:** about 80% touchless in month one (Feb 2025); 4 FTE to 1 [S11]. E3, vendor case.
- **Belfius:** 40% of mailroom work automated. **VKG:** over 60% of claims mail [S12][S13]. E3, vendor cases.
- **ONS (UK), occupation coding:** rules code 55-65% of answers; a tested model 70-76% at 92-96% accuracy [S14]. E3, public body.
- **DNB, six insurers, 2021:** no fully automated AI decisions; flagged claims always reach a person [S15]. E3, regulator.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Keying and checking (about 15 tasks):** eliminate. UBL e-invoices, forms and APIs deliver fields; ERP validation replaces re-keying. Dutch B2B e-invoicing is planned for July 2030, not yet law [S16]. A bounded model reads leftover PDFs, checked against KvK, IBAN and order data; below a threshold, a person.
- **Claims and policies (20 tasks):** records app plus rules: cover check, claim formula, payment, notices. A model classifies free-text letters; rules decide; exceptions to a handler. For a logistics firm: cargo and fleet claims (E1).
- **Typing, proofing, layout (28 tasks):** artefact generator from templates and data; spelling checks are configuration. A model drafts captions; a reviewer approves.
- **Statistics (11 tasks):** pipeline and dashboard; code lists first, a model for leftover free text.
- **Questions (3 tasks):** answer desk on approved policy text and record status; unknowns to a person.
- **Glue:** Excel and shared mailboxes link systems; integrations and a work queue replace them.
- **Agent:** none needed (E1).

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR art. 22:** a solely automated claim refusal needs human review and a way to contest [S17].
- **AI Act Annex III 5(c):** life and health pricing only; the Verbond reads non-life as not high-risk [S18][S19]. Cargo and fleet premiums: not Annex III.
- **Verbond Ethisch Kader 2025:** binding on insurers; covers claims, STP and chatbots; requires a register; asks whether less digitally able customers reach review [S19].
- **WOR art. 27(1)(l):** per-clerk speed or touchless dashboards need works council consent [S20].
- **Failures:** Whisper invented phrases in about 1% of transcriptions, 38% of those harmful [S21]. PHE dropped 15,841 positive cases (25 Sep-2 Oct 2020) at an XLS row limit [S22].
