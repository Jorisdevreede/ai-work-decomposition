# 47-2 Construction trades workers

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 4 automate/amplify tasks across 4 occupations (carpenters, electricians, plumbers and pipefitters, solar PV installers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

The trades are hands-on; the scored tasks are paperwork: work records, progress reports, PV operation records, ordering supplies.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Syntess Atrium (Syntess)**: ERP for installation firms. Work orders, hours and materials captured on site; service and maintenance orders; material ordering and call-off. [S1] E2
- **Exact for Bouw (Exact; bouw7.nl redirects here)**: staff and equipment planning in the app, hours logged in the app, purchase orders with invoice recognition, quality-check templates. [S2] E2
- **PlanRadar**: site diaries, defect logging, checklists, handover reports. For "written progress reports". [S3] E2
- **Sunny Portal (SMA)**: PV yield and status every 5 minutes, event lists, scheduled reports, alarms, an installer's whole fleet in one account. For "records of system operation". [S4] E2
- **2BA (Utrecht)**: product and price data hub for the Dutch installation sector that installer software connects to. For "ordering supplies". [S5] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **RRS Riool Reinigings Service, Enschede, 2009**: about 200 handhelds replaced paper work orders. Technicians had spent half an hour a day on hold for their next job. Orders per day rose from 1,500-1,600 to about 2,000; payback in six months. RRS director's figures in trade press; old, not independently measured. [S6] E3
- **FAGSI (German container-building firm)**: one project manager saves "about 10 hours of work per week" on site diaries, defects and acceptance with PlanRadar. Self-reported, vendor-published, no baseline. [S7] E3, vendor claim

## 3. Deterministic first

- **"Records of work assignments"**: a records app (digital work order) with planning board and mobile app: job, hours, materials, photos, signature. Rung: configure an existing ERP. Glue today: phone calls, WhatsApp, paper forms retyped by the office [S6].
- **Progress reports**: an artefact generator builds the daily report and quality file from checklist items, located photos and hours. Bounded model step: a fitter's voice note becomes checklist fields and a draft remark. Check: fields validated against the job checklist; a photo required for every item that will be closed in. Stop: the fitter confirms.
- **PV records**: integration with the inverter portal; an alarm opens a service ticket. Deterministic.
- **Ordering**: material list from the job calculation, priced from catalogue data, sent electronically. A model may read a handwritten list into article codes; unknown articles go to a person.
- **Stays with a person**: the work, measuring as built, signing test reports.

## 4. Gates

- **Wkb**: since 1 January 2024 for consequence class 1. The contractor and an independent quality assurer build the file the client submits at completion; contractors stay liable for defects found later [S8]. A KPCV task group reports this file often arrives late and too incomplete to enforce [S9].
- **Electrical**: building installations must meet NEN 1010 (Bbl art. 4.199) [S10]. Arbobesluit art. 3.4(3) requires up-to-date diagrams of every electrical installation; art. 3.5 limits hazardous electrical work to expert, instructed, authorised workers [S11].
- **GDPR**: GPS in company vans needs a legitimate interest and necessity; tracking private use usually fails [S12].
- **WOR art. 27(1)(l)**: hours, location and job logs per fitter observe presence and performance; works council consent [S13].
- **EU AI Act**: Annex III 4(b) makes high-risk an AI system that allocates tasks "based on individual behaviour or personal traits" or evaluates worker performance [S14]. Rule-based scheduling on skills and distance is not that; a model ranking fitters on speed is.
