# 51-9 Other production workers (inspectors, CNC programmers, coating, semiconductor, glass)

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 23 automate/amplify tasks across 11 occupations (CNC programmers, inspectors and testers, semiconductor technicians, coating operators, photo processing). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **ESPRIT EDGE with ProPlanAI (Hexagon, Sweden)**: learns from past jobs, suggests processes for new parts, programs simple repeat parts. For "determine the sequence of machine operations" and "select the proper cutting tools". [S1] E2
- **NX CAM feature-based machining and CAM Copilot (Siemens, Germany)**: recognises features, applies standard operations, proposes a sequence, learns from the programmer's choices. [S2] E2
- **Q-DAS O-QIS (Hexagon)**: SPC reading gauges, CMMs and PLCs; control charts, alarms, SAP QM link. For "compute defect percentages" and "analyze test data". [S3] E2
- **Inspekto (Siemens)**: visual inspection set up from 20 good samples, linked to PLC or MES. For "review computer-processed digital images" and pass/fail. [S4] E2
- **Critical Manufacturing MES (Portugal)**: recipe parameters computed by business rules, SPC, defect capture, SECS/GEM links. For "calculate etching time". [S5] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Siemens Electronics Works Amberg, Germany, 2019**: a model on process data predicts good solder joints so the X-ray test can be skipped: up to 30% fewer X-ray tests, no extra €500,000 X-ray machine. Siemens' own plant and slides; an "up to" figure. [S6] E3
- **Schmitt + Meissner, Germany, 2025**: Inspekto checks 100% of moulded automotive light panels in line; about 95% of inspection automated, payback under a year. Vendor reference, no baseline. [S7] E3
- Context only: CAM vendors claim up to 75% [S1] and 80% [S8] less programming time from own trials.

## 3. Deterministic first

Most automate tasks are arithmetic and records. "Compute defect percentages", "calculate etching time", "compute costs of labor and materials": formulas in SPC, MES recipes or ERP costing (configure). "Maintain logs of instrument readings", "record operational data on specified forms": machine data and tablet forms straight into MES or historian (eliminate). Paper sheets and Excel re-keyed or carried to QA are the glue today. "Upload digital images onto Web sites": an integration.

CNC programming is the amplify core. Feature recognition and templates are deterministic (configure the CAM library). A bounded model step suggests strategies from past jobs. Check: simulation with collision and gouge checks, then first-article inspection. Stop: no release without clean simulation and programmer sign-off. "Compare encoded tapes with part specifications" is simulation against CAD; "sort shop orders to minimize setup" is a scheduling solver.

Visual inspection is a bounded loop. Check: challenge parts each shift, false rejects and escapes in SPC. Stop: unsure parts go to an inspector. Polishing, glassblowing and setup stay manual.

## 4. Gates

- **EU AI Act**: no annex III point fits. Article 6(1) makes AI high-risk as a safety component of a product under annex I law, including machinery [S9]; sorting good from bad parts is not a safety function.
- **Machinery Regulation 2023/1230**, applying from 14 January 2027: safety components and embedded systems "with fully or partially self-evolving behaviour using machine learning approaches ensuring safety functions" need third-party conformity assessment (annex I part A, points 5-6) [S10]. Keep learning models out of guards and stops.
- **WOR art. 27(1)(k, l)**: consent for systems that can monitor staff presence, behaviour or performance; per-operator MES logs and line cameras qualify [S11]. DPIA (GDPR art. 35) where cameras capture staff [S12].
- **Risk**: Siemens says copilot output needs programmer validation and simulation before any NC code runs [S2]; failure means a crash or scrap.
