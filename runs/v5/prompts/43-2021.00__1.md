# 43-2 Communications Equipment Operators

O*NET minor group 43-2: 2 occupations (Switchboard Operators, Including Answering Service; Telephone Operators), 11 automate and amplify tasks (10 automate, 1 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

The telephone operator tasks (toll charges, directory assistance) are a legacy US telco job; in the Netherlands this work sits with receptions, switchboards and answering services (E1).

## 1. Products that do this today

- Answer simple questions and schedule appointments: Teams Phone Agent (generally available 30 September 2026) answers from a Questions and Answers source, books via Microsoft Bookings and routes calls, in Dutch too [S1] E2.
- Directory lookup and alternate spellings: Teams Dial by Name takes a spoken full or partial name, lists multiple matches and can add the department to separate namesakes [S2] E2.
- Answer from reference files: Webex AI Receptionist answers from uploaded files and transfers to people or queues; Dutch is not in its locale table [S3] E2.
- Intake and routing: CM.com Conversational AI Cloud identifies the caller, routes to the right person or department and answers FAQs [S4] E2.
- Records of calls and charges: the Teams PSTN usage report lists time, numbers, duration and charge per call, exportable for billing [S5] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- Switchboard routing, vendor case: AudioCodes Voca via Axians at an unnamed academic hospital automates about 13,000 calls a week, with about 50% lower workload at the central reception. No method stated [S6] E3.
- Message taking: none found.

## 3. Deterministic first

- Records of calls and charges (2 of 11 tasks): eliminate. The phone platform keeps the record [S5] (E1).
- Stamp and file messages (1 of 11): eliminate. A message logged in the CRM carries its own timestamp and notifies the colleague (E1).
- Directory updates and data entry (2 of 11): integration from the HR or identity system, replacing the reception's phone-list spreadsheet (E1). Teams search can lag changes by up to 24 hours [S2].
- Spelling and directory requests (2 of 11): deterministic fuzzy search, then disambiguation [S2].
- Record messages (1 of 11): deterministic plus a bounded model loop: transcribe, summarise into fixed fields, read back to the caller. A records app replaces the mailbox (E1).
- Simple questions from reference files (1 of 11): answer desk on approved files; the answer names its source, else a person takes over (E1).
- Order forms, appointments, rooms (2 of 11): conversation into workflow automation with field checks; staff confirm orders (E1).
- No task needs an agent.

## 4. Gates

- EU AI Act art. 50(1): callers must be told they talk to an AI unless that is obvious; art. 50(2): synthetic audio is marked machine-readably [S7]. They apply from August 2026 [S8]. Teams Phone Agent says "I'm a virtual assistant" by default; who disables it must inform callers in real time [S1].
- Annex III 5(d): AI that evaluates and classifies emergency calls or sets dispatch priority is high-risk [S9], with Annex III duties from 2 December 2027 [S8]. A switchboard bot passes emergencies straight to a person (E1).
- Art. 5(1)(f): no inferring operators' emotions at work [S7].
- WOR art. 27(1)(l): per-operator call statistics, listening in or recording need works council consent [S10].
- AVG: call records are personal data; Teams keeps Dutch records 180 days and masks 3 digits of external numbers [S5].
- Liability: a tribunal held Air Canada liable for its chatbot's wrong fare advice; the bot was not a separate entity [S11].
