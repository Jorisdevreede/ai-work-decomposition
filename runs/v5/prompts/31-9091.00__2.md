# Digest 31-9: Other Healthcare Support Occupations

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 28 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 7 occupations (medical transcriptionists 13, speech-language pathology assistants 5, dental and medical assistants, pharmacy aides, phlebotomists, massage therapists).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Juvoly (Netherlands), ambient scribe:** drafts the consult note for the clinician to approve. Vendor says 1,500+ GP practices (35% of Dutch practices), 200,000+ consults a month [S1]. Owner Tandem Health (Sweden, E1) says its scribe is CE Class IIa since 21 May 2026 [S2]. E2.
- **Autoscriber (Netherlands, E1) with ChipSoft HiX:** summarises the consult, recorded with consent; the doctor copies it into HiX. An API to fill the note and draft orders is planned [S3]. E2.
- **Microsoft Dragon Copilot:** ambient notes, dictation, referral letters, orders from the conversation; lists the Netherlands [S4]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **12 Dutch GPs and trainees, ambient scribe, December 2024 to July 2025 (535 consults):** documentation time fell 42.7 seconds per consult; total consult time did not change. Drawbacks: inaccurate summaries, patients holding back sensitive topics [S5]. E3, academic.
- **Capio (Nordics), Tandem scribe, 375,000 notes, 1,295 clinicians:** self-estimated time per note fell from 6.69 to 4.71 minutes (29%); measured median editing time was 93 seconds per note. Tandem co-authored [S6]. E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Dictation, transcription, editing (9 tasks):** artefact generator; configure a CE-marked scribe inside the EHR. The bounded model step turns speech into a fixed-template summary, which settles "what to include". Abbreviations and drug names are checked against fixed lists (E1). Check: the clinician signs. Stop: no unsigned note enters the dossier. Querying mistakes with doctors stays a judgement step.
- **Records and data entry (5 tasks):** records app plus integration: lab orders and specimen labels come from the order system (E1).
- **Scheduling, phones, case-progress questions (3 tasks):** online booking; a phone bot is fixed menus plus a bounded intent step, medical questions go to the assistant (E1).
- **Billing, claims, copays, drug usage (5 tasks):** tariff tables, standard claim messages, arithmetic. No model (E1).
- **Speech therapy data and charts (5 tasks):** dashboard; a model may draft exercise material for the therapist to pick (E1). Post-operative instructions (1 task): fixed templates.
- **Agent:** none.
- **Glue:** audio queued to typists, letters printed for signature, data retyped between systems. Replace with dictation and sign-off inside the EHR (E1).

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR Art. 9:** health data is special category; care use rests on 9(2)(h) with professional secrecy [S7].
- **WGBO:** the dossier is kept 20 years from the last change, shared only with consent, with an access log on request [S8]. Jeroen Bosch Ziekenhuis keeps only the checked summary and deletes the rest within a day [S9].
- **MDR:** Sweden's regulator found Class I "not compatible" for a scribe whose notes feed continued care; Class IIa is the floor [S2]. That brings AI Act Annex I rules from 2 August 2028 [S10] (E1 on the link). No Annex III area covers documentation.
- **AI Act Art. 50:** a phone bot tells callers they speak to AI [S11].
- **WOR art. 27(l), Annex III 4(b):** scribe usage logs can measure clinicians; using them on staff needs works council consent [S12] and is high-risk [S13].
- **Reported failures and risks:** a preprint found verified errors in 31.3% of 565 notes from three scribes on recorded or simulated consults [S14]. A GP practice employee put patient data into a public chatbot: a reported breach [S15].
