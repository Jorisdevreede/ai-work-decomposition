# 21-2 Clergy and directors of religious activities

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 12 automate/amplify tasks across 2 occupations (clergy; directors of religious activities and education). Most of the work is pastoral and stays with people; the automatable part is member administration, budgets, publicity and event logistics. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **LRP, Landelijke Ledenregistratie (Protestantse Kerk in Nederland)**: the national member register for Protestant congregations; moves and deaths arrive from the municipal register (BRP) through SILA. For "analyze member participation" and mailing lists. [S1] E2
- **DocBase with Exact Online (Roman Catholic parishes, Netherlands)**: member, church-contribution, cemetery and financial administration in one set, linked to SILA; DocBase is mandatory for all parishes. For "analyze revenue and program cost data". [S2, S3] E2
- **Actie Kerkbalans campaign kit (Actie Kerkbalans, Netherlands)**: yearly calendar, logo pack, social posts, banners and a digital-giving section for the annual church appeal. For "publicize programs" and "plan fundraising activities". [S4] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found. Searched Dutch church and parish administration (ledenadministratie, SILA, Kerkbalans, parish software) for deployments with a measured result; the session's web-search budget ran out before a wider search.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Records app and integration**: the member register with BRP updates through SILA already removes manual address keeping. Rung: configure.
- **Dashboards**: "analyze revenue and program cost data" and "member participation or changes" are queries over the accounting package and the register: pledges, giving, costs per programme, age cohorts, moves. Deterministic.
- **Artefact generator**: "newsletters, bulletins, or mailings" as a template filled from the event calendar and a consent-filtered member list. A bounded model step may draft the announcement text. Check: dates, places and names match the calendar; an editor approves. Stop: one draft per item.
- **Workflow automation**: "schedule special events", "reserving space", "ordering needed materials", "scheduling speakers": a room-booking calendar, a registration form and a supplier order list. Configure.
- **Glue today**: volunteer lists, pledge follow-up and event sign-ups in spreadsheets and mail between the church council, treasurer and volunteers (E1).
- **Stays with a person**: "study and interpret religious laws, doctrines", teaching, choosing curricula, devising ways to grow membership, fundraising strategy.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR art. 9**: membership of a church reveals religious belief, a special category. Art. 9(2)(d) allows a religious not-for-profit body to process it for members and regular contacts, but not to disclose it outside the body without consent [S5]. An external mail or AI service needs a processor contract (E1).
- **Church rules**: since 2020 the Protestant Church registers a new person only after checking they know of the registration and do not object to SILA updates [S1]. Catholics can have the SILA link removed [S3].
- **Reported failure**: the Roermond diocese reports gaps in the shared register, mainly young Catholics, because some Limburg parishes do not register new members [S2].
- **EU AI Act**: no Annex III point applies; congregational education does not decide admission to, or grade students in, an educational institution (point 3). Article 4 AI literacy applies to any AI use. [S6]
- **WOR art. 27** consent only matters where a church body employs 50 or more people (art. 2) [S7].
- **ANBI**: churches with ANBI status publish financial data online (E1).
