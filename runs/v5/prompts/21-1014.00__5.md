# 21-1 Counselors, social workers and community health workers

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 49 automate/amplify tasks across 12 occupations (mental health counselors, career counselors, social workers, probation officers, health educators, community health workers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Rapportage-assistent (Verticai, NL)**: records the Wmo/Jeugdwet intake of Stichting Wijkteams Amersfoort and drafts the report with language models; a worker checks every output. Pilot since May 2025, DPIA done [S1]. Task: "maintain case history records". E2
- **Spraakgestuurd Rapporteren (Attendi, NL)**: dictation in the ECD that turns spoken notes into a SOEP report or letter; elder care, disability care and GGZ, 300+ care organisations [S2]. E2
- **Autoscriber (NL)**: consultation notes into HiX, Nedap ONS and other EHRs, including GGZ and forensic care. Its "70% less time in GGZ" has no baseline: vendor claim [S3]. E2
- **Magic Notes (Beam, UK)**: turns recorded visits into case notes and assessments for council social workers [S4]. E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Somerset Council (UK), children's social care, 2025**: Magic Notes pilot, 20 staff. About 11 hours a week saved per practitioner (self-reported), weekly admin time down 46%, assessments submitted 65% faster. Council publication, no named evaluator [S4]. E3
- **Gemeente Hengelo with Deloitte, 2024**: summaries of Wmo kitchen-table talks. No figures. Weaker on mental complaints and multiple problems than on mobility; consultants kept their own notes, so little time was saved [S5]. E3, no number.
- Context, not a case: jeugdzorg staff spend 38% of their time on administration (CBS survey), the most of client-facing jobs [S6].

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Registration and management reports** ("numbers of applications completed", "reports for owner or management"): records app and dashboard fed by the case system and the iWmo/iJw flows between municipalities and providers [S7], replacing Excel tallies and mailed referrals (E1). Deterministic.
- **Progress notes and case files** ("reports describing offenders' progress", "progress notes"): transcript to draft is a bounded model step. Check: the worker approves; a validator flags any risk statement (suicide, violence, abuse) absent from the transcript. Stop: no approval, nothing written to the file.
- **Referral packets** ("packets containing information about social service agencies"): an answer desk quoting a maintained social map, not generated advice. Configure.
- **Forms and budgets** ("tax or rent forms", "food budgets"): prefilled forms, budget calculator. Deterministic.
- **Stays with a person**: eligibility, treatment plans, parole advice, cultural mediation, counselling.

## 4. Gates

- **Wmo 2015 art. 2.3.2**: investigation within six weeks, then a written report to the client [S8]; the AI draft becomes the worker's report.
- **GDPR**: health data is special category (art. 9) [S9]; no eligibility decision based solely on automated processing (art. 22) [S10]; DPIA first [S1].
- **EU AI Act**: annex III 5(a) covers eligibility for public assistance and services, including healthcare, by or for public authorities; 6(d) covers re-offending risk [S11]. Amersfoort claims the art. 6(3) "narrow procedural task" exception for drafting; profiling is always high-risk [S12, S1]. Annex III duties apply from 2 December 2027 [S13]; AI literacy (art. 4) applies now [S14].
- **WGBO** for GGZ: dossier kept 20 years [S15].
- **WOR art. 27(1)(k, l)**: recording staff conversations needs works council consent [S16].
- **Failures**: UK social workers found invented suicidal ideation and "fish fingers" in AI notes; one council's own check was less positive than the vendor's evaluation [S17]. Inspectie JenV found Reclassering's OxRec formulas swapped since 2018, about 21% of cases in another risk category, mostly too low; paused February 2026 [S18].
