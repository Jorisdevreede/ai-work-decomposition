# Digest 33-2: Firefighting and Prevention Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 3 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 2 occupations (firefighters 2, forest fire inspectors and prevention specialists 1).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **NIPV Kerncijfers (Netherlands), dashboards:** monthly incident figures from the fire service control rooms for the 25 safety regions, with response time split into processing, turn-out, driving and arrival time [S1]. Covers part of "maintain records and logbooks". E2.
- No product for drafting Dutch fire incident reports was found in this run.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **NIPV, residential fire alerts 2019 to 2023:** about half the rise reflects better registration, not more fires. NIPV finds fatal home fires through press reports; fire services then fill in an online questionnaire per fire [S2]. E3, independent.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Incident reports and logbooks (2 tasks):** records app plus integration. Times, address, units and alarm class come from the control-room record, so the crew adds only what they saw (E1). A bounded model step can turn a spoken debrief into the narrative field. Check: the officer edits and signs. Stop: no unreviewed text leaves the record. Prefilling the fatal-fire questionnaire from the incident record removes the press-report search (E1).
- **Public fire-prevention education (1 task):** stays with people. A model can draft material from the existing Brandweer.nl topic pages [S3]; a person edits it.
- **Agent:** none.
- **Glue:** Word reports, Excel logbooks and a separate questionnaire per fatal fire. Replace with one incident record that later forms read from (E1).

## 4. Gates

- **Archiefwet art. 3:** public bodies keep records in good, orderly and accessible state and destroy what is due [S4]. Safety-region incident reports are such records (E1 on the application).
- **GDPR Art. 9:** victim injury details are health data [S5].
- **AI Act:** Annex III area 5(d) covers classifying emergency calls and dispatch priority, not these tasks; no Annex III area applies [S6]. Published AI text that informs the public is disclosed unless a person holds editorial responsibility (Art. 50) [S7].
- **WOR art. 27(l):** voice or location logs that show crew performance need works council consent [S8].
- **Reported failures and risks:** registration changes can look like trends: about half the 2019 to 2023 alert rise came from registration [S2].
