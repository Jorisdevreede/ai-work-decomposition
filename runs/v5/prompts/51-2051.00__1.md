# Digest 51-2: Assemblers and Fabricators

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 3 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 2 occupations (electrical and electronic equipment assemblers, team assemblers).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Azumuta, digital work instructions:** builds variant-specific instructions from MRP jobs, pushes jobs to the shop floor, sends serial numbers back to the ERP, and records pass or fail from connected scanners and test equipment [S1]. Fits reading work orders into assembly steps, and the production reports. E2.
- **Siemens Industrial Copilot for Operations (built with Microsoft), Erlangen electronics plant:** turns machine error codes into plain language and suggests fixes from about 600 manuals and parts lists; operators ask in German on tablets. No measured savings given [S2]. Fits interpreting documents. E2.
- **Evocon, production monitoring:** replaced paper reports and spreadsheets at H+H factories [S3]. Fits the team production report. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Navtech Radar (UK), Azumuta:** end-to-end rework down 30%, with no baseline or period given; 30 minutes of admin saved per product through automatic serial-number matching [S1]. E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Production, time and waste reports (2 tasks):** records app at the station. Counts come from scanners and test equipment, time from job start and stop, scrap from a reason pick list. The report is a query, so the reporting task vanishes. Deterministic. E1.
- **Reading schematics, specifications and work orders (1 amplify):** the bill of materials in the ERP already lists materials; exploding it is plain code. Variant instructions come from the job [S1]. A bounded model step answers questions over the manual set [S2]; the check is that each answer cites a page, and no source means asking the engineer. Reading a drawing to derive materials stays with a person. E1.
- **Agent:** none.
- **Glue:** paper travellers, Excel waste logs and printed drawings. Station tablets linked to the ERP replace them. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230:** applies from 20 January 2027 where the firm builds machinery; allows digital instructions and covers AI-powered safety functions [S4]. A question-answering tool is not a safety function. E1.
- **WOR article 27(l):** station logins that record time and scrap per person can check performance; works council consent first [S5].
- **AI Act Annex III 4(b):** applies only if per-operator data is used to monitor and evaluate performance or allocate tasks on behaviour [S6].
- **Reported failures and risks:** none found in this run for these tasks.
