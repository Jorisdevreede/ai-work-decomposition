# Digest 39-2: Animal Care and Service Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 1 automate/amplify O*NET task across 1 occupation (animal caretakers).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **FirstVet, online vet:** vets advise by video and help owners judge whether a problem is urgent; included in major UK pet insurance policies; vendor says 1 million video calls [S1]. Its country list covers Sweden, the UK, Denmark, Germany, Finland, Norway and the US, not the Netherlands [S2]. E2.
- **LICG (Netherlands), pet care information:** independent advice per species on care, health and welfare, supported by the Ministry of LVVN, the Utrecht vet faculty and Dierenbescherming [S3]. A vetted content base for an answer desk. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found. Looked for measured results of pet-care chatbots and online vet triage in Europe; found only vendor counts without a base or period [S1].

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Advise owners on pet health (1 task, with people):** answer desk over vetted care sheets per species, plus red-flag rules in code (not eating, bleeding, breathing trouble: call a vet now). A bounded model maps the owner's free text to species and topic and quotes the sheet. Check: every answer cites a sheet. Stop: any symptom, medicine or dose question goes to a vet. Much of the value is deterministic: a care record per animal that sends vaccination, worming and weight-check reminders. Agent: none. E1.
- **Glue:** advice given by phone and care notes in Word become a care record per animal with sheets sent to the owner. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **Wet dieren:** professional "diergeneeskundige handelingen", which include examining an animal and prescribing or giving treatment for diagnosis or prevention, are reserved for admitted persons such as registered vets [S4]. General care advice is fine; diagnosis by staff or a bot is not (E1 on where the line falls).
- **GDPR:** owner contact data is personal data; animal health data is not a special category (E1).
- **EU AI Act:** no Annex III area applies [S5]. A care chatbot must say it is AI [S6].
- **WOR article 27:** only touched if the tool logs and scores staff answers (E1).
- **Reported failures and risks:** a Canadian tribunal held Air Canada liable for its chatbot's wrong advice (CA$812, February 2024) [S7]. A pet shop or kennel whose bot gives wrong care advice carries the same kind of risk (E1).
