# Digest 37-1: Supervisors of Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 9 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 2 occupations (housekeeping and janitorial supervisors, landscaping and groundskeeping supervisors).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **FacilityApps (Purmerend, Netherlands), cleaning operations suite:** schedules per site and employee with working-time and cao checks; clock-in by GPS, QR or NFC linked to payroll; digital work orders; dashboards for clients. Vendor says 500+ cleaning companies [S1]. Schedules, hours, reports. E2.
- **FacilityApps quality control app:** predefined DKS inspection forms, photos as evidence, reports for clients [S2]. Reports on work performed. E2.
- **Planbition (Netherlands), workforce planning:** scheduling and time registration that apply collective-agreement rules [S3]. Planbition X drafts a schedule from a plain-language brief, explains each shift choice, and the planner can override [S4]. Cleaning is not among its listed sectors. E2.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Schedules, staffing and stock forecasts (2 tasks, importance 4.33 and 4.20):** configure existing software. Hours follow from the client's room data and frequencies, which tenders supply [S6], times norm minutes per room type (E1). Code checks cao and working-time rules; stock uses reorder points per site. Optional bounded model: draft the roster from a brief, code checks hard rules, the planner publishes [S4].
- **Hours, payroll, leave and time sheets (2 tasks):** records app plus clock-in to payroll integration [S1]. No model.
- **Activity, personnel and project reports and records (3 tasks):** dashboard over rows that already exist. Quality rules are exact too: one tender sets VSR-KMS 3.0 sampling four times a year, AQL 7% for offices and 4% for sanitary rooms, pass or fail per room group, and a 10% fee penalty after a second fail [S6]. Plain code.
- **Service estimates (1 task):** calculation from labour, material and machine rates [S5]. A bounded model may turn a free-text request into line items; the supervisor checks each line.
- **Customer questions on methods, materials, prices (1 task):** answer desk on the firm's own price list and method sheets.
- **Glue:** spreadsheet rosters, paper inspection forms, mailed monthly client reports (E1), replaced by one planning, time and quality system with a client view [S1].
- **Agent:** none.

## 4. Gates

- **WOR art. 27:** a roster scheme sets working and rest times: consent under 27(b). Clock-in and inspection results tied to named cleaners can check attendance or performance: 27(l), plus 27(k) for staff data [S7].
- **AI Act:** an AI scheduler that allocates shifts on behaviour or traits, or scores cleaners, is Annex III 4(b) [S8], with obligations from 2 December 2027 [S9]; workers' representatives are informed before use [S10]. A customer chatbot must say it is AI [S11].
- **GDPR:** an automated roster or performance decision with significant effect needs human intervention and a right to contest [S12].
- **Cao:** the cleaning cao runs 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2028, signed by Schoonmakend Nederland and CNV; generally binding status requested [S13]. Rule tables must follow it.
- **Reported failures and risks:** the AP fined a maintenance company EUR 15,000 for recording sickness causes and leaving absence records online without authentication [S14].
