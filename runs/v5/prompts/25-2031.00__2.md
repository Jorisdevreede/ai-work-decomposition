# 25-2 Preschool, primary, secondary and special education teachers

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15, with XSCOUT signals. 65 automate/amplify tasks across 13 occupations (primary, secondary, career/technical, special education teachers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Snappet (NL)**: adaptive practice; AI drafts a class activity for the lesson goal [S1]; its Copilot, piloted at Lucas Onderwijs, suggests arithmetic lessons the teacher accepts or changes [S2]. Tasks: "prepare objectives", "adapt materials". E2
- **LessonUp (NL)**: its lesson editor rewrites text at another level, summarises, translates [S3]. Task: "prepare materials". E2
- **ParnasSys (NL)**: pupil records, OPP module, learning-goal tracking, Parro parent app; no AI named [S4]. Task: "maintain student records". E2
- **CheckMate (Cito, NL)**: suggests scores for open questions; seven teachers test it; one says 1.5 hours of marking took under 15 minutes (his account) [S5]. E2, pilot.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **259 science teachers, 68 English secondary schools, 2024**: ChatGPT plus a guide cut weekly planning from 81.5 to 56.2 minutes (-31%); teacher panel: no quality difference. Randomised; NFER for the EEF, independent [S6]. E3
- **464 primary teachers, 108 English schools, autumn 2025**: Oak's Aila cut weekly preparation from 3.3 to 2.5 hours (-24%), quality no worse. But it covered 7% of lessons, use declined over ten weeks, and under one in five felt it meaningfully influenced their teaching. Randomised; NFER for the EEF [S7]. E3
- **Context (survey)**: Teacher Tapp, October 2026: 7% of secondary teachers use AI for marking; 31% expected to in 2023 [S8].

## 3. Deterministic first

- **"Prepare reports on students", "maintain student records"**: data sits in ParnasSys, Magister or Somtoday (E1); a report is a query plus template. A model drafts the comment from teacher notes; check: each sentence traces to a mark or note, no diagnostic labels; stop: the teacher signs, or writes it after two redrafts.
- **"Prepare objectives and outlines"**: core goals as a fixed list; a model drafts; check: each objective maps to a goal code.
- **"Assign and grade class work"**: closed items marked by code. Open answers: a model suggests a rubric score; check: the teacher sees every answer, double-marks a sample; stop: below agreed agreement, manual.
- **"Write IEPs"** (Dutch OPP): records app with legal deadlines as timers; a NOLAI project will draft the justification against quality criteria; no results yet [S9].
- **Glue** (E1): group plans and score exports in spreadsheets, parent mail outside the system.
- **Stays human**: instruction, adapting methods, remedial help, disability strategies.

## 4. Gates

- **AI Act annex III point 3**: AI that admits pupils, evaluates learning outcomes, sets the education level or watches tests is high-risk [S10] from 2 December 2027 [S11]. Marks that count fall here. Inferring pupils' emotions is banned [S12]. A school adapting an AI tool can become its provider [S13].
- **GDPR**: public task (art. 6(1)(e)); support needs in an OPP are health data (art. 9); DPIA for new pupil-data systems (art. 35) (E1). SURF advised against Microsoft 365 Copilot in 2024; a 2025 re-review still found two medium risks, including wrong personal data [S14, S15].
- **Medezeggenschap**: WOR does not apply to schools; under the WMS staff consent to tools that can monitor them, parents and pupils to pupil-data rules (art. 12-14, 39) [S16].
- **OPP**: set within six weeks of enrolment after consulting parents, evaluated yearly (WPO 40a) [S17].
- **Failure**: Texas machine-scores state tests; rescoring requests rose from 2,758 (2024) to 64,489 (2026); computers first scored 58% of 13,300+ raised reading scores [S18].
