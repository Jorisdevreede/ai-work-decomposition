# 19-5 Occupational Health and Safety Specialists and Technicians

O*NET minor group 19-5: 2 occupations (Occupational Health and Safety Specialists; Occupational Health and Safety Technicians), 6 automate and amplify tasks (4 automate, 2 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Incident trends and reports: Quentic (Berlin, AMCS Group) Pattern Analysis flags recurring issues and hotspots; its Reporting Assistant turns notes, photos and voice recordings into draft reports (January 2026) [S1] E2.
- Environmental and substance records: EcoOnline SDS Smart Extraction reads supplier safety data sheets; a person reviews before records are created [S2] E2.
- Orientation materials: Mitti (by SafetyCulture) converts documents to inspection templates and translates training courses [S3] E2.
- Incident capture: Benchmark Gensuite's Describe It AI gives frontline writing help [S4] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- US Bureau of Labor Statistics: machine coding of injury narratives grew from 26% of occupation codes (2014) to about 92% of all codes (2021-22), with an expected dip to 81% (2023). Codes are assigned only above a confidence threshold. Measured by BLS [S5] E3.
- EHS vendor cases with a measured safety result: none found; Quentic and EcoOnline publish usage figures and claims only [S1][S2].

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Incident trends (1 of 6):** a records app with fixed fields (cause, body part, location, days lost) and a dashboard. The bounded model step codes the free-text narrative; below a confidence threshold a person codes it, as at BLS [S5].
- **Reports (1 of 6):** figures come from the records by template. A model drafts the investigation narrative; every fact must link to a record; the investigator signs [S1].
- **Orientations (amplify):** a model drafts course and quiz from the RI&E and house rules; a safety expert approves (E1).
- **Environmental records (1 of 6):** records app plus integrations; reading a data sheet is a bounded model step with review [S2].
- **Safety equipment specs (1 of 6):** configure the purchasing catalogue: approved items only, each with its EN standard, risk category, CE marking and EU declaration of conformity [S6]. A model may fill fields from a datasheet; the declaration is the check.
- **Reviewing inspection, lab and staffing records (amplify):** judgement step. A model pulls findings and deadlines into the RI&E action plan [S7]; a person decides.
- Today incident forms arrive by mail and accident lists and action plans live in spreadsheets; a records app with notifications replaces them (E1). No agent needed.

## 4. Gates

- **Arbowet:** written RI&E with action plan (art. 5); serious accidents reported at once to the Labour Inspectorate, plus a list of accidents with over three days' absence (art. 9); the arbodienst or company doctor tests the RI&E (art. 14) [S7]. A model must not delay a report.
- **AVG:** the AP fined maintenance firm CP&A EUR 15,000 for recording the cause of employees' sickness; that belongs with the company doctor [S8]. Keep medical detail out of trend data.
- **AI Act:** inferring workers' emotions is banned since 2 February 2025, except for medical or safety reasons [S9][S10][S11]. Monitoring or evaluating workers' behaviour is high-risk (Annex III 4(b)) [S12] from 2 December 2027 [S10][S11]. Camera-based PPE or fatigue checks need legal review (E1).
- **WOR 27(1)(d) and (l):** arbo rules and systems that can monitor behaviour or performance need works council consent [S13].
- **Reported risk:** in LabSafety Bench (Nature Machine Intelligence, 2026) no model passed 70% accuracy at hazard identification [S14]. Model-written safety material needs expert review.
