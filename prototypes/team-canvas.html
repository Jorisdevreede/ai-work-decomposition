The team's Amdahl limit

How the calculation works
Time left = Σ task hours × (1 − adoption + adoption ÷ task speedup).
Team speedup = total hours ÷ time left.
Hard ceiling = total hours ÷ hours that stay at 1×.

Human work and machines stay at 1× unless a model explicitly estimates otherwise. Review and handoffs must already be included in task estimates. The ceiling assumes no new demand and no new bottleneck.
