# The recruiter job

Two recruiters, one journey. A person applies, or an agency sends a name. A manager decides. At 90 days they are still here, or not. That is the North Star.

Three flows. Vacancy to hire: requirements, match, inform, interview, recommend, status. Candidate mail repeats duties, pay and shift. Agencies place 150 to 300 flex workers through three firms. Outlook and Excel are the join: Recruitee holds the vacancy, AFAS the scale, Studytube the certificate, SharePoint the wording.

Keep the systems. Five blocks: a fact pack; a clock of quiet days; an agency order a site lead files, pay fields required; a knockout confirmed once per vacancy (pass, fail, or needs a look; a fail names a field; no score); an outcome table of stage days, source, offer acceptance and still here at 90 days. Loops extract, match an approved snippet, or draft. A person releases the checklist, a rejection, outreach, an order and a criterion change. Interview, referral, reference judgement and test result stay human. No loop reads a medical or drug file. Interviews are not transcribed: the note can store a bad question [S6].

Pack, clock and stock mails first; later blocks quote them. Then the agency order, after terms with the three agencies. Then the knockout, only after works-council consent, which CV screening needs now [S14]. Then the question bank and scorecard. Then references, only with consent on file. The outcome table last, once day-90 dates exist.

## Three builds

**Stage clock and stock status.** 295 hours × 82% × €65 = €15,700. North Star input: days in stage. Uniper named 27 shortlisted candidates past 14 days; screening fell 13 days and time to contract 27, with no baseline [S1]. Not a target. Fourteen days is a setting. A Venlo reach-truck file is 16 days in manager review, the manager is on leave, two others wait, one is internal. The artefact is three rows with days and the next action. The recruiter approves a nudge to the stand-in and a mail that states only the stage. The stage moves, or a reason is dated. The number is the count past the threshold, and this vacancy's days. GDPR covers the data; the mail is not a decision. A person-approved template needs no Article 50 label; a chatbot would, at the first turn [S9]. It does not evaluate people, so it is outside Annex III, and it is not an automated rejection. Tell the works council.

**Agency order and certificate match.** 157 hours × 72% × €65 = €7,300. A missed shift and a low scale are unmeasured, so excluded. North Star input: time to fill flex, and a certificate valid on day one. Tilburg nights orders six pickers for Monday: one VCA expired, one pay field empty. The artefact is the order and both failures. The recruiter approves the send and both refusals. The agency replaces the names, or the site accepts a short order. The number is filled versus ordered, and failures this week. Works-council consent before use if personnel data moves [S14]. GDPR: terms with the three agencies before a CV is forwarded. A person confirms the refusal: a review that must follow the result is not a review [S12]. From 2 December 2027 the filter is high-risk: an overseer, six-month logs, representatives informed [S10][S11]. Emotion inference is banned [S10]. A VCA date is not health data. Medical and drug files stay out. Pay fields are inlenersbeloning (model knowledge).

**Knockout shortlist, no score.** 429 hours × 76% × €65 = €21,200. The recommendation uses this pack and is not added. North Star input: time to hire beside 90-day retention, so faster refusals cannot win alone. Of US hiring leaders, 26 percent fear hidden candidates and 71 percent think most applications were model-written [S2]. Needs-a-look stays visible. Forty Venlo applications: a certificate photo, an English CV with no VCA, an employee with the certificate in Studytube and a thin CV. The artefact is three lists, each row naming the field. The recruiter approves before any rejection or referral, and the field is logged. The number is days to a manager-ready list, and the untouched pile. Works-council consent before use [S14]. A back-end ranker is generally outside the Article 50 chatbot duty [S9] and inside Annex III from 2 December 2027 [S10][S11]. GDPR: no rejection on a score alone [S12]. The Dutch authority says recruitment AI explains badly [S13]. US cases are not Dutch law. A contract is not the control [S7][S8].
