# Reflection

The hard part was one hire split into twelve tasks. A certificate check sits in matching, in tests and in the agency flow. A stage clock sits in status, strategy and criteria. If each task saves that hour, the role total is fiction. I left the hours on the task that owns the step and said so. The schema cannot say so. There is no workflow id, so a later roll-up will add the same clock again unless a person stops it.

I am unsure about the agency hours. The profile says 150 to 300 flex workers and portals that answer by mail and CSV. The task is labelled more than yearly and given 157 hours for both recruiters. I did not inflate it. If the truth is a daily order, the euro figure moves and the build order does not: the form is still second. I am also unsure whether Recruitee already sends stage mail. No Recruitee page was opened in the digest, so "configure" may mean switch on, or may mean it is already on. The 90-day field may be empty for flex. The works council may already have a view. Those four facts change the plan more than the wording of any step.

The probes were half right and a bad build order. Matching as a judgement step (0.73) hides the checklist. Strategy as a workflow (0.68) hides the meeting. I followed the shape on tests, references and agencies for the paperwork, and the human class for the decision. Gating the whole task would have zeroed the booking and the chase. The step schema can hold a gate on an action. The human_gate flag cannot. A stock status mail is approved once; a rejection is approved each time. One boolean has to cover the step, so I split steps the work itself does not split, just to give the flag a place to sit.

Evidence stops at E3, and several E3s are the vendor's own numbers. Uniper's 27 days has no baseline [S1]. I kept the number and refused it as a target. There is no grade for "opened, and it does not transfer". Inlenersbeloning and special-category health data are model knowledge, because the digest does not carry them. I had the jev probabilities in front of me. I treated them as something to disagree with. That is not the same as an estimate made without them.

Three changes before this runs on all 4,400 automate and amplify tasks.

First, put a workflow id on every step and sum hours on the workflow once. Without that, the business case becomes a headcount that nobody will see.

Second, let the company profile override O*NET frequency when they conflict, and mark the hours unconfirmed. A 2.89× ceiling on the wrong base is false precision. The customer's time correction belongs in the inputs, and the model should be told when it is missing.

Third, split the shape question into the share a closed checklist can finish and the residue, and tag the action: inform, reject, score, or request special-category data. The legal gate is then a dated table for those actions, reused across roles, not a paragraph rewritten on every task. Do not pass the probe probabilities into the prompt if the step times are meant to be an independent reading.
