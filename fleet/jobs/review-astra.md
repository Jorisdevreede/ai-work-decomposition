Before 4,400 tasks, make these changes separately:

- **Schema:** Add stable workflow/step identities, dependencies, separate current/future actors, shared-work ownership, new work and transferred effort. Replace Boolean gates with approver, scope and validity conditions. Enforce numerical and semantic constraints; repair the contradictory bookkeeper example.
- **Jev:** Ask which steps have rules and what evidence supports coverage. Obtain actual interfaces, volumes and authority from intake. Allow unknowns.
- **Prompt:** Separate reusable occupational knowledge from company assumptions. Require opportunity-to-step mappings, review/upkeep budgets and failure demonstrations. Use globally unique source IDs and dated legal guidance.
- **Merge:** Select coherent workflows after checking assumptions and dependencies. Neither majority voting nor choosing the simplest isolated label proves a workable design.
- **Roll-up:** Count shared work once; include maintenance, exceptions and other roles’ effort. Preserve potential and adoption ramp separately. Distinguish capacity, cash, elapsed time and productive retained hires.
