# Review brief: five models worked out the same job

Five models (Fable 5.1, Astra, Sol 6.1, Opus 5.5, Grok 4.7) each worked out the **Recruiter** at the
fictional Dutch company Noordhaven Logistics, following `fleet/jobs/JOB-BRIEF.md` (read it first).
Their answers are in `fleet/jobs/recruiter/<model>/`: `taskgraph.json` (all 12 tasks as a task graph,
schema `schema/taskgraph-v2.schema.json`), `job.md` (the job reimagined, opportunities, golden demos)
and `reflection.md` (what each found hard and would change). The input with the jev measures is
`fleet/jobs/recruiter/input.json`; the research is `research/digests/recruiter.md`.

The owner's bets, to judge against: deterministic first (artefact generators, workflow automation,
records apps, dashboards, integrations, stitched together, built 10–100× faster with AI coding),
small bounded model loops where truly needed, autonomous agents rare; potential shown with an
adoption ramp, not discounted; gates on actions, not whole tasks.

## Your review (at most 1,200 words, plain English)

1. **Ranking.** Rank the five answers for usefulness to a recruiter, a team lead and a builder.
   Give the two or three reasons that decide each place. Quote specific lines.
2. **Best ideas.** The ten best concrete ideas across all five (a step design, a loop, a gate, an
   opportunity, a golden demo, an ambition), each with the model that had it. These seed the merge.
3. **Errors.** Factual or logical errors, invented products or sources, inconsistent numbers
   (steps that do not add up, time saved that does not follow from the steps, ceilings computed
   wrongly), legal mistakes (EU AI Act, GDPR, works council), and places where an answer overfits
   agents or overclaims deterministic.
4. **The jev measures.** Where did the models agree with jev, where did they reject it, and who was
   right? Which jev questions earned their place, which should change?
5. **The method.** From the five reflections and your own reading: the most important changes to the
   method, the schema and the prompt before running on 4,400 tasks. Separate what to change in the
   schema, the jev questions, the prompt, the merge and the roll-up.
6. **Which model for the full run**, and whether a second model or a merge is worth its cost, for
   generation and for review.

Read all five folders fully before writing. Write your review as your final answer.
