AI work decomposition

Static prototypes that break a team's work into O*NET tasks and steps, and show what AI could change: per role, per team, and as a ranked portfolio of initiatives. The company in the examples, Noordhaven Logistics, is fictional. Hours and euros are gross estimates from generic task graphs.
