## S1. Ambient notes: about three hours a week less documentation

- Date: 24 September 2026 (page metadata).
- Source: https://www.chiefhealthcareexecutive.com/view/ardent-health-says-ambient-ai-saves-doctors-3-hours-a-week
- Who: Ron Southwick, Chief Healthcare Executive, interviewing Brad Hoyt, chief medical information officer, Ardent Health. The tool is Ambience Healthcare's ambient documentation. Rollout began September 2025 after a pilot, which is before this window. The interview is inside it.
- What: Hoyt says click and keystroke analytics show less documentation time and less after-hours "pajama time." The figure is the health system's own analytics, told to a trade journalist. There is no independent audit in the article.
- Number: about 3 hours a week less documentation, and a three-hour decrease in after-hours pajama time. The article presents these as the same order of result. It does not say whether they are two measurements or one.
- Grade: E3 company-reported, via a trade interview.
- Flag: self-report. Do not double-count three hours plus three hours unless the health system publishes them as separate. The article does not make that split clear.

## S2. Use in clinic visits, on the system's own count

- Date: 24 September 2026.
- Source: https://www.chiefhealthcareexecutive.com/view/ardent-health-says-ambient-ai-saves-doctors-3-hours-a-week
- Who: Brad Hoyt, Ardent Health.
- What: The rollout covers a multi-state hospital system. Hoyt says ambulatory clinicians use the tool in most encounters, and that this is more than twice as often as other systems. The comparator systems are not named. Visit volume is stated as a cumulative count since the rollout, not as a weekly rate inside this 90-day window.
- Number: 30 hospitals and more than 280 other sites, six states. More than 1 million patient visits. Ambulatory use in 87% of encounters.
- Grade: E3 company-reported.
- Flag: "more than twice as often as other systems" has no named comparator and no cited benchmark. The 87% is Ardent's.

## S5. "Twice as often as other systems" has no named comparator

- Date: 24 September 2026.
- Source: https://www.chiefhealthcareexecutive.com/view/ardent-health-says-ambient-ai-saves-doctors-3-hours-a-week
- Who: Brad Hoyt, as reported by Ron Southwick.
- What: The 87% encounter figure is paired with a claim that Ardent's clinicians use ambient documentation more than twice as often as other systems. The article does not name those systems, does not cite a published benchmark, and does not describe an independent audit. A clinician reading the piece can use the visit count and the hour figure as the health system's own analytics, and cannot check the "twice" claim from the page.
- Number: 87% of ambulatory encounters. "More than twice" is Hoyt's comparison. The other side of the comparison is not on the page.
- Grade: E3 as a reported claim. The comparison is unverified.
- Flag: do not repeat "twice as often" as a finding. Repeat it as a sentence with no denominator.
