## S5. A waterfall chart that did not tie

- Date: runs 11 to 26 September 2026. The opened article body did not show a publication date. A search listing said 6 October 2026; that date is not on the page.
- Source: https://gptforwork.com/blog/best-ai-model-for-excel-autosheetbench
- Who: GPT for Work, scoring its own Excel agent on 187 atomic tasks, one attempt each. The bench leaves out end-to-end financial models and dashboards.
- What: The failure that matters for an analyst: most models' waterfall ended at the wrong cash figure because ending cash was not treated as a total. Two models got it right. A second failure: one model built a formula summary instead of a pivot. Another missed a #DIV/0! because it looked at a partial preview. Two models dropped region values when sorting merged cells. Only two models grouped rows per category.
- Number: wrong waterfall ending €1.16M instead of €580K. Opus 5.5 Medium and Fable 5.1 Medium succeeded on that chart. GPT-5.4 built the formula summary. Opus 4.8 and Opus 5.5 failed a cross-sheet dependent trace (Opus 5.5 mistyped one reference). GPT-5.4 missed the #DIV/0!. Luna and GPT-5.4 lost region values. Only Opus 5.5 and Astra grouped rows. Overall: Opus 5.5 Medium 94.7% (missed 10 of 187). Astra Low 92.0% (15). Fable 5.1 Medium 92.0% (15). Opus 4.8 High 87.2% (24). Luna High 87.2% (24). GPT-5.4 Low 81.3% (35).
- Grade: E2 vendor benchmark.
- Flag: not a close. A 94.7% atomic score can still publish a cash figure that is about double. Durations on the page are roughly half a minute per task and are not a month-end saving.
