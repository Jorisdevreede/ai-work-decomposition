## S6. Sorting merged cells dropped the region, and a pivot came back as formulas

- Date: runs 11 to 26 September 2026. No article date on the opened body.
- Source: https://gptforwork.com/blog/best-ai-model-for-excel-autosheetbench
- Who: GPT for Work, on its own Excel agent. Relevant to a data-entry keyer or a clerk who is handed an agent instead of a workbook.
- What: On atomic tasks, two models lost region values when sorting merged cells. One model built a formula summary instead of a pivot. One missed a #DIV/0! because it used a partial preview. Only two models grouped rows per category. The bench says it is not an end-to-end workbook. One attempt per task.
- Number: 187 tasks. Luna and GPT-5.4 lost the region values. GPT-5.4 built the formula summary and missed the #DIV/0!. Only Opus 5.5 and Astra grouped rows. Opus 5.5 Medium missed 10 of 187 (94.7%). GPT-5.4 Low missed 35 (81.3%).
- Grade: E2 vendor benchmark.
- Flag: not a clerk deployment. A high score can still drop a column a mail-merge or a report depends on. The vendor sells the agent.
