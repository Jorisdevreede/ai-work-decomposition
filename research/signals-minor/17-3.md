## S1. Renumbering sheets was faster than a Dynamo script, and one sheet was missed

- Date: 2 October 2026.
- Source: https://interscale.com.au/blog/what-can-autodesk-assistant-do/
- Who: David Gerber, BIM/CAD technical consultant at Interscale, written up by Danoe Santoso. Revit 2027, retested after the tech-preview label was removed. Interscale sells BIM services.
- What: Sheet create, rename, and renumber completed quickly. One rename missed a sheet. A scrambled set was put into the A100 series in about five seconds. Gerber says that was faster than his own Dynamo script. A later bulk uppercase of sheet names also missed one sheet. Dynamo still won a straight bulk rename on the clock. Undo works and is labelled, for example "AI: Change Element Type."
- Number: about five seconds for the A100 renumber. Dynamo bulk rename 0.12 seconds versus about two seconds for the assistant. One sheet missed, twice (rename, and uppercase).
- Grade: E3 hands-on review, vendor-adjacent. Not a timed study on a live project.
- Flag: five seconds on a dummy file is not a drawing-office saving. The miss is the part a drafter has to check.

## S2. Finding walls was quick; placing one view was not faster than a hand

- Date: 2 October 2026.
- Source: https://interscale.com.au/blog/what-can-autodesk-assistant-do/
- Who: David Gerber, Interscale, on a five-wall dummy model in a cloud project with the firm's template.
- What: "Find 300 mm walls" returned IDs, levels, and lengths. Pasting those IDs selected the correct walls. Placing a view on a sheet centred it, and Gerber says he can place one view faster by hand. A wall-type change proposed five wall IDs. In the pictured test four updated and one was already the target 200 mm type.
- Number: find walls in 14 seconds. Place a view in about 11 seconds. Four of five wall IDs updated; the fifth was already 200 mm.
- Grade: E3 hands-on review.
- Flag: a five-wall file. Do not scale 14 seconds to a hospital model. The 30-model hospital limit is in S6.

## S3. Grids were explained and not drawn; door schedules multiplied

- Date: 2 October 2026.
- Source: https://interscale.com.au/blog/what-can-autodesk-assistant-do/
- Who: David Gerber, Interscale.
- What: Place grids: the assistant explained three methods and did not place them. A door schedule was created as a starting point. Follow-up prompts left three schedules. Wording mattered. "Add the field to this schedule" failed. "Create the same type of schedule and add the field" worked. A code-compliance question was passed to a Victorian building surveyor rather than answered as design advice.
- Number: three methods, no grids. Three door schedules left. No hour saving.
- Grade: E3 hands-on review.
- Flag: a drafter who accepts the first schedule will file two extras. The page shows that.
