# Digest 51-5: Printing Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 5 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 3 occupations (prepress technicians, press operators, binding and finishing workers).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Enfocus PitStop and Switch (Enfocus, Gent, Belgium), preflight and flow automation:** PitStop preflights and edits PDF files; Switch automates the job flow [S1]. Fits the preflight task and storing and retrieving files for equipment. E2.
- **GlobalVision inside Esko Automation Engine, automated proof checks:** compares text, artwork, spelling, braille and barcodes against the approved file [S2]. Fits the proofreading and quality-control task. E2.
- **MultiPress (Dataline, Loppem, Belgium), print MIS:** quotations, order forms, stock, cost price and accounting link. For a Bruges printer spending about 62 admin hours a month, the vendor projects 42 hours saved; this is a projection, not a measurement [S3]. Fits job records; the page does not name scheduling. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **WeGo Print (Brazil, large format), Enfocus PitStop, Switch and Griffin:** preparing one banner from order to print start went from about 2 hours to 20-30 minutes; no volume or period given [S1]. 2026; E3, vendor case.
- **Bemis (US packaging, 61 facilities), GlobalVision in Esko:** the barcode check alone saves about 10 minutes per job; Bemis estimates it would have caught eight errors the year before [S2]. 2017; E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Preflight (1 task):** configure existing software. Rule profiles check embedded fonts, image resolution, bleed and colour space. No model needed. E2.
- **Proofreading and quality control (1 amplify):** deterministic compare of the new file against the approved one: text difference, spelling list, barcode grade [S2]. A bounded model step can read meaning, such as whether a claim matches the customer brief; it only flags, and every flag goes to the proofreader. E1.
- **Entering data on computer-aided equipment, scheduling and job tracking (2 tasks):** integration plus a planning board. The MIS sends the job ticket to the press or RIP and the press reports status back (JDF/JMF is the usual standard); the schedule is rules on due date, machine and paper. Deterministic. E1.
- **Daily production records in finishing (1 task):** records app at the machine; counts come from machine counters where available, so the paper form vanishes. E1.
- **Agent:** none.
- **Glue:** paper job bags, Excel schedules and proofs sent back and forth by email. A job ticket in the MIS and a proof approval page replace them. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **Sector rules:** no print-specific rule applies to these record and check tasks. E1.
- **WOR article 27(l):** press and finishing data that logs output per operator or shift can check performance; works council consent first [S4].
- **AI Act Annex III:** no area applies, unless output records are used to evaluate individual operators, area 4(b) [S5].
- **Reported failures and risks:** manual visual comparison was slow and inconsistent at Bemis [S2]. Automated fixes change customer files; log every fix and show it on the proof. E1.
