# Digest 41-4: Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 29 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 3 occupations (technical and scientific products, solar sales and assessors, other wholesale and manufacturing).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Elfsquad (Drachten, Netherlands), configurator and CPQ:** configurator linked to ERP and CRM; AI helps build it, "a human checks the work, always" [S1][S2]. Quotes, bid specs, product choice. E2.
- **Workist (Berlin, Germany), order intake:** AI keys forwarded orders (items, prices, customer) into the ERP; staff check [S3]. Order forms, forwarding orders. E2.
- **Reonic (Augsburg, Germany), installer software:** AI agents plan a full energy system (solar, storage, heat pump, EV charging) from past projects; live in the UK and Germany since 22 June 2026 [S4]. Solar sizing, proposals. E2.
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales agents:** research and qualify leads, send outreach [S5]. Prospecting. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **EVG (Duisburg, electromechanical components distributor), Workist:** order entry fell from 4.5 minutes to 16 seconds per order; 68 working days saved in Q1 2025 [S3]. E3, vendor case.
- **ZARGES (Weilheim, light-metal products), Elfsquad:** a quote fell from about 15 minutes to 90 seconds; no dates given [S6]. E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Quotes, contracts, orders (6 tasks):** CPQ with product rules and price lists; orders by EDI or an ERP-linked portal. Bounded model step only for PDF or mail orders: extract, check lines against item master and price list; stop and route to a person when a check fails. E1.
- **Customer answers and delivery information (7 tasks: prices, stock, credit terms, warranties, delivery, technical support):** portal and answer desk reading stock, price, order and warranty records from the ERP. Checking delivery dates against project deadlines is a rule plus a notification. Questions outside the manuals go to a person. E1.
- **Calculation and selection (7 tasks: cost and savings, solar yield, product choice, energy packages, proposals):** calculators and the configurator, fed by site data and tariffs. Site assessment and final choice are judgement steps. E1.
- **Records and admin (3 tasks):** CRM, expense tool, dashboard. No model. E1.
- **Prospecting and market watch (6 tasks):** CRM dashboard and alerts; a model summarises documents and trade news with links. E1.
- **Glue:** PDF orders retyped into the ERP and Excel quotes link sales, order desk and supplier; portal, EDI and CPQ-to-ERP links replace them. Agent: none needed. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **Calling rules:** calls to bv and nv buyers are allowed; state name, company and purpose, announce recording [S7]. Since 1 July 2026 calls to consumers, zzp and vof need prior opt-in, existing customers included [S8]. Home solar selling falls here.
- **End of net metering:** salderen stops on 1 January 2027; until 2030 the feed-in fee must be at least 50% of the bare supply tariff [S9]. Savings calculators must use it.
- **AI Act:** B2B selling touches no Annex III area [S10]; an AI credit check for consumer solar financing would be 5(b), high-risk from 2 December 2027 [S11]. A customer-facing AI chat must say it is AI [S12].
- **WOR art. 27(l):** CRM activity tracking or call analytics that observe sellers need works council consent [S13].
- **Reported failures and risks:** Air Canada was held liable for its chatbot's wrong fare answer in February 2024 [S14]; an answer desk quoting prices or warranties carries the same exposure (E1).
