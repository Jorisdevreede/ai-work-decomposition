# 35-2 Cooks

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 8 automate/amplify tasks across 3 occupations (institution and cafeteria cooks, private household cooks, restaurant cooks). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

Cooking is hands-on; the scored tasks are its paperwork.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Apicbase (Apicbase NV)**: recipes linked to supplier prices; costs follow price changes; allergen and nutrition data per ingredient flow into every recipe and menu. For "determine meal prices" and "plan and price menu items". [S1] E2
- **Menutech**: detects the 14 EU allergens on a menu and prints the legend; a healthcare module hides dishes a resident may not eat. For menus with "diet restrictions". [S2] E2
- **Hygiënecode Online (Flexiwebs, Dutch-language app)**: HACCP registration that warns on missed measurements; modules for allergens and 24/7 temperature monitoring. For "keep records". [S3] E2
- **Orbisk** and **Winnow (London)**: each discard is weighed, photographed and identified automatically; Winnow also forecasts production per service. For "records of food use". [S4, S5] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Vermaat (contract caterer) at Triodos, 2025**: Orbisk units; 44% less food waste, 11,693 kg avoided; baseline and period not given. [S6] E3, vendor claim
- **Novotel Amsterdam City; LUMC Leiden**: 33% less waste and EUR 27k saved in under 12 months; 17,000 kg saved in a year, quoted from LUMC's head of nutrition. Vendor homepage, no baselines. [S4] E3, vendor claim

No case measures time saved on costing, menus or HACCP logs. Searched Apicbase, Nutritics and Dutch HACCP apps; only marketing figures appeared.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Prices and food cost**: plain code in a recipe records app: quantities, yields, a supplier price integration, a target food-cost percentage. Today a spreadsheet fed by e-mailed price lists. Rung: deterministic.
- **"Records of food use and expenditures", "records and accounts"**: integrations (invoices, stock counts, till sales, waste scale) into a dashboard of cost per cover. HACCP checklists with sensor feeds and a notification on a missing or out-of-range reading. Rung: configure.
- **Allergen information**: generated from ingredient specifications, never typed. A bounded model step may read a free-text spec sheet into allergen fields; a person confirms each new spec.
- **Menu planning ("nutritionally balanced")**: the model drafts a cycle menu from house recipes; code checks hard rules (no excluded allergen, nutrient targets, cost cap, no repeat within N days). Stop when the rules pass or after three failed drafts; the cook chooses.
- **"Estimate expected food consumption"**: forecast from bookings and history, par levels, suggested order. Deterministic; the cook adjusts.
- **Stays with a person**: tasting, seasonal choices, talking diet with the household employer.

## 4. Gates

- **HACCP**: Regulation 852/2004 art. 5 requires records "commensurate with the nature and size" of the business, kept current and shown to the authority [S7]. In the Netherlands an approved hygiene code can replace an own HACCP plan; the NVWA checks [S8].
- **Allergens**: Regulation 1169/2011 art. 44 makes allergen information mandatory for non-prepacked food; art. 8 keeps the operator responsible for accuracy [S9]. In 2023 the NVWA inspected 6,548 businesses: 51.8% of horeca fell short, and 30.7% of inspections found no allergen information [S10]. A model must never be the last source of an allergen claim.
- **GDPR**: diet restrictions of an employer, patients or residents can reveal health or religion, art. 9 data [S11].
- **EU AI Act**: no Annex III point covers costing, menus or food records [S12].
- **WOR art. 27(1)(l)**: a waste camera or HACCP app that logs who did what observes staff behaviour; works council consent [S13].
