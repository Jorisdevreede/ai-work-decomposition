# Digest 51-1: Supervisors of Production Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 4 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 1 occupation (first-line supervisors of production and operating workers).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Siemens Opcenter APS, detailed production planning:** schedules production ahead from orders, linked to the ERP [S1]. Fits reading work orders and schedules to set requirements, and the labour and equipment calculation. E2.
- **Evocon, production monitoring:** H+H (wall-building materials) replaced paper reports and spreadsheets with it in Poland and now runs it in 23 factories in four countries; the story gives no numbers [S2]. Fits the operations data and report task. E2.
- **Microsoft Copilot Chat, shift report:** Microsoft's scenario has a supervisor give brief notes and get a shift report draft; no measured result [S3]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **C. E. Schneckenflügel (Germany), Siemens Opcenter APS:** daily planning time fell by two hours a day and customer delivery time by 50% [S1]. 2022; E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Attendance and hours (1 task):** configure the existing time system (O*NET lists Kronos). Clock-in at a terminal, rules for shifts, breaks and overtime, export to payroll. No model. E1.
- **Production requirements, labour and equipment calculation (2 tasks):** planning board on standard formulas: order quantity times routing time, divided by capacity, gives machine hours and crew. Plan versus actual is a dashboard. Deterministic. A bounded model step only reads free-text order notes and flags them; it changes nothing. E1.
- **Operations data and management reports (1 task):** dashboard fed by machine counts and time bookings [S2], plus a report generator. A bounded model step may draft the commentary [S3]; the check is that every number in the text matches the table, and a mismatch stops the draft. E1.
- **Agent:** none.
- **Glue:** Excel planning sheets, paper shift reports re-typed at the end of the shift and mailed to management. Machine data, the time system and the planning tool feeding one dashboard replace them [S2]. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **WOR article 27:** consent for rules on working and rest times (b), staff personal data (k), and systems that can observe attendance or performance, such as line monitoring with operator login (l) [S4].
- **AI Act Annex III 4(b):** high risk if a model allocates tasks on behaviour or traits, or monitors and evaluates operators [S5]; obligations from 2 December 2027 [S6]; workers' representatives must be informed before use [S7]. Rule-based planning on machine capacity is outside it.
- **Reported failures and risks:** Schneckenflügel's earlier planning system was poorly adopted because of its user interface [S1].
