# 45-4 Forest, Conservation, and Logging Workers

O*NET minor group 45-4: 1 occupation (Logging Equipment Operators), 1 automate and amplify task (1 automate, 0 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

The task is US-flavoured: board feet and cords are US units. In Europe the same work is measured in solid cubic metres and stère.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Volume per log and per load: PONSSE Manager prints a load certificate with the volume of each load and compares harvester and forwarder production against the estimated volume of the work area [S1] E2.
- Conversion rules as configuration: StanForD 2010, the shared data standard of the Nordic machine makers, holds the price-volume rules per country (top or mid diameter, Norwegian and Estonian mid, the Estonian Nilsons' formula, the French NF B53.020 type) so the machine computes volume per log by rule [S2] E2.
- Volume of a roadside pile: Timbeter reads a smartphone photo of a timber pile, detects the log ends and calculates diameters and volume [S3] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- An independent study measured 71 stacks (3,481 m³ stacked) at the Forest Experimental Station of Poznań University of Life Sciences. Against manual measurement, photo apps gave larger volumes: LogStackPro +3.37%, iFovea Pro +8.07%, Timbeter +9.08%; the authors found the differences not statistically significant [S4] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- The task itself is a table lookup: rung **configure** where a harvester computer already does it [S1][S2], **deterministic** (plain code, one formula per agreed volume type) everywhere else. No model is needed for the arithmetic.
- The hard part is the agreement, not the sum. The Dutch guideline for stacked wood measures in stère and converts to solid m³ with a factor, but strongly prefers pricing in the unit that was measured; buyer and seller must also agree where the pile is measured, because wood settles on the truck [S5].
- A **bounded model step** fits only for photo measurement of piles [S3]. Check: a scheduled manual control measurement per site, since the error runs to several percent [S4].
- Today the glue is paper tally sheets and spreadsheets between harvester, forwarder, haulier and buyer (E1, model knowledge). Replace with an **integration** of the StanForD hpr files into the buyer's **records app** and a load **dashboard** [S1][S2].

## 4. Gates

- EUDR: wood in the rough and fuel wood are in scope; the due diligence information must state quantity, in net mass for goods placed on or leaving the market, otherwise mass, volume or number of items [S6]. So a volume to mass conversion must be recorded and repeatable. Application: 30 December 2026 for medium and large operators, 30 June 2027 for micro and small [S7].
- WOR art. 27(1)(l) and (k): harvester data registers operator work times individually and links stems to an operator key [S2]. Using it for performance review needs works council consent [S8] and is personal data under the AVG (E1, model knowledge).
- EU AI Act: pile counting is not in an Annex III area [S9]; no high-risk duty applies (E1, model knowledge).
