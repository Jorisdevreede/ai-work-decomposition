# 11-1 Top Executives

O*NET minor group 11-1: 4 occupations (chief executives, chief sustainability officers, general and operations managers, legislators), 43 automate and amplify tasks (9 automate, 34 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

The legislator tasks are US elected officials'; Dutch council members and griffies fit them only loosely (E1).

## 1. Products that do this today

- Board papers: Board Intelligence Report Writer drafts a structured paper from management documents and flags missing forward-looking analysis [S1] E2.
- Dashboards: Copilot in Power BI summarises reports, answers data questions and builds report pages [S2] E2.
- Budget and performance review: Pigment agents flag anomalies, build reports and build models from plain language [S3] E2; Board's FP&A Agent writes narratives on assumptions and risks [S4] E2.
- CSRD: Coolset (Amsterdam) runs the double materiality assessment, links evidence to each datapoint and suggests ESRS answers [S5] E2.
- Explaining rules: Struck at Gemeente Amsterdam answers Omgevingsplan questions with source references that staff check [S6] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- UK cross-government Copilot trial, 20,000 staff, late 2024: 26 minutes a day saved, self-reported; drafting 24 minutes, presentations 19; 17% saw no clear saving; "human oversight was required at all times" [S7] E3.
- Pigment: Supercell's P&L actuals and forecast update from 8 days to 4 minutes; Fivetran saves 12 hours a month on executive reporting. Vendor case, method not given [S3] E3. Supercell's gain looks like integration, not AI (E1).
- Board Intelligence: Apex Group got 60% of report time back. Vendor case [S1] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Recurring reports, performance, supplier and programme reviews (7 of 43):** dashboard on ledger, planning and operations data. A bounded model drafts variance commentary; each number it cites is checked against the data (E1). Unprepared data models give inaccurate answers [S2].
- **Budgets, control systems, schedules, prices, monitoring systems (6 of 43):** configure a planning tool, records app or scheduling module. Glue today: Excel budget templates mailed to managers and merged by hand; replace with a planning workflow (E1).
- **Drafting reports, speeches, proposals, grants, impact reports, bills (6 of 43):** artefact generator: figures from the system of record, model drafts, owner signs [S1]. CSRD data often comes by spreadsheet questionnaire (E1); a reporting tool keeps evidence per datapoint [S5].
- **Reading and review of staff reports, legislation, rules, research, constituent letters (8 of 43):** judgement step or answer desk citing sources [S6].
- **Strategy, direction, approvals, campaigns, risk assessments (16 of 43):** stays manual; a model may prepare inputs. No agent.

## 4. Gates

- **CSRD after Omnibus I:** Directive (EU) 2026/470 keeps only companies above 1,000 employees and EUR 450 million turnover; transposition by 19 March 2027; firms under 1,000 employees may refuse excessive value-chain requests [S8]. Simplified ESRS adopted 3 July 2026 [S9].
- **AI Act:** task allocation by traits, staff performance monitoring (Annex III 4(b)) and credit scoring of natural persons (5(b)) are high-risk [S10], from 2 December 2027 [S11]. Public-interest text needs an AI label unless a person reviews it and holds editorial responsibility (art. 50(4)) [S12], from 2 August 2026 [S11].
- **Dutch government:** generative AI allowed with risk analyses and reliable models, European or open-source preferred [S13].
- **GDPR and works council:** art. 22 limits solely automated decisions [S14]; WOR 27(1)(k) and (l) consent on scheduling and performance tools [S15].
- **Reported failure:** Deloitte agreed to repay A$439,000 after an Australian government report, later disclosed as GPT-4o-assisted, cited non-existent references and a fabricated court case [S16].
