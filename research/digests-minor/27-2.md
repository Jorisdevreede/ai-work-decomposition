# 27-2 Producers, programmers, coaches, referees and musicians

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 43 automate/amplify tasks across 9 occupations (producers and directors, programming directors, coaches and scouts, referees, composers, DJs). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Mediagenix Scheduling Automation (Mediagenix, Belgium)**: checks rights, repeat limits and content rules; builds playlists. For "plan and schedule programming". [S1] E2
- **Yamdu (Seriotec, Germany)**: call sheets, schedules, budgets, AI script breakdown (beta since October 2025). [S2, S3] E2
- **Wyscout Scouting Area (Hudl)**: scouting reports with video clips and data, used by FC Utrecht. [S4] E2
- **KNVB Wedstrijdzaken app**: digital match form; the referee enters the result. [S5] E2
- **BumaStemra music identification (DJ Monitor, SoundAware, Pioneer DJ)**: fingerprints festival and club audio into setlists; others upload Excel setlists. [S6, S7] E2
- **ScoreCloud**: audio to editable score with chords, MusicXML export. [S8] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Buma/Stemra, Dutch dance festivals, 2022 data**: DJ Monitor identifies 80-90% of tracks; 60-70% import automatically; the rest goes to an outsourced team in India. The vendor claims 93% setlist accuracy. Reported by Billboard. [S9] E3
- **BumaStemra, now**: own figure, at least 95% of works identified per fingerprinted event. [S6] E3
- **FIG and Fujitsu, World Championships Antwerp, 2023**: the Judging Support System was used only on inquiries or blocked scores. Fujitsu claims about 90% accuracy against a human; uses were not counted. One inquiry added 0.2 to a high-bar score, enough for silver. [S10] E3

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Configure**: DJ "itemized invoices" and "contracts for bookings" are templates filled from a booking record; "talent files" are a records app with availability.
- **Plain code**: "transpose music" is exact arithmetic in notation software. "Compile scores" and "verify scoring calculations" are sums and rule checks: recompute, flag mismatches. "Competition schedules" are a constraint problem for a solver.
- **Integrations and dashboards**: log checks against quota and rights rules, budget against spend, network "advisories" as notifications.
- **Bounded model loops**: setlists already do this: auto-import matches to the registered repertoire, queue the rest for a person [S9]. Script breakdowns, outlines and bid proposals: the model drafts, a producer confirms each element; stop when every scene is confirmed. Scouting: a model summarises tagged video, the scout signs.
- **Glue today**: Excel setlists [S7], call sheets by mail, scouting notes in spreadsheets (E1).
- **Stays with people**: composing, casting, game strategy, judging artistry.

## 4. Gates

- **AI Act Annex III 4(a)**: AI that filters or evaluates candidates, including for self-employment, is high-risk [S11]. Ranking players to sign or performers to audition fits (E1 reading); duties apply from 2 December 2027 [S12]. Scoring and scheduling are not listed.
- **Article 50(4)**, from 2 August 2026: disclose deep fakes (for artistic work, without hampering it) and AI text published to inform the public, unless a person reviewed it under editorial responsibility [S11].
- **Copyright**: text and data mining is allowed unless rightholders reserve it, online by machine-readable means (DSM art. 4(3); Auteurswet art. 15o) [S13, S14].
- **Mediawet**, the Dutch "FCC rules": commercial broadcasters give at least 50% of programming to European works, 10% independent, reported to the Commissariaat voor de Media every two years [S15].
- **GDPR and WOR**: injury notes in scouting files are health data (art. 9) [S16]; tracking a club's own players needs works council consent (WOR art. 27(1)(l)) [S17].
- **Reported risks**: JSS inquiries are not on score sheets [S10]; Buma/Stemra pays on registered song length, not the part played [S9].
