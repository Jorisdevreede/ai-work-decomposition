# Digest 41-1: Supervisors of Sales Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 12 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 2 occupations (retail and non-retail sales supervisors).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Quinyx (Quinyx AB), workforce management:** forecasts demand from order history, builds schedules within working-time and labour-law rules, lets staff swap shifts; Flink runs it across 160 warehouses [S1]. Schedules, time cards, duties. E2.
- **RELEX Solutions, forecasting and replenishment:** My Jewellery ('s-Hertogenbosch) is moving from manual planning to AI forecasting and replenishment across 65 stores [S2]. Demand estimates, purchase records. E2.
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales, research and opportunity agents:** answer questions on sales data in plain language; flag risks on open deals [S3]. Sales reports, performance follow-up. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Kendra Scott (US jewellery retailer, about 200 stores), Quinyx:** 8.5% saving on the total personnel budget in the first full year, 2023; schedules took about 15 minutes instead of hours; method not stated [S4]. E3, vendor case.
- **Luo, Qin, Fang and Qu, Journal of Marketing (2020):** field experiments at two fintech firms with 429, 100 and 451 sales agents. An AI coach helped middle-ranked agents most; bottom-ranked agents were overloaded, top-ranked ones resisted; AI feedback relayed by a manager beat either alone. No effect sizes on the page [S5]. E3, academic.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Records and reports (4 tasks):** POS, ERP and CRM data into scheduled reports and a dashboard. No model. E1.
- **Schedules and duties (2 tasks):** configured workforce software: forecast sets staffing, Arbeidstijdenwet and CAO rules are hard limits, the supervisor approves. Assigning duties is a judgement step. E1.
- **Demand and pricing (3 tasks):** statistical forecast with supervisor overrides; pricing policy as rules (margin floors, price ladders) plus a margin dashboard. Choosing the policy is a judgement step. E1.
- **Performance, territories and quotas (2 tasks):** CRM dashboard, quota maths as code; the supervisor judges. Route any AI coaching through the manager [S5]. E1.
- **Lease agreements (1 task):** template filled from a rental records app. E1.
- **Glue:** Excel rosters and mailed weekly report packs; workforce software and live dashboards replace them. Agent: none needed. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **WOR art. 27:** works council consent for schemes on working and rest times (b), staff data (k) and tools that observe performance (l) [S6].
- **AI Act Annex III 4(b):** AI that allocates tasks on behaviour or traits, or monitors and evaluates performance, is high-risk [S7], from 2 December 2027 [S8]. Deployers need competent human oversight, six months of logs, and must inform the works council and staff first [S9].
- **GDPR Art. 22:** quota, bonus or shift decisions made solely by a system need human review and a right to contest [S10].
- **Price announcements:** a stated discount must refer to the lowest price of the prior 30 days (EU Price Indication Directive; model knowledge, E1).
- **Reported failures and risks:** feedback overload for weaker sellers and AI aversion among top sellers [S5]. Microsoft says agent data may leave the user's region [S3].
