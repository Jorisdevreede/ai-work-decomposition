# 47-5 Oil and gas service unit operators

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 1 automate/amplify task in 1 occupation (service unit operators, oil and gas). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

The work on the well is hands-on. Only the billing paperwork is in scope: "prepare reports of services rendered, tools used, or time required, for billing purposes".

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Dynamics 365 Field Service, service reports (Microsoft)**: the technician builds a report of tasks done and parts used on the booking, captures a signature and delivers a PDF; the report opens offline. [S1] E2
- **Dynamics 365 Field Service, post work order and invoice (Microsoft)**: after review, posting a completed work order creates the invoice. Used products are billed as quantity times the price-list rate, services as "Duration to Bill" times the hourly rate; estimated lines are left out. [S2] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found. I opened vendor documentation only; the session's web search budget ran out before a search for field-ticket cases in well services.

## 3. Deterministic first

This task is a records app plus an invoice rule (deterministic). The field ticket captures crew hours from the time clock, equipment and consumables from pick lists, and the job reference. The rate sheet from the service contract turns that into invoice lines. The client's site representative signs on the tablet. That replaces the paper ticket, the spreadsheet and the e-mailed scan that someone re-keys into the ERP, which is where disputes over hours start today.

A bounded model step is optional: turn the operator's free-text job notes into a short work description for the client. Check: the step may not touch quantities or amounts, which come only from structured lines. Stop: the operator approves the text before the ticket is signed.

## 4. Gates

- **Tax records**: billing records are part of the administration and must be kept seven years (AWR article 52(4)) [S3].
- **WOR art. 27(1)(k, l)**: time and location data per crew member can monitor presence and performance; works council consent [S4].
- **GDPR**: crew names and hours are personal data; legal basis is the contract and the employer's records (E1).
- **EU AI Act**: no annex III point applies to billing paperwork (E1).
