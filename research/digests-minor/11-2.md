# Digest 11-2: Advertising, Marketing, Promotions, Public Relations, and Sales Managers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 59 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 5 occupations (advertising and promotions, marketing, sales, public relations, fundraising managers).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Spotler (Dutch site, +31 numbers), marketing automation:** email and SMS journeys, social publishing, lead management, predicted customer value, plain-language questions to campaign data [S1]. For "track response rates". E2.
- **Coosto (Eindhoven), listening and monitoring:** social listening with emotion analysis, trending-news alerts, one webcare inbox, dashboards [S2][S3]. For "observe and report on trends". E2.
- **Funnel, marketing data hub:** 600+ connectors, standardised channel data, automated reports, marketing mix modelling [S4]. For "analyze effectiveness of channels". E2.
- **Pipedrive (started in Estonia), CRM:** predicts deal win probability, suggests next actions, helps forecast revenue [S5][S6]. For "project sales". E2.
- **HubSpot agents:** campaign plans, branded content, nurture emails, prospecting outreach; vendor average "26% higher deal win rate", no method [S7]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Neleman (organic wine, Dutch shops), Spotler segmentation:** 18% fewer emails, 21% more revenue per email, conversion up 10-23% per segment; no period [S8]. E3, vendor case.
- **Kemkens (Dutch installer), Spotler linked by API to Business Central:** about 75,000 incoming messages a day, 300,000 follow-up actions without manual work [S9]. E3, vendor case.
- **DPD, Spotler emails with a one-hour delivery window:** 4% fewer kilometres driven; no base or period [S10]. E3, vendor case.
- **DHL eCommerce, Spotler Events replacing Excel:** 460 customers registered for one event; NPS +72 [S11]. E3, vendor case.
- Sales forecasting accuracy, PR monitoring, fundraising: none found (searched vendor customer pages).

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Budgets, results, forecasts (about 10 tasks):** integration plus dashboard, configure rung. Connectors pull spend and responses; the ledger gives budget against actual; forecast is stage value times stage probability. A bounded model writes weekly commentary; check: every number traced; it moves no budget.
- **Prices and discounts (2 tasks):** price list and discount matrix in CRM or ERP, approval above set limits.
- **Content (about 11 tasks: press releases, brochures, offer lists, grant files):** artefact generator filling brand templates from a product records app. A model drafts; check: claims match the records; a named person publishes.
- **Monitoring and requests (about 9 tasks):** keyword rules and alerts; an answer desk on approved facts.
- **Strategy, staff, agencies, events, crisis (about 27 tasks):** stay with people.
- **Glue:** Excel budget trackers, event lists, agency mail threads; a CRM, a project board and a ledger feed replace them.
- **Agent:** none needed (E1). Autonomous outreach collides with consent rules.

## 4. Gates

- **ePrivacy:** non-essential cookies need consent; marketing email needs prior consent, except own customers for similar products with an opt-out each time [S12]. Dutch Code E-mail 2025: active consent, opt-out within 7 days [S13].
- **GDPR art. 21:** an objection to direct marketing, profiling included, stops it [S14].
- **AI Act art. 50 (from 2 August 2026):** chatbots say they are AI; synthetic content marked; deepfakes disclosed [S15].
- **Reclamecode RSM art. 4e:** AI and virtual influencers may not mislead [S13].
- **Annex III 4(b):** AI scores that evaluate sales staff are high-risk from 2 December 2027 [S16][S17]. **WOR art. 27(1)(l):** works council consent [S18].
- **Failures:** after an update on 18 January 2024, DPD's service chatbot swore and called DPD the "worst delivery firm in the world"; DPD disabled the AI element [S19].
