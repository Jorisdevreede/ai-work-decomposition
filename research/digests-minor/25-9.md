# 25-9 Other Educational Instruction and Library Occupations

O*NET minor group 25-9: 5 occupations (instructional coordinators, three kinds of teaching assistant, farm and home management educators), 40 automate and amplify tasks (16 automate, 24 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source. Farm educator tasks (6) and state programmes are US-specific (E1).

## 1. Products that do this today

- Lesson materials: LessonUp rewrites slide text by tone or level, translates, makes word lists [S1] E2.
- Classroom activities: Snappet generates one from the lesson's learning objective [S2] E2.
- Course documentation: Easygenerator drafts a course from PowerPoint, PDF or Word; vendor claims "up to 9x faster" [S3] E2.
- Grading: Ans (Erasmus University) grades closed questions, splits open ones across markers, runs item analysis [S4] E2.
- Essay feedback: FeedbackFruits gives instant formative feedback on grammar, citations, word count [S5] E2.
- Records and support plans: ParnasSys (Deventer) holds pupil administration and an OPP module [S6] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- Oak National Academy's Aila, randomised trial in England (NFER for the EEF): 49 minutes less a week on lesson preparation (2.5 against 3.3 hours); quality "no worse" [S7] E3.
- FeedbackWriter, randomised trial at Michigan (354 students, 11 TAs): revisions improved, like moving from the 50th to the 70th percentile; TAs accepted 88% of the AI's rubric judgements. No time measured [S8] E3.
- CitoLab CheckMate pilot, Eindhoven: one teacher's open-question test for about 25 pupils took under 15 minutes, not 1.5 hours; he checks every answer [S9] E3, anecdote.
- No results yet: NOLAI pilots of Snappet Copilot in four schools [S10] and OPP drafting for special education [S11].

## 3. Deterministic first

- Grading and records (5 tasks): closed questions, recording and returning results are deterministic: answer keys, export to the records app, a notification [S4]. Open answers add a bounded model loop: the model proposes a rubric score, a grader confirms each [S8][S9].
- Materials (11 tasks): artefact generator; the teacher approves before class [S1][S7].
- Objectives, policies, grant proposals (3): drafted, a person signs (E1).
- Evaluation and needs analysis (7): a dashboard on records and results; a model clusters comments, group level only (E1).
- Research and recommendations (6): answer desk with citations (E1).
- Teaching, tutoring, advising, training (8): stay human; at most a disclosed AI tutor (E1). No agent needed.
- Glue today: grade lists in Excel, marked work by mail, OPPs in Word (E1). Replace with exam-to-records integration [S6].

## 4. Gates

- AI Act Annex III point 3: admission, evaluating learning outcomes (also when steering learning), assessing level and test proctoring are high-risk [S12], from 2 December 2027 [S13]. Kennisnet (2 April 2026) still says 2 August 2026; deployer schools need human oversight, six-month logs and a fundamental rights assessment [S14].
- Emotion recognition in education: banned since 2 February 2025 [S15].
- AVG: under 16, consent comes from the legal representative (UAVG art. 5) [S16]. Kennisnet: no personal data in AI tools, a processor agreement, a DPIA [S17]. SURF moved Microsoft 365 Copilot to amber in September 2025; two medium risks remain [S18].
- Grades: the exam board appoints the examiners who set results (WHW art. 7.12c) [S19]; AI only proposes (E1).
- Participation: schools fall under the WMS; staff data and monitoring tools need staff consent (art. 12(1)(m),(n)), pupil data the parent or pupil section's (art. 13, 14) [S20].
- Failure: Texas machine-scores open answers; rescoring requests rose from 2,758 (2024) to 64,489 (2026), and over 13,300 reading scores went up [S21].
