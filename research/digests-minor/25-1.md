# 25-1 Postsecondary teachers

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15, with XSCOUT signals. 364 automate/amplify tasks across 35 occupations (business, nursing, law, engineering teachers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **FeedbackFruits Automated Feedback (Amsterdam, NL)**: formative LMS feedback on writing criteria such as citation style; teachers edit the criteria. [S1, S2] E2
- **Ans (NL)**: auto-marks closed questions, splits open ones among markers, item statistics; no AI listed. [S3, S4] E2
- **D2L Brightspace Lumi Feedback**: drafts text and rubric feedback from instructor notes. [S5] E2
- **Gradescope (Turnitin)**: AI groups similar answers so each group is graded once. [S6] E2
- **Scopus AI (Elsevier)**: topic summaries, foundational papers; for "compile bibliographies". [S7] E2
- **EduGenAI (SURF, NL)**: chat platform (pilot); Utrecht forbids automated grading on it. [S8] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **University of Michigan, economics, 2026**: assistants edit FeedbackWriter's rubric suggestions. Randomised, 354 students, 1,366 essays: assistants agreed with 88% of AI judgments; revisions significantly better. University researchers; no time data. [S9, S10] E3
- **Four US political science classes, 2023-24**: 271 students, 3,080 short answers, 88% graded with GPT-4 help, randomly assigned against human grading. No significant grade difference; students found human feedback slightly more helpful (coefficient 2.13). Peer reviewed; no time data. [S11] E3
- **Penn State Harrisburg physics, 2019**: answer grouping "reduces grading time by over half" on 170 exams. One professor's estimate. [S6] E3, vendor claim

No Dutch case with numbers found.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **"Maintain attendance records, grades"**: records app plus sync. Radboud's Brightspace grades "must always also be published in OSIRIS" [S12]: double entry. No model.
- **"Compile, administer, and grade examinations"**: configure item banks, closed-question marking, item analysis. Open answers: a model groups answers, proposes rubric scores; check: the examiner confirms each grade, double-marks a sample; stop: high disagreement sends the batch to manual.
- **"Evaluate and grade ... papers"**: code checks length, citation format, DOIs; a model drafts rubric feedback the marker edits; stop when a rubric item lacks a quoted passage.
- **"Compile bibliographies"**: reference manager, catalogue; a model suggests titles; check: each resolves by DOI or catalogue, else dropped. Invented references pass plagiarism checks unflagged [S13].
- **Syllabi, grant proposals, recommendation letters**: generator from learning outcomes, timetable, publications; a model drafts; the teacher signs.
- **Mailbox glue**: fraud suspicions go by e-mail with screenshots to the examination board [S13].
- **Stays human**: lecturing, research, advising, fraud and grade decisions.

## 4. Gates

- **EU AI Act**: annex III point 3: admission, evaluating learning outcomes, setting education level, detecting cheating in tests [S14]. AI grading and proctoring duties apply from 2 December 2027 (Regulation 2026/1744) [S15]; drafting materials is not covered. Emotion inference in education: banned since February 2025 [S16]. Art. 4 AI literacy covers all staff [S17].
- **GDPR**: a grade set solely by a model is likely an art. 22 decision; systematic automated evaluation needs a DPIA (art. 35(3)(a)) [S18].
- **WHW**: examiners appointed by the examination board set results (7.12c); only the board sanctions fraud (7.12b) [S19]. Inspectorate: generative AI threatens test validity [S17].
- **Failures**: Leiden switched off Turnitin's AI detector in June 2023: its claimed <1% false-positive rate was unverifiable [S13]. A recent study Tufts cites: Pangram and GPTZero often flagged lightly AI-edited writing [S20]. VU Proctorio: no discrimination found, careless complaint handling [S21].
- **NWO**: referees may not use generative AI; uploading a proposal breaches confidentiality [S22].
- **WOR 27(1)(k, l)**: consent for systems able to monitor staff, e.g. per-examiner marking dashboards [S23].
