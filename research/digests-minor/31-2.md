# 31-2 Occupational and physical therapy assistants and aides

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 5 automate/amplify tasks across 4 occupations (occupational therapy assistants and aides, physical therapist assistants and aides). The work is hands-on; the automatable part is the paperwork: progress notes, scheduling, billing, supplies. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Fysio.AI in Intramed (NL)**: records the physiotherapy intake and writes a structured report into the intake form; audio is deleted after transcription; the page asks the therapist to check and correct the report [S1]. Version 10.6 brings it to the web version; a "Smartintake AI" for occupational therapy is announced, not yet live [S2]. Task: "document patient information, such as notes on their progress". E2
- **MijnZorgApp and declaratiesoftware (Intramed, NL)**: appointment confirmations and reminders to patients [S3]; claims to insurers always via VECOZO after a built-in check [S4]. Tasks: "schedule patient appointments", "documenting health insurance billings". E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found. I looked for Dutch physiotherapy and occupational therapy practices measuring time saved on notes or scheduling; the only figure is Intramed's "up to 20 minutes per patient", a vendor claim with no study behind it [S1].

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Scheduling** ("schedule patient appointments ... coordinate therapists' schedules"): online booking, reminders and a waiting list in the practice system. Configure; no model.
- **Billing** ("documenting health insurance billings"): claims generated from the treatment record and sent through VECOZO [S4]; rejections return as a worklist (E1). Deterministic.
- **Supplies** ("taking inventory, ordering supplies"): a stock list with reorder points and a standing order. Deterministic.
- **Phone and messages**: a booking link and call routing. A bounded model step could sort written messages into "reschedule", "admin" and "clinical question"; anything clinical goes to the therapist unanswered by the system (E1).
- **Progress notes**: ambient draft into the SOEP fields. Check: the therapist or assistant reviews every field; empty mandatory fields block saving. Stop: no sign-off, no save (E1).
- **Glue today** (E1): paper or Excel stock lists, phone notes passed by hand.
- **Stays with a person**: treatment, exercise coaching, transfers, the patient's questions about their care.

## 4. Gates

- **WGBO**: the therapist keeps a dossier with the health data needed for good care, for 20 years [S5]. Notes drafted by an assistant or a tool enter that dossier under the therapist's responsibility.
- **Wet BIG**: physiotherapists are BIG-registered; assistants and aides are not [S6].
- **GDPR art. 9**: recordings and notes are health data [S7]; a DPIA and a processor agreement with the scribe vendor (E1).
- **MDR**: once a tool proposes diagnoses or treatment, rule 11 makes it class IIa or higher [S8]; a plain transcript is not a decision aid (E1).
- **EU AI Act**: no annex III point applies; notes, scheduling and billing are not among the listed uses [S9]. A booking chatbot must tell patients it is AI (art. 50) [S10].
- **WOR art. 27**: only larger practices and rehabilitation centres have a works council; there, recording staff conversations needs its consent (E1).
