# 47-1 Construction trade supervisors

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 3 automate/amplify tasks across 2 occupations (first-line supervisors of construction trades, solar energy installation managers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

Supervisors run the site; the scored tasks are paperwork and planning.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Solar Monkey**: PV, battery and heat-pump design with yield estimates; branded online quotes from templates; a central product catalogue with price rules; business-case logic per national market. [S1, S2] E2
- **Reonic (Reonic GmbH, Germany)**: 3D roof layout to branded quote with variants and digital signing; project workflow and field reporting; AI chat and agents. [S3] E2
- **PlanRadar**: site diaries, checklists, handovers, defect reports. For "forms or reports". [S4] E2
- **Exact for Bouw (Exact)**: planning of staff, subcontractors and equipment from the budget; hours in the app; won quotes become a project or invoice. For estimates and schedules. [S5] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **FAGSI (German container-building firm)**: one project manager saves "about 10 hours of work per week" with PlanRadar. Self-reported, vendor-published, no baseline. [S6] E3, vendor claim

No European solar-quoting case with a measured result found; Solar Monkey's "24% more quotes approved on average" is a vendor average, context only [S1].

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Forms and reports**: the daily site report as a mobile form; personnel and hours from time registration, production from quantities ticked off against the plan. Generated, not written. Rung: configure. Today: paper day reports or Excel plus WhatsApp photos, retyped weekly (E1).
- **"Estimate materials, equipment, and personnel"**: plain code. Panel count and layout give the bill of materials (rails, clamps, cable, inverter sizing rules); crew-days follow from standard times per panel and roof type; scaffold or lift from roof height. Rung: deterministic. Today: calculation spreadsheets.
- **Proposals, budgets, schedules**: an artefact generator from estimate and price list; the schedule from crew availability and lead times. Payback must use the rules after net metering ends on 1 January 2027 [S7]: a parameter, not a judgement.
- **Bounded model steps**: (1) read a customer's roof photos and free text into intake fields; a person confirms before the estimate runs. (2) Draft the cover letter from the generated numbers. Check: every figure in the text equals a calculation field. Stop: the salesperson sends it.
- **Stays with a person**: the roof survey, safety calls, leading the crew, negotiating price.

## 4. Gates

- **Wkb**: since 1 January 2024 for consequence class 1, a quality assurer checks the work and the client submits a completion file; contractors stay liable for later defects [S8]. A KPCV task group reports this file often arrives late and too incomplete to enforce [S9].
- **Arbobesluit art. 2.28**: for works with special hazards or a notification duty, the client ensures a health and safety plan of hazards, measures and agreements, filled in as work progresses [S10].
- **NEN 1010**: building installations must meet it (Bbl art. 4.199) [S11]; a PV system is part of that installation (E1).
- **Net metering**: ends 1 January 2027; then a feed-in payment of at least 50% of the bare supply rate until 2030 [S7]. A quote assuming full netting overstates savings (E1).
- **WOR art. 27(1)(k) and (l)**: names, hours and output per worker are personnel data and performance observation; works council consent [S12].
- **EU AI Act**: Annex III 4(b) makes high-risk an AI system that allocates tasks "based on individual behaviour or personal traits" or evaluates worker performance [S13]. Rule-based crew planning is not that.
