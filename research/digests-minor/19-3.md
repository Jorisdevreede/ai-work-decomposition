# Digest 19-3: Social Scientists and Related Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 159 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 15 occupations (economists, survey researchers, psychologists, sociologists, planners, historians, political scientists).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **ASReview (Utrecht University, NL), literature screening:** open source; puts likely relevant records first; claims "reduce your workload by 95%" [S1]. E2.
- **ATLAS.ti (Lumivero) and MAXQDA (VERBI GmbH), qualitative coding:** ATLAS.ti drafts codes from a research goal via OpenAI GPT, "up to 90%" faster (vendor) [S2]; MAXQDA suggests codes and advises a human check of every AI-coded segment [S3]. E2.
- **Caplena (Zurich, CH), open survey answers:** themes and sentiment, "95% faster at human-level accuracy", method not given [S4]. E2.
- **ONS Survey Assist (UK):** Gemini asks follow-up questions and assigns industry codes; accuracy "not yet tested" [S5]. E2.
- **Dutch mental health care:** 24 of 25 large providers use speech-driven reporting (Medendo, ValueCare, Autoscriber) [S6]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **British Election Study, "most important issue" answers, 50 codes:** best LLM 93.9% accurate, a second human coder 94.7%; novel text 80.9% against 88.6% [S7]. E3, academic.
- **ASReview simulations, six review datasets:** 63.9% to 91.7% fewer records screened to find 95% of relevant ones [S8]. E3, academic.
- **CNWL NHS child mental health clinic, London, 10 clinicians:** median admin time per encounter 27 to 10 minutes with ambient voice notes [S9]. E3, academic abstract.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Writing, literature, grants (about 58 tasks):** artefact generator plus bounded loop: templates and a reference library hold structure and sources; a model drafts from the analyst's own tables; every citation must resolve to a DOI or archive record (E1).
- **Survey operations and coding (about 14):** response-rate dashboard in plain code; a model codes open answers to a fixed codebook, a person double-codes a sample, release above an agreed agreement rate (E1).
- **Analysis and forecasts (about 29):** versioned R, Python or SPSS scripts; NDW open data [S10] feeds PTV Visum [S11]; a model writes only text around fixed numbers (E1).
- **Planning, GIS, permits (about 25):** zoning rules and checklists are configuration; a model pre-reads environmental reports, a planner signs (E1).
- **Assessing people, client records (about 20):** test scoring against norm tables is plain code; speech-to-note is a bounded loop the psychologist corrects; selection, performance and eligibility stay judgement steps.
- **Teaching and advising (about 14):** manual. No agent needed anywhere.
- **Glue:** Excel, Access and Outlook move data between survey tool, analysis and report; a scripted pipeline on one store replaces them.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR Art. 89, UAVG Art. 24:** research on special-category data needs safeguards and, without consent, four joint conditions [S12][S13]. Session notes and test results are health data [S14].
- **NIP:** the psychologist stays accountable for AI-drafted notes and test reports [S15]; AI selection scores must be explainable, consistent, valid, non-discriminatory [S16].
- **AI Act:** selection is Annex III 4(a), staff assessment 4(b), special-needs eligibility may touch area 3; duties from 2 Dec 2027 [S17][S18][S19]; Art. 22 [S20]. WOR 27(1)(k),(l) consent for staff surveys and selection tools [S21]. AI text informing the public: disclose unless a human is editor [S22].
- **Research integrity:** EU guidelines advise against generative AI in peer review and evaluations [S23].
- **Reported failures:** Deloitte partly refunded an A$440,000 government report with fabricated references [S24]. Synthetic respondents varied less than real ones and drifted over three months [S25]; at least 4% of one platform's responses were bots [S26].
