# 19-2 Physical scientists

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 159 automate/amplify tasks across 12 occupations (chemists, environmental scientists, climate policy analysts, hydrologists, remote sensing scientists). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **SimaPro (SimaPro B.V., Netherlands)**: parameterised life cycle models; Report Maker refreshes Word and Excel tables when the model changes. For "identify environmental impacts caused by products". [S1] E2
- **Tanso (Germany)**: CSRD tool; maps the materiality assessment to ESRS datapoints, extracts figures from invoices, keeps an audit trail. [S2] E2
- **ASReview LAB (Utrecht University)**: open-source active learning that reorders abstracts while a person screens. For "locate and review research articles". [S3] E2
- **Scopus AI (Elsevier)**: abstract summaries citing up to 20 papers; Elsevier warns against relying on them alone. [S4] E2
- **Copernicus openEO (EU)**: rerunnable Python or R scripts over Sentinel datacubes, for "land cover maps". [S5] E2
- **Satelligence (Netherlands)**: deforestation alerts and EUDR evidence, data assured by EY. [S6] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **ECMWF, February 2025**: machine-learning forecast AIFS operational beside the physics model; gains of up to 20% on measures including tropical cyclone tracks, about 1,000 times less energy per forecast. Developer's claim, best case. [S7] E3
- **Ringeval and colleagues, ECIS 2026**: on a published review of 5,808 references, ASReview found all 28 included studies after screening 746 (92 minutes against 1,018 manual); Covidence found 5. Independent academics, one dataset. [S8] E3
- None found for LIMS, CSRD tools or permit review; vendor pages give no named baseline.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Records apps, integrations (deterministic)** for "build and maintain databases of information about … pollutants" and "compile and analyze test information": a LIMS with barcodes, instrument import, specification checks and certificates. An Irish ISO 17025 water lab replaced "paper and Excel-based processes" this way [S9].
- **Statutory reporting (integration)**: emissions and waste go to the government e-MJV form by 31 March (Omgevingsbesluit 10.42a) [S10]. Today spreadsheets glue meters and lab results to it.
- **Artefact generators (deterministic)** for "prepare charts or graphs from data samples" and "compile and format image data": scripted pipelines and report templates.
- **Deterministic plus a bounded model loop**: (a) screening: the model ranks, a person labels, a stop rule fixed beforehand is tested on known-relevant records. (b) briefs, grant applications, "technical portions of legal documents": drafts from a fixed evidence pack; check every reference and number against the pack; stop on any miss. (c) "review applications for site plans and permits": the Omgevingsloket Vergunningcheck decision tree stalls even on a dormer; Zeewolde is exploring a chatbot [S11]. Crisp rules stay rules, a model quotes the plan article, a person decides.
- **Stays manual**: fieldwork, accident investigation, peer review, defending proposals, teaching.

## 4. Gates

- **EU AI Act**: reports and analysis are not Annex III; point 2 covers AI as a safety component in water, gas, heating or electricity supply. Article 50(4): AI text informing the public, such as weather or climate briefs, is disclosed unless reviewed under a person's editorial responsibility. Article 4: AI literacy. [S12]
- **Pharma labs**: draft GMP Annex 22: generative AI and LLMs "should not be used in critical GMP applications" [S13].
- **CSRD**: Directive (EU) 2026/470 limits mandatory reporting to undertakings above EUR 450 million turnover and 1,000 employees [S14].
- **WOR**: audit trails naming staff are personal data; art. 27(1)(k) consent, and (l) if used to check performance [S15].
- **Risk**: in 84 generated literature reviews, 55% of GPT-3.5 and 18% of GPT-4 references were fabricated [S16].
