# 43-3 Financial clerks

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15, with XSCOUT signals. 85 automate/amplify tasks across 7 occupations (bookkeeping, payroll, billing and procurement clerks, collectors, tellers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **AFAS Profit (AFAS, Netherlands)**: Scan & herken reads PDF invoices and finds the supplier by KvK, VAT number and IBAN [S1]; bank lines auto-match on payment reference, IBAN or invoice number; misses go to a person [S2]. E2
- **Medius AP Automation (Medius, Sweden)**: line-level three-way match of invoice, order and receipt, for "compare suppliers' bills with bids and purchase orders" [S3]. E2
- **Nmbrs Payroll (Visma, Netherlands)**: a run check tests each salary change on 190 points [S4]; an output checker compares three periods per employee [S5]. E2
- **Onguard CreditManager (Onguard, Netherlands)**: dunning workflows (letters, reminders, call campaigns); Enexis runs it for over 150,000 debtors [S6]. E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Huuskes, Netherlands, 2023**: Medius on over 55,000 invoices a year, about 200 lines each. Nine months after go-live, automatically processed invoices rose from 40% to 65%. Vendor case [S3]. E3
- **Syncasso, Netherlands, 2026**: drafts a summons in two minutes; total handling time from 165 to 62 minutes; a legal professional reviews. Syncasso's own figure [S7]. E3

None found for payroll, timekeeping or bid comparison.

## 3. Deterministic first

Most tasks are arithmetic on records. "Interest charges", "compute wages and deductions", "prepare trial balances" and ledger transfers are configuration. "billing machines" and printout-to-journal comparisons go (eliminate). Integrations: CAMT.053 bank feed, UBL, payroll journal, wage tax return. Records apps: purchase orders with approval, price lists, leave balances. Dashboards: ageing, "monitor status of loans and accounts".

Glue: the mailbox. Of 594 Wolters Kluwer survey respondents, 70% approve invoices on paper or by email [S8]. Ramp vendor cases (US): OCR ended Precision Neuroscience's full day of retyping 20-30 weekly quote PDFs into orders; SAM Construction moved approvals out of the CEO's inbox: approvals 12.5 days faster on average [S9].

Bounded model steps:
- PDF invoices: extract; check lines sum to total and VAT, supplier known, order matched; any miss stops at a person.
- Remittance mails: propose a match; post only on an exact amount.
- "Inquiries about order status": draft from the record; every figure traced; disputes to a person.
- Payroll: explain each run-check warning; the clerk approves.
- Supplier quotes: read into a fixed bid table; scoring is code.

A person keeps: persuading debtors, payment plans, "service disconnection", lawyer hand-over, payment release, supplier interviews. No Noordhaven tellers or cage workers.

## 4. Gates

- **E-invoicing:** ViDA makes structured e-invoices the default and allows national mandates [S10]; Dutch B2B e-invoicing is planned from 1 July 2030 (draft bill) [S11].
- **BW 6:119a, 6:96(4):** interest from the due date, else 30 days after the invoice; €40 minimum costs without reminder [S12].
- **Wki:** since 1 October 2026 an unregistered third-party collector gets no collection costs or further interest; the debt stands [S13].
- **Wage tax:** over 10 staff file via commercial software and Digipoort; errors go in the next return [S14].
- **WOR 27(1)(k, l):** council consent for staff-data rules and attendance or performance checks, so time registration [S15].
- **GDPR art. 9:** the AP fined a company €725,000 for fingerprint clocking [S16]; time monitoring needs a DPIA (art. 35, E1).
- **AI Act:** Annex III 4(b) worker monitoring and 5(b) creditworthiness of natural persons [S17]; capture, matching and payroll checks are not listed. A supplier bot must disclose it is AI [S18]; art. 4 literacy applies (E1).
