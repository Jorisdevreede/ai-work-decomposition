# 53-1 Supervisors of transport and material moving workers

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 11 automate/amplify tasks across 5 occupations (aircraft cargo handling supervisors, recycling coordinators, supervisors of material movers, vehicle operators and passenger attendants). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **NetLine/Load (Lufthansa Systems, Germany)**: rule-based auto load planning with safety checks and alerts, so a load controller handles exceptions; vendor says up to 100 flights per 6-hour shift. For "calculate load weights for different aircraft compartments". [S1] E2
- **Evionica Weight & Balance (Poland)**: auto-load with hold limits and dangerous-goods separation; claims over 80% of final loadsheets need no person. [S2] E2
- **AMCS Route Optimisation (Ireland)**: master collection routes; RenoNorden uses it for Stockholm (50 vehicles, 25,000 bin lifts a day). For "coordinate recycling collection schedules". [S3] E2
- **Quinyx (Sweden)**: forecasting, schedule generation, time and attendance for logistics sites. For "records of time ... or crew activities". [S4] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **University of Belgrade researchers (one also at Ink Innovation), 2026**: a statistical rule set tested on 48 Airbus A350 load sheets found 81.25% of flights eligible for release without a load controller, 4.17% needing a notice, 14.58% needing mandatory review. Red cases came from cargo offloads, dangerous-goods changes and baggage or zero-fuel-weight swings. Measured by the authors on past flights, not live. [S5] E3
- Context only: AMCS says customers "typically" drive 5 to 15% fewer miles, a sales claim [S3]. No measured case found for waste paperwork or supervisor reports.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Load weights**: already deterministic. Configure exception rules; a person signs red cases. No language model.
- **Recycling logs, bills of lading, receipts**: a records app fed by the weighbridge generates the transport document, the receipt and the monthly waste report. Today a spreadsheet and a mailbox sit between weighbridge, carrier and customer (E1).
- **Time, wages, receipts, crew records**: time and attendance plus the WMS. Verification is plain rules: hours against roster and working-time limits, materials against orders.
- **Reviewing orders and shipping notices**: WMS wave planning. A bounded model step reads free-text or PDF notices into fields. Check: every line matches a purchase order line. Stop: unmatched lines go to a person.
- **Activity and accident reports**: numbers come from records; a model drafts the narrative from the supervisor's notes. Check: every figure in the draft equals its source field. Stop: supervisor signs; one redraft.
- **Grant applications, recycling plans**: drafting help; goals and submission stay human.

## 4. Gates

- **EU AI Act**: annex III point 4(b) makes high-risk any AI that allocates tasks based on individual behaviour or traits, or monitors and evaluates performance [S6]. AI crew scheduling or productivity scoring falls here, from 2 December 2027 [S7]. Point 2 does not cover aviation, warehousing or waste.
- **WOR article 27(1)(l)**: time registration, on-board computers and workflow systems are staff tracking systems; without works council consent they may in principle not be used [S8].
- **GDPR**: a tachograph is statutory and cannot be refused; any other GPS tracker needs a valid reason weighed against privacy [S9]. A DPIA is mandatory for GPS in employees' vehicles (AP list, item 11) [S10].
- **Waste**: receivers report monthly to the LMA through AMICE (waste stream number, kilograms, transports) and keep records five years [S11].
- **Freight documents**: from 9 July 2027 authorities must accept freight data from certified eFTI platforms [S12].
- **Aviation**: Air Ops rules require signed mass and balance documents per flight; ILT supervises (E1).
