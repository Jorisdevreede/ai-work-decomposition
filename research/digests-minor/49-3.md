# Digest 49-3: Vehicle and Mobile Equipment Mechanics, Installers, and Repairers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 2 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 2 occupations (aircraft mechanics, farm equipment mechanics).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **AMOS (Swiss AviationSoftware Ltd.), e-jobcards and e-signature:** job cards are created and completed in AMOS and signed through a PDF overlay, so the repair log stays digital. SunExpress went live in 2023 and is described as the first approved airline in Türkiye to go paperless [S1]. Fits the aircraft repair-log task. E2.
- **TrustFlight Tech Log, electronic technical log:** replaces the paper tech log on the aircraft [S2]. E2.
- **OutSmart (Visma, Nieuwegein), work order to invoice:** covers "job to invoice", with AFAS used for post-calculation and invoicing at one customer [S3]. Fits the farm-equipment billing task; no agricultural customer named. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Unnamed operator, TrustFlight Tech Log:** in a two-month parallel run, the paper process had 52 errors and the electronic log had 1 [S2]. 2020; E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Aircraft repair logs (1 task):** records app inside the maintenance system, configure existing software. Mandatory fields per task card, part and serial number checked against the fleet record, the signature tied to an authorised person. Plain validation does most of the work. A bounded model step could suggest a chapter code from a free-text defect, with the certifying technician confirming; any doubt leaves the field for manual entry. E1.
- **Repair bills from labour and parts (1 task):** deterministic code. Hours from the closed work order times the rate, parts at list price, VAT, pushed to accounting through an integration [S3]. The task largely vanishes when time and parts are booked on the work order. No model needed. E1.
- **Agent:** none. The release signature stays with a person. E1.
- **Glue:** paper job cards or tech logs re-typed at base, and Excel or Word bills. The digital job card and a work-order-to-invoice link replace both. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **EASA Part-145, point 145.A.55:** keep detailed maintenance records for 3 years from release, protected from damage, alteration and theft, with backups stored elsewhere (EASA amending text, 2016 draft) [S4]. Going paperless needs the authority's approval; SunExpress is described as approved [S1].
- **WOR article 27(l):** if log time stamps are used to watch technician speed, works council consent first [S5].
- **AI Act Annex III:** no area applies to these two tasks unless the logs feed performance evaluation of staff, area 4(b) [S6].
- **Reported failures and risks:** the paper log itself is the reported risk: 52 errors in two months in one parallel run [S2]. Aircraft work is rare in a logistics firm; the billing pattern is the transferable part.
