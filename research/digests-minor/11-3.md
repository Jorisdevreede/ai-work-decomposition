# Digest 11-3: Operations Specialties Managers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 201 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 19 occupations (largest: supply chain, HR, transport, reward). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Slim4 (Slimstock), inventory planning:** forecasts and sets stock per item and store [S1]. E2.
- **ORTEC Routing and Dispatch (ORTEC, Dutch):** route planning [S2][S3]. E2.
- **Transporeon Freight Audit (Trimble):** checks carrier invoices against agreed rates; "up to 70%" less manual work, vendor says [S4]. E2.
- **Nomentia (Finland), cash forecasting:** statistical models on bank and ERP data, forecast against actual [S5]. E2.
- **AFAS Profit 8 (AFAS, Dutch):** records job schemes for pay transparency; scores candidates against vacancies [S6]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Lasaulec, Dutch technical wholesaler, Slim4:** stock down over 20%, availability above 98%; period not stated [S1]. E3, vendor case.
- **Walmart, Pactum chatbot:** deals with 68% of equipment suppliers approached, 3% average savings [S7]. E3, vendor figures, 2023.
- **UK university, job evaluation:** a ChatGPT tool graded 50 job descriptions, above 70% agreement with experts [S8]. E3, self-reported pilot, 2025.
- **Denmark, 25,000 workers, 11 occupations incl. HR, 2023-2024:** chatbots saved 2.8% of hours; earnings and hours unchanged [S9]. E3, academic.

## 3. Deterministic first

E1 throughout. Of 201 tasks, 18 are 80%+ rule-covered, 109 have structured input, 195 hinge on judgement.

- **Logistics planning (about 26):** configure TMS and inventory tools for routes and stock; cost-to-serve scenarios in plain code; the manager decides.
- **Purchasing and freight cost (about 20):** receipts, PO policy and tariff checks: ERP and freight-audit rules; a bounded loop reads contracts into fields, checks them against the PO, stops at a mismatch.
- **Records, quality, compliance (about 46):** records app plus dashboard; a model drafts nonconformance reports citing record IDs; a person signs.
- **Finance and treasury (about 50):** payroll deductions and disbursements: configuration; cash: a dashboard on bank files; a model drafts commentary on queried figures; investment-fund tasks barely apply.
- **HR and reward (about 42):** statistics dashboard; grading records app where a model proposes and an evaluator decides; CAO answer desk.
- **Security and IT (about 17):** ticket and incident-log configuration; judgement stays manual.
- **Glue:** mailed rate sheets, carrier invoices and Excel budgets become a rate table, integrations and dashboards. No task needs an agent.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR art. 22:** no significant decision on staff solely by automation [S10].
- **AI Act Annex III:** match scores, applicant tests: 4(a); allocation, performance scoring: 4(b) [S11]; duties from 2 December 2027 [S12]; tell workers' representatives first [S13].
- **WOR art. 27:** consent for pay or job-evaluation systems (1c), hiring and promotion (1e), appraisal (1g), staff data (1k), monitoring (1l), unless a CAO settles it [S14].
- **Pay Transparency Directive 2023/970:** The transposition deadline of 7 June 2026 has passed. Bill 36949 aims at 1 January 2027, but a plenary debate is listed for 11 January 2027 [S15]; a law firm calls that date unreachable on this planning [S16]. Duties: pay range before interview, no pay-history question, gender-neutral job evaluation, gap reports from 150 staff over 2027, joint assessment if an unexplained gap above 5% lasts six months [S17].
- **CSRD/CS3D:** now from 1,000 staff and €450m (CSRD), 5,000 and €1.5bn (CS3D) [S18]; carbon tasks matter via customers (E1).
- **Reported failures and risks:** IBM's HR NPS fell to −35 in AskHR's first year, when managers lost HR partners and the phone line [S19]. Age claims over Workday's screening AI may proceed collectively; employers keep their duties [S20].
