# 13-1 Business Operations Specialists

O*NET minor group 13-1: 30 occupations (e.g. buyers, claims adjusters, customs brokers, HR specialists, logisticians, fundraisers), 370 automate and amplify tasks (145 automate, 225 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

Some tasks are US public-sector (licensing, EEO, EPA, coroners); read them as EU counterparts.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Route planning: ORTEC (Dutch, founded 1981) routes over 300,000 Albert Heijn orders a week [S1][S2] E2.
- Freight invoice audit: Transporeon checks carrier invoices against agreed rates [S3] E2.
- Purchasing: Pactum agents check requisitions against policy inside Coupa and negotiate [S4][S5] E2.
- Customs: EU TARIC holds duty rates and measures; EBTI is a searchable register of binding tariff decisions [S6][S7] E2.
- Applicant matching: AFAS Profit 8 scores applications per vacancy [S8]; Textkernel parses CVs and matches [S9] E2.
- Claims: Guidewire ClaimCenter guided intake [S10]; Shift Technology fraud screening from first notice [S11]; Tractable damage assessment from photos [S12] E2.
- Product copy: Shopify Sidekick writes product descriptions [S13] E2.
- Regulatory submissions: Veeva RIM tracks registrations and publishes submissions [S14] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- SUEZ UK, Pactum on Coupa: requisition cycle time down 90%, 80% of manual reviews gone (vendor case) [S5] E3.
- US travel insurer, about 400,000 claims a year, Shift: automation 0% to 57% (46% pay, 11% deny), 98% accuracy on pay decisions (vendor case) [S15] E3.
- AXA Switzerland: over 1 million claims screened, over €12M fraud stopped (vendor case) [S11] E3.
- DNB survey: nearly 80% of large and medium Dutch insurers used AI in regular processes in early 2025, small ones 21% [S16] E3.

## 3. Deterministic first

- 86 of 370 tasks (23%) are reports, records and monitoring: on-time rates, inventory turns, budgets, donor and claim files. **Deterministic**: **records app** and **dashboard** on ERP, TMS or HR data (E1).
- Duty, freight, tax and cost calculations: **configure** on TARIC and contracted rates [S3][S6].
- Classification: search EBTI first [S7]; a **judgement step** suggests a code, the broker signs.
- 40 tasks (11%) draft text (SOPs, letters, product copy, grant bids, RFQs); others read claims files, CVs and contracts. **Deterministic plus a bounded model loop**: work from approved sources, a named person signs.
- **Agent**: only requisition-to-order inside spend limits [S4]. **Stays manual**: bargaining, discrimination complaints, donor asks.
- Glue today: spreadsheets for freight rates, suppliers and costs; shared mailboxes for RFQs, carriers and candidates (E1). Replace with **integrations** and status **notifications**.

## 4. Gates

- AI Act: CV screening and worker evaluation are Annex III 4(a)(b); life and health insurance pricing is 5(c) [S17]; duties from 2 December 2027 [S18]. Emotion recognition at work is banned since 2 February 2025 [S19].
- AVG art. 22: solely automated decisions with significant effect need contract, law or explicit consent, and human intervention [S20]. A determining score counts (CJEU SCHUFA) [S21], so automatic claim denials need this test. A DPIA is mandatory for employee monitoring, fraud prevention and credit scoring [S22].
- WOR art. 27(1)(k)(l): works council consent for HR data and monitoring [S23].
- Pay transparency: gender-neutral job evaluation for employers; the Dutch bill targets 1 January 2027, likely late [S24].
- Insurers: the Verbond's 28-norm data ethics framework binds members since 2021 [S25]; DNB found incomplete documentation and weak monitoring [S16].
- CSRD after Omnibus I: only firms over 1,000 staff and €450M turnover [S26].
- Failure: Deloitte partly refunded an A$439,000 Australian government report that used GPT-4o and cited non-existent sources [S27].
