# 25-3 Adult education, ESL and self-enrichment teachers, substitutes and tutors

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 28 automate/amplify tasks across 4 occupations (adult basic, secondary and ESL instructors; self-enrichment teachers; short-term substitutes; tutors). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Momoyoga (NL)**: class schedule, online booking and payment, memberships, attendance reports, messaging; no AI named [S1]. For "schedule tutoring appointments", "maintain student records". E2
- **LessonUp (NL)**: lesson-editor AI rewrites text at another difficulty, summarises, translates and pulls word lists with definitions [S2]. For "develop … handouts, study materials". E2
- **Easygenerator (NL)**: EasyAI turns documents or slide decks into a first course draft; localisation in 75 languages; data centre in Frankfurt [S3]. For "write instructional articles", "prepare … outlines". E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Tutor CoPilot, about 900 tutors and 1,800 K-12 pupils**: live AI suggestions to tutors raised topic mastery by 4 percentage points, 9 for lower-rated tutors, at about $20 per tutor a year. Preregistered randomised trial, measured by the authors [S4]. E3
- **Edo State, Nigeria, 2024**: six weeks of after-school English with Microsoft Copilot as tutor and teachers guiding; gains of about 0.3 standard deviations, randomised. Measured by World Bank staff who ran the programme [S5]. E3

## 3. Deterministic first

- **"Schedule tutoring appointments"**: configure a booking tool with self-service slots, reminders, payment.
- **"Maintain records … ensuring confidentiality"**, **"prepare reports on students"**: records app with role-based access; attendance and progress reports come from the register. Deterministic.
- **"Develop … handouts, study materials, or quizzes"**, **"prepare lesson plans … according to students' needs"**, **"write instructional articles"**: a model drafts. Check: quiz answers match a key, text stays within the learner's CEFR word list, the teacher reads every item. Stop: approved, or after two redrafts the teacher writes it.
- **"Assign and grade class work"**: closed items marked against a key; for ESL writing a model suggests rubric feedback and the teacher sets the grade.
- **"Research or recommend textbooks"**, exam guidance: answer desk over a curated catalogue and official exam pages.
- **"Review … student evaluations"**: dashboard plus a model clustering comments; check: each cluster lists its source comments; a person decides.
- **Live tutoring**: a suggestion step like Tutor CoPilot; the tutor chooses.
- **Glue** (E1): bookings over WhatsApp and spreadsheets, progress notes in documents, invoices by hand.
- **Stays with the person**: teaching, reviewing work with students, study skills, adapting methods, recruitment.

## 4. Gates

- **AI Act annex III point 3**: AI that admits learners, evaluates learning outcomes or monitors tests in "educational and vocational training institutions at all levels" is high-risk [S6], from 2 December 2027 [S7]. Adult education and language schools fit; a freelance tutor's app is unclear (E1). An AI tutor must say it is AI from 2 August 2026 (art. 50) [S8].
- **Inburgering**: civic integration courses need a provider with a certificate or quality mark (Wet inburgering 2021, art. 16(3), 32) [S9].
- **GDPR**: tutors hold minors' grades and support needs; contract basis, parents informed, data minimised (E1).
- **WOR art. 27(1)(k, l)**: a tutoring firm with a works council needs consent for staff-data rules and for tools that can monitor tutors, such as session logs [S10].
- **Reported risk**: tutors flagged suggestions that were not grade-appropriate [S4].
