# Digest 41-3: Sales Representatives, Services

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 52 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 5 occupations (advertising, insurance, securities and financial services, travel, other services).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales, qualification agent:** researches leads, judges fit, sends outreach emails [S1]. Prospecting. E2.
- **Salesforce Agentforce for Revenue, quoting:** builds a quote from one typed sentence [S2]. Quotes, estimates. E2.
- **Teamleader (Ghent, Belgium):** CRM, quotes signed online; its Focus AI acts only after approval [S3]. Records, agreements. E2.
- **ANVA (Dutch insurance software), SIVI AFD standard:** SIVI-certified product definitions give insurers and agents one data format [S4]. Policy records, renewals. E2.
- **Perk (London HQ):** books flights, hotels, trains and cars under travel rules set once [S5]. Booking, ticketing. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Salesforce, own sales team:** 75% less quoting time, 87% fewer clicks; no base or period given [S2]. E3, vendor case.
- **Luo, Tong, Fang and Qu, Marketing Science (2019):** outbound sales calls to more than 6,200 customers. Undisclosed chatbots matched proficient staff and sold four times more than novices; disclosing the bot up front cut purchases by 79.7% [S6]. E3, academic.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Quotes and cost sums (7 tasks: ad estimates, travel costs, service quotes, RFQs, bid packs):** CPQ or a rate-card app with prices, discounts and approval limits as rules. A bounded model step drafts a quote from a mailed request; check: lines match the price book; stop: missing fields go back to the seller. E1.
- **Records, orders, bookings, reports (14 tasks):** records app with integrations (SIVI policy data, broker tickets, travel platform); commissions, position reports and transaction checks as code and dashboards. No model. E1.
- **Answering and explaining (9 tasks):** answer desk over approved documents, with citations; hand off when a question becomes advice. E1.
- **Documents (6 tasks: media kits, sample ads, proposals, agreements):** CRM-filled templates; a model drafts, the seller signs off. E1.
- **Prospecting and market watch (9 tasks):** CRM and market-feed dashboards with alerts; a model summarises news with links. E1.
- **Advice and judgement (7 tasks, e.g. insurance programmes, financial plans, securities advice):** stays with the person. E1.
- **Glue:** Excel rate sheets and Outlook threads carry deals to operations; CPQ-to-ERP links and CRM mail logging replace them. Agent: none needed. E1.

## 4. Gates

- **Telemarketing:** since 1 July 2026 calls to consumers, zzp and vof need prior opt-in, existing customers included [S7]. Calls to bv and nv are allowed: state name, company and purpose, announce recording [S8].
- **AI Act Art. 50:** from 2 August 2026 an AI caller or chat must say it is AI unless obvious [S9]; see Luo for the sales cost.
- **Wft/IDD:** advising staff need a Wft diploma per product and PE every three years (art. 4:9 Wft) [S10]; a model drafts, the adviser signs.
- **MiFID II:** record order-related calls and messages, even without a trade; keep five years, up to seven [S11].
- **AI Act Annex III:** 5(c) life and health insurance pricing and 5(b) consumer creditworthiness are high-risk [S12] from 2 December 2027 [S13]; no other task touches Annex III. Automated consumer credit terms also meet GDPR Art. 22 [S14].
- **WOR art. 27(k), (l):** call recording and scoring of sellers need works council consent [S15].
- **Reported failures and risks:** Air Canada was held liable for its chatbot's wrong fare answer (CAD 812.02, 14 February 2024) [S16].
