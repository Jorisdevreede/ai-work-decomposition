# Digest 19-1: Life Scientists

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 97 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 16 occupations (bioinformaticians, geneticists, epidemiologists, biologists, conservation, food and soil scientists, foresters).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Benchling, lab notebook AI:** drafts notebook entries and study reports, turns PDFs into structured data, checks entries, writes SQL [S1]. E2.
- **SciSure (eLabNext's successor), ELN and LIMS:** sample tracking, inventory, workflows; no AI named [S2]. E2.
- **Castor, Catalyst:** AI turns source documents and health records into study entries; a human confirms each; vendor says reviewers changed 0.8% of values [S3]. E2.
- **Elicit, evidence review:** screening and data extraction; "up to 80% time savings", self-reported [S4]. E2.
- **nf-core:** community Nextflow bioinformatics pipelines with tagged releases, containers and tests [S5]. E2, deterministic.
- **EIOS (WHO, EU Joint Research Centre):** open-source epidemic intelligence, used in 120 countries by end 2025 [S6]. E2.
- **Copernicus Land Monitoring Service (EU):** free land cover, land use and water data [S7]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **otto-SR, 12 Cochrane reviews, 146,276 citations:** redone in about two days; screening sensitivity 96.7% against 81.7% for dual human review; extraction 93.1% against 79.7% [S8]. E3, academic (preprint abstract).
- **Claude 2 data extraction, 10 trials, 160 data elements:** 96.3% accurate; 6 errors, 4 of them missed items [S9]. E3, academic.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Papers, reports, grants (about 22 tasks):** artefact generator plus bounded loop: funder templates, a reference library, script-made figures; a model drafts from the lab's results; every citation must resolve to a DOI (E1).
- **Literature and staying current (about 6):** saved searches and alerts are configuration; screening and extraction are a bounded loop, a second human checks a sample [S8][S9].
- **Analysis, genomic databases, tools (about 20):** deterministic code: versioned pipelines, SQL on genomic databases, scripted summary statistics. A model may write code, checked by tests on known datasets (E1). Genetic result sign-off stays judgement.
- **Standards, plans, compliance reviews (about 15):** records app plus rules: annual reports checked against a required-field list; a model pre-reads free text against the checklist, a scientist decides (E1).
- **Study design and field research (about 13):** manual, with structured capture in forms or an EDC.
- **Advice, teaching, public questions (about 20):** answer desk on approved pages for routine questions (wetland rules, nuisance wildlife) with hand-off; advising farmers and diagnosing patients stay manual (E1).
- **Glue:** Excel, Access and mail move results between lab, analyst and funder; an ELN or LIMS with integrations replaces them. No agent needed.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR Art. 9 and 89, UAVG Art. 24:** genetic and health data are special categories; public-health use needs a basis in law; research use needs safeguards and, without consent, four joint conditions [S10][S11][S12].
- **IVDR (EU 2017/746):** software informing on genetic predisposition or congenital impairments can be an in vitro diagnostic device [S13]; AI in it falls under product-linked high-risk rules from 2 Aug 2028 [S14] (E1). No Annex III area applies elsewhere.
- **WOR 27(1)(l):** ELN and LIMS audit trails log who did what, so they may count as a facility able to observe performance (E1) [S15].
- **Grants and research integrity:** NWO bars generative AI for its assessors and hidden prompts in applications [S16]; EU guidelines advise against generative AI in peer review and evaluations [S17].
- **Reported failures:** GPT-3.5 fabricated 55% and GPT-4 18% of 636 generated citations [S18]. Frontier models reached 17% accuracy on open-answer bioinformatics analyses in BixBench [S19].
