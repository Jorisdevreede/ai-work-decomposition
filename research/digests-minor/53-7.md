# Digest 53-7: Material Moving Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 1 automate/amplify O*NET task in 1 occupation (stockers and order fillers): keep records of outgoing orders.
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Boltrics 3PL Dynamics (Netherlands), warehouse management:** WMS for logistics service providers, with transport, forwarding and customs modules, built on Microsoft technology [S1]. E2. A WMS of this kind keeps the outbound order record as part of picking and shipping (E1).

## 2. Cases with numbers

- No case measures outbound record keeping itself. The nearest measured case found is at the dock: Smurfit Kappa's Hoya plant cut truck turnaround from 120 to 52 minutes with Transporeon time slots; undated [S2]. E3, vendor case.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Keep records of outgoing orders (1 task):** eliminate. A scan at pick, pack and load writes the record in the WMS; the shipping document and eCMR come from the same record. Short picks and damage are a coded reason on the scanner. No model, no agent (E1).
- **Glue:** paper pick lists and Excel tallies end once scanning is in place.

## 4. Gates

- **WOR art. 27 (k, l):** scanners that log each pick per person can observe performance, so the works council must consent before use [S3].
- **GDPR:** France's CNIL fined Amazon France Logistique €32 million (December 2023) for scanner indicators of exact idle time and of items scanned within 1.25 seconds of the previous one, and for keeping all indicators 31 days [S4]. Keep the order record; do not turn it into a per-person score without a purpose and consent.
- **EU AI Act:** record keeping touches no Annex III area. Using pick data to evaluate staff or allocate tasks by behaviour is 4(b) [S5], with duties from 2 December 2027 [S6].
