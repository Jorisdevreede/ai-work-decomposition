# 17-3 Drafters, Engineering Technicians, and Mapping Technicians

O*NET minor group 17-3: 14 occupations (e.g. drafters, engineering technicians, NDT specialists, surveying and mapping technicians), 56 automate and amplify tasks (20 automate, 36 amplify). DIGEST-B, 2026-10-15. Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

The list is the paperwork around drafting and survey computing, not drawing itself.

## 1. Products that do this today

- Check models against codes: Solibri Advanced runs custom regulatory rules, clash detection and IDS validation on IFC models [S1] E2.
- Bills of materials: Eplan Electric P8 generates reports, wire lists and BOMs [S2] E2; Eplan Copilot answers from verified documentation [S3] E2.
- Quantities and plan sheets: Bentley OpenRoads Designer updates plans, profiles and cross-sections from the model [S4] E2; OpenSite+ evaluates thousands of grading scenarios within constraints [S5] E2.
- Survey computations: Trimble Business Center does least squares adjustment with reports [S6] E2; RDNAPTRANS2018, the official RD/NAP to ETRS89 transformation, is free for developers [S7] E2.
- Data for maps: PDOK serves 213 open government datasets via OGC APIs [S8]; government bodies must use the BGT [S9] E2.
- Aerial photo interpretation: ArcGIS AI models extract features from imagery [S10] E2; Readar matches BAG and BGT to its aerial imagery, checking every hit by hand [S11] E2; Rijswijk's Changemapp has staff review every signal [S12] E2.
- Test result documentation: DewesoftX records test data and produces PDF or Word reports [S13] E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- Harness design, vendor case: MTA (Eplan Harness proD): 25% shorter lead time, half as many production changes; no method; design software, not AI [S14] E3.
- Map upkeep, vendor analysis: NEO used AI on aerial photos and found over 900,000 building changes missing from BAG and BGT, worth up to €36 million a year in property tax [S15] E3.
- AI drafting with a measured result: none found.

## 3. Deterministic first

- Survey and map computing (8 of 56 tasks): deterministic, on PDOK and BGT data; aerial change detection is a bounded model loop with review [S12].
- Quantities, BOMs, costs (11, 20%): deterministic from model and price tables. An integration from takeoff to estimating replaces the Excel sheet mailed to the estimator. A bounded model step reads supplier quotes, checked against totals (E1).
- Code and spec checks (8): rule-based first [S1]; a bounded model loop proposes rules from code text or permits; an engineer signs (E1).
- Reports and procedures (11, 20%): artefact generator: figures from test and project systems [S13], model-drafted text, each figure traced. Certified inspectors sign NDT reports (E1).
- Records, logs, inspection schedules (5): records app with notifications (E1).
- Reading drawings, sourcing, analysis, training (13): judgement steps; a model extracts title blocks and tables (E1).
- Agent: rare; Bentley's MCP server lets agents drive MicroStation [S16]; an engineer reviews (E1).

## 4. Gates

- AI Act Annex III 2: AI safety components in critical infrastructure, road traffic or utilities are high-risk [S17]; from 2 December 2027, Annex I products from 2 August 2028 [S18].
- Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 covers AI-powered safety functions from 20 January 2027 [S19].
- Annex III 4(b) and WOR art. 27(1)(l): assigning work by worker performance is high-risk and needs works council consent [S17] [S20].
- Wkb: since 1 January 2024 an independent kwaliteitsborger tests new class 1 buildings against the Bbl during construction and at delivery [S21].
- AVG art. 22: change detection feeding property tax must not decide alone [S22]. Rijswijk rates Changemapp limited risk; its impact assessment field is empty [S12].
