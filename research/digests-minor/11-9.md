# Digest 11-9: Other Management Occupations

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 272 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 28 occupations (largest: regulatory affairs, compliance, education).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Corlytics (Ireland), Clausematch:** versioned policy library, multi-stage approvals, mapping to rules; AI classifies and summarises regulatory documents and flags gaps [S1]. E2.
- **CERRIX, GRC platform:** compliance, audit, incident and regulatory-monitoring modules; DORA and NIS2 frameworks [S2]. E2.
- **Zenya, DOC and FLOW:** protocol library, incident reports forwarded to the person in charge; De Waalboog (Dutch elderly care) uses it under the Wkkgz [S3]. E2.
- **Quinyx (Sweden) and Zermelo (NL):** shift planning [S4]; school timetables for 1,050 schools [S5]. E2.
- **Castor, Catalyst:** proposes case report form values from source documents; a person approves each [S6]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Barona (Finland), Quinyx:** shift-planning time down an average 15% per week; 2,200 employees, 120 planners [S4]. E3, vendor case (Dec 2024).
- **Novo Nordisk, clinical study reports:** drafts from expert-approved text; writing time down 90%, review cycles halved, people approve [S7]. E3, vendor case.
- **az West hospital, Zenya incident register:** 125 reports in 2011 (old system), 36,440 in 2023 [S8]: more reporting, not fewer incidents. E3, vendor case.
- No measured case found for rule-change watch or policy management.

## 3. Deterministic first

Rules cover under half the cases in 248 tasks; standard software does 16 fully, 138 partly; "vanishes" is unscored. The 23 tasks at 80% rules or more are mostly registers.

- **Compliance and regulatory (about 71 tasks, 50 on documents):** a records app for policies and obligations (versions, approvals, read receipts, review dates). Rule-change watch: a filtered feed; a model summarises each rule and proposes affected policies; an owner decides; stop when every item has a decision (E1).
- **Budgets, records, reports (about 64, 42 structured):** generated from the ERP or booking system, no Excel rekeying; a model drafts commentary, checked against the figures.
- **Scheduling and bookings (25, highest mean importance, 4.0):** configured product; working-time and CAO rules are solver constraints (E1). No model.
- **Incidents, complaints, emergencies, loss (34):** routed register with deadlines, plan library with test dates, fixed exception queries on till and stock data; a model classifies free-text reports and drafts status reports from logged facts; a person approves.
- **Property, projects, bids (21):** lease records and bid tables; a model marks deviations from standard clauses.
- **Strategy, curriculum, marketing, people (57):** manual; drafting help only. Agent: none.
- **Glue:** Excel, Word and Outlook hold registers, rosters and policy mail-outs; records apps with notifications replace them.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR Art. 22:** scholarship, admission and staff decisions must not rest on automation alone without human intervention and a right to contest [S9].
- **AI Act Annex III:** 4(a), 4(b): hiring, evaluating or rostering staff on behaviour or traits; 3(a), 3(b): admissions, learning outcomes; not 5(d), as no task classifies emergency calls [S10]. Duties from 2 December 2027 [S11].
- **WOR art. 27:** rosters, staff data and loss-prevention monitoring of staff need works council consent [S12].
- **Cyberbeveiligingswet (NIS2):** since 15 August 2026, transport included: registration, duty of care, incident reporting [S13].
- **Wkkgz:** health managers need a written incident procedure, calamity reports to the inspectorate without delay, and a complaints scheme [S14].
- **Reported failures:** a Deloitte review (A$439,000) of an Australian welfare compliance system, partly drafted with GPT-4o, cited non-existent sources and a fabricated court case; Deloitte agreed to repay the final instalment [S15].
