# 35-9 Restaurant hosts

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 1 automate task in 1 occupation (hosts and hostesses in restaurants, lounges and coffee shops): "receive and record patrons' dining reservations". Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Zenchef (Zenchef SAS, France)**: booking widget on the restaurant's site, Reserve buttons on Google and Instagram, a digital seating plan, the guest's profile and booking history shown during a phone call, a phone link that texts a booking link, automated SMS and email reminders against no-shows. [S1] E2
- **Formitable and Resengo**: now the same platform as Zenchef, with continued support for Formitable users. [S2] E2
- **TheFork Manager (LaFourchette SAS, France)**: reservation book, seating plans, central guest database. [S3] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

None found. Searched the Zenchef, Formitable and TheFork Manager pages for no-show or time-saved figures. They carry only a revenue testimonial ("nearly a 20% increase") and a ranking claim, not counted. [S1]

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Eliminate most of the task by configuration**: guests book in a widget that checks table capacity and opening hours; the booking lands in the book; a reminder goes out. No host touches it.
- **Phone and walk-in bookings**: the host types them into the same book, with the caller's profile on screen (configure).
- **Bounded model step, only for free text**: turning booking e-mails and group requests into fields (date, covers, notes). Check: the host confirms before it enters the book. Stop: anything unclear goes back to a person.
- **Voice bot on the phone**: the only agent-like option; rarely worth it for one restaurant.
- **Glue today**: a paper book, voicemail and a mailbox for group bookings.
- **Stays with a person**: greeting, seating judgement on the night, the waitlist, complaints.

## 4. Gates

- **GDPR**: guest profiles need a basis and a retention limit. Allergy notes are health data; processing is prohibited unless an exception applies, outside healthcare usually explicit consent [S4]. Record only what the kitchen needs for this visit.
- **EU AI Act**: no annex III point applies; taking bookings is not a listed use. Article 50(1): a phone or chat bot must tell callers they are dealing with AI [S5].
- **WOR article 27(1)(l)**: works council consent only if the system is also used to watch staff attendance or performance, and only where a works council exists (50 or more staff, article 2) [S6].
