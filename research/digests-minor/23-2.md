# Digest 23-2: Legal Support Workers

DIGEST-A, 2026-10-15. 19 automate/amplify O*NET tasks across 2 occupations (paralegals and legal assistants; title examiners, abstractors and searchers).
Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Dutch frame:** title examiners are a US role. Dutch property passes by notarial deed plus registration in the public registers [S1]; the notary researches the Kadaster registers and registers the deeds [S2].
- **Kadaster AkteAI (Netherlands, public body), deed extraction:** own models and rule algorithms pull structured data from notaries' deeds to update the registration [S3]; OCR reads handwritten deeds back to 1832 and flags easements, a person confirming each result [S4]. E2.
- **Qualia Clear (US), title exams:** drafts preliminary title exams from a search package and proposes curative tasks [S5]. E2.
- **Libra (Wolters Kluwer, Netherlands) and Legora (Sweden):** research, drafting and review for paralegal work [S6][S7]. E2.

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **AEGIS Land Title, Qualia Clear:** examiners went from about 10 to 20 commitments a day; every file now gets a full audit; no period given [S8]. E3, vendor case.
- **Kadaster, deed processing (about one million deeds a year [S4]):** AkteAI "usually at least halves" deeds dropping out to manual checks; no base or period [S3]. E3, own claim, 2026 job posting.
- **Ashurst, drafting trial (400+ people):** 80% time saved on corporate filings drawn from articles of association, 45% on first-draft briefings [S9]. E3, firm's own trial, June 2024.

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Registry queries and intake (instrument lists, restriction checks, search requests, record entry; 4 tasks):** eliminate or deterministic code. Query the registry by parcel number; an order form replaces reading requests; structured deed delivery (KIK, first fully automated deed in 2009 [S10]) removes re-keying. E1.
- **Document and registration checks (ownership, registrability, closing files; 4 tasks):** validation rules on structured deeds; a bounded model step extracts fields from PDFs. Parcel, names and amounts must match the registry; a mismatch or low confidence goes to a person, as at Kadaster [S3]. E1.
- **Artefacts (commitments, encumbrance reports, closing statements, document summaries, affidavits, legal documents; 6 tasks):** templates; closing statements are arithmetic. A model summarises free-text clauses only, each sentence quoting its source page. E1.
- **Research (facts and law, statutes, reference summaries; 3 tasks):** answer desk on licensed sources; an unresolved citation blocks the answer. E1.
- **Reviewing title searches, directing searchers (2 tasks):** judgement step plus a work-queue dashboard.
- **Glue:** mailed requests, re-keyed PDFs and Excel closing figures give way to an order form, a case records app and structured deed exchange. Agent needed: none.

## 4. Gates

- **AI Act:** no Annex III area applies [S11]; 8(a) only if the work is done for a court or ADR body.
- **Advocatenwet art. 11a:** a lawyer's staff share the duty of confidentiality [S12]. NOvA: no confidential data in free tools, check citations by hand [S13].
- **Kadasterwet bill (submitted 31 August 2026):** adds a legal basis for automated deed processing and tightens registration requirements [S14].
- **GDPR and WOR:** Kadaster has a person confirm AI-flagged easements [S4]; vendors need an Art. 28 contract [S15]. A per-examiner output dashboard needs works council consent under WOR art. 27(l) [S16].
- **Reported failures:** €2,315 costs for a lawyer's fake Hoge Raad quote (August 2026) [S17]; full costs for unchecked AI-drafted pleadings, Rechtbank Oost-Brabant, December 2025 [S18]; research tools wrong on over 17% of queries [S19].
