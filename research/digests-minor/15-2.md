# 15-2 Mathematical science occupations

DIGEST-C, 2026-10-15. 128 automate/amplify tasks across 9 occupations (actuaries, statisticians, data scientists, BI analysts, clinical data managers). Grades: E1 model knowledge, E2 a named product does this today, E3 a documented case with a source.

## 1. Products that do this today

- **Copilot in Power BI (Microsoft)**: questions on a semantic model, report pages, DAX; needs Fabric capacity. For "standard or custom reports". [S1] E2
- **AI/BI Genie (Databricks)**: questions answered in SQL over Unity Catalog, tuned with example SQL and trusted assets. [S2] E2
- **Castor EDC, CDMS, Catalyst (Castor, Amsterdam)**: query management; AI flags discrepancies at entry, extracts source data with confidence scores and human sign-off. [S3, S4] E2
- **Clinical Data Studio (Medidata)**: automated listings review, query management, reconciliation, audit-trail review. [S5] E2
- **Akur8 (Paris)**: automated transparent GLM/GAM pricing models, rate optimisation. [S6, S7] E2
- **pharmaverse (open-source R)**: admiral derives ADaM datasets, rtables builds tables. For "prepare tables and graphs". [S8] E2

## 2. Cases with numbers

- **Novo Nordisk**: Claude drafts clinical study reports from approved text; 10+ weeks to 10 minutes, review cycles halved. Vendor claim (Anthropic). [S9] E3
- **PowerGPT trial, 2025**: LLM picks the test, statistical engine computes; sample-size accuracy 94.1% versus 55.4%, 4.0 versus 9.3 minutes. Developers measured. [S10] E3
- **Uber, 2024**: text-to-SQL cut query writing from about 10 to 3 minutes. Company on itself. [S11] E3
- **Webmotors, Brazil**: Genie cut manual data tickets 72% and saved 200 analyst hours a month. Vendor case. [S12] E3

## 3. Deterministic first

- **Sample size**: formula code. A model maps the question to test and inputs; check: engine computes, statistician signs; stop: inputs confirmed [S10].
- **Tables, figures**: deterministic pipelines, double programmed; no model touches numbers.
- **Data queries**: configured edit checks. A model reads free text (adverse events, comments), proposes a query or code; check: data manager accepts; stop: never writes site data. "Receipt, entry" becomes lab, ePRO, FHIR integrations.
- **BI reports**: scheduled refresh and subscriptions. Ad hoc: answer desk on a prepared model; check: certified measure or benchmark set passed; stop: misses go to the analyst.
- **Actuarial tables, reserves**: engines and code; a model drafts the movement narrative, checked against figures.
- **Reports, plans, SOPs**: template generator plus model draft; check: each number traces to a dataset; a person approves.
- **Glue (E1)**: query trackers, transfer reconciliations and reserving triangles in spreadsheets; report packs by mail. Replace with integrations and a records app.
- **Stays human**: method choice, interpretation for monitoring committees, actuarial sign-off, stakeholder advice, teaching.

## 4. Gates

- **EU AI Act**: annex III 5(c), life and health insurance pricing of natural persons, is high-risk; 5(b) creditworthiness [S13]. Non-life pricing and BI chat are not listed. These duties apply from 2 December 2027 [S14]. DNB found insurers' AI documentation and monitoring short [S15]. Article 4 AI literacy since February 2025 [S16].
- **Solvency II**: EIOPA expects data governance, records, fairness, explainability and human oversight for other insurance AI [S17].
- **GDPR**: trial health data is article 9; research needs 9(2)(j) and article 89 safeguards [S18]; DPIA (E1).
- **CTR 536/2014**: verifiable records (art. 56); master file kept 25 years (art. 58) [S19].
- **EMA GCP guideline**: validated edit checks; sponsors never change investigator data unauthorised; secure audit trail; AI-specific rules deferred [S20]. AI raises queries, never corrects.
- **WOR 27(1)(k), (l)**: dashboards showing individual staff performance need works council consent [S21].
- **Risks**: the best agent solved 21.3% of enterprise text-to-SQL tasks [S22]; Uber's tool still invents tables [S11]; Copilot misreads unprepared models [S1].
